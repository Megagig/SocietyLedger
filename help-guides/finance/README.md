# Finance - Managing Your Association's Money

## What this module is for

The Finance module helps you track and manage all money-related activities in your association. You can organize different types of income and expenses, record transactions, track member payments, and generate financial reports. This is where you keep your association's financial records organized and up-to-date.

## Who can use it

**Full Access (View, Create, Edit, Approve):**
- Association Administrators
- Treasurers

**Create and Edit (Limited):**
- Financial Secretaries (can record transactions but may need approval)

**View Only:**
- Regular Members (can only see their own payment history)

## What you can do in this module

### Financial Categories
- Create categories for different types of income (dues, donations, fundraising)
- Set up expense categories (utilities, maintenance, events)
- Organize categories by type (income vs expenses)
- Set whether categories require approval for transactions

### Transaction Management
- Record money coming in (member payments, donations, grants)
- Record money going out (bills, purchases, expenses)
- Track payment methods (cash, bank transfer, online payment)
- Add descriptions and attach receipts or documents

### Member Financial Tracking
- View each member's payment history
- Track outstanding balances and overdue amounts
- Record partial payments and payment plans
- Generate individual member statements

### Payment Processing
- Confirm payments received from members
- Process refunds when necessary
- Handle payment disputes or corrections
- Track payment methods and references

### Financial Reporting
- Generate income and expense reports
- View financial summaries by time period
- Track category-wise spending and income
- Export financial data for external accounting

## Common use cases

### Setting up your financial structure
1. Create income categories (Monthly Dues, Registration Fees, Donations)
2. Create expense categories (Utilities, Maintenance, Events, Administration)
3. Set which categories need approval before money can be spent
4. Configure whether members can see category details

### Recording member payments
1. Go to the member's financial profile
2. Select what they're paying for (monthly dues, special assessment, etc.)
3. Enter the amount and payment method
4. Add any reference numbers (bank transfer reference, receipt number)
5. Confirm the payment to update their balance

### Processing monthly dues collection
1. Generate a list of members with outstanding dues
2. As payments come in, record each one against the member's account
3. For partial payments, note the amount and remaining balance
4. Send reminders to members with overdue payments
5. Generate a monthly collection report

### Managing association expenses
1. When paying a bill, create a new expense transaction
2. Choose the appropriate expense category
3. Enter the amount, payment method, and vendor details
4. Attach a photo or scan of the receipt
5. If required, get approval from authorized members

### Preparing financial reports
1. Choose the time period you want to report on
2. Select which categories to include
3. Generate the report showing income vs expenses
4. Review for accuracy and add any necessary notes
5. Export or print for meetings or record-keeping

## Important notes and limitations

### Transaction Approval
- Some transactions may require approval from administrators or treasurers
- Large amounts might need multiple approvals depending on your association's rules
- Approved transactions cannot be easily changed or deleted

### Payment Confirmation
⚠️ **Important:** Always confirm payments only after you've actually received the money. Confirming a payment updates the member's balance and cannot be easily undone.

### Financial Categories
- Choose category names that are clear and specific
- Don't create too many categories - it makes reporting complicated
- Income and expense categories are separate - you can have similar names for both

### Member Balances
- Positive balance = Member has overpaid or has credit
- Negative balance = Member owes money to the association
- Zero balance = Member is up to date with payments

### Payment Methods
- **Cash:** Physical money received in person
- **Bank Transfer:** Money transferred directly to association account
- **Online Payment:** Payments through payment gateways like Paystack
- **Check:** Payment by check (where applicable)

### Record Keeping
- Keep all receipts and supporting documents
- Add clear descriptions to all transactions
- Regular backups of financial data are essential
- Follow your local accounting and tax requirements

### Partial Payments
- Members can make partial payments toward their dues
- The system tracks the remaining balance automatically
- Set up payment plans for members who need them
- Send regular reminders for outstanding balances

### Financial Year
- Your association's financial year may be different from the calendar year
- Set this up in Settings to ensure reports align with your accounting period
- Historical data remains organized by the financial year it was recorded in

### Getting Help
- If you're unsure about recording a transaction, ask your treasurer or administrator
- Keep transaction descriptions clear so others can understand them later
- When in doubt about approvals, check with your association's financial policies
