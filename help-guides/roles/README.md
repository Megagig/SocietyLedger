# Roles & Permissions - Managing User Access

## What this module is for

The Roles & Permissions module controls what different people can do in your association's system. It defines who can view information, who can make changes, and who can approve important decisions. This ensures that sensitive information and critical functions are only accessible to the right people.

## Who can use it

**Full Management:**
- Association Administrators
- Super Administrators (for platform-wide settings)

**View Only:**
- Treasurers and Financial Secretaries (can see role assignments but cannot change them)

**No Access:**
- Regular Members (cannot see or change role assignments)

## What you can do in this module

### Role Management
- Assign roles to association members
- Change someone's role when their responsibilities change
- Remove roles from members who no longer need them
- View who has which roles in your association

### Permission Control
- Understand what each role can and cannot do
- See which features are available to different roles
- Ensure proper access control for sensitive functions

### User Access Review
- Review who has administrative access
- Check that former officers don't retain unnecessary permissions
- Ensure new officers get appropriate access levels

## Available Roles and What They Can Do

### Association Administrator
**Who should have this role:** Association President, General Secretary, or other top leadership

**What they can do:**
- Manage all aspects of the association
- Add, edit, and remove members
- Configure all association settings
- Approve financial transactions and loans
- Manage meetings and elections
- Access all reports and data
- Assign roles to other members

**What they cannot do:**
- Access other associations' data
- Change platform-wide settings (only Super Admins can do this)

### Treasurer
**Who should have this role:** Association Treasurer or Financial Manager

**What they can do:**
- Manage all financial activities
- Record income and expenses
- Process member payments
- Generate financial reports
- Configure payment settings
- Approve loan applications
- View member financial records

**What they cannot do:**
- Add or remove members (except for financial reasons)
- Change association settings unrelated to finance
- Manage elections or meetings

### Financial Secretary
**Who should have this role:** Assistant to the Treasurer or Financial Record Keeper

**What they can do:**
- Record financial transactions
- Process member payments
- View financial reports
- Help with loan processing
- Update member financial information

**What they cannot do:**
- Approve large transactions (may need Treasurer approval)
- Change financial settings
- Delete financial records

### Secretary
**Who should have this role:** Association Secretary or Meeting Coordinator

**What they can do:**
- Schedule and manage meetings
- Track meeting attendance
- Record meeting minutes
- Send communications to members
- Manage association documents

**What they cannot do:**
- Handle financial transactions
- Change member roles
- Access sensitive financial data

### Regular Member
**Who should have this role:** All other association members

**What they can do:**
- View their own information and update contact details
- See their payment history and outstanding balances
- Participate in meetings and elections
- Apply for loans and manage their savings accounts
- Receive communications and notifications

**What they cannot do:**
- See other members' personal or financial information
- Change association settings
- Approve transactions or applications
- Access administrative functions

## Common use cases

### Setting up a new officer
1. When someone is elected to a leadership position
2. Go to the member's profile
3. Change their role from "Member" to the appropriate officer role
4. Confirm they understand their new responsibilities
5. Remove the role from the previous officer if applicable

### Handling role transitions
1. When officers change (after elections or resignations)
2. First, assign the new role to the incoming officer
3. Train them on their new responsibilities
4. Then remove the role from the outgoing officer
5. Ensure no gaps in critical functions during the transition

### Regular access review
1. Quarterly, review who has administrative roles
2. Confirm that all officers still need their current access level
3. Remove roles from people who are no longer active
4. Add roles for new officers or volunteers
5. Document any changes for audit purposes

### Troubleshooting access issues
1. When someone reports they can't access a feature
2. Check their current role assignment
3. Verify what permissions that role should have
4. Either change their role or explain the access limitations
5. Consider if association policies need updating

## Important notes and limitations

### Role Assignment Rules
- Each person can only have one primary role
- Roles should match actual responsibilities in your association
- Don't give people higher access than they need for their duties
- Document role changes for accountability

### Permission Inheritance
- Higher roles generally include permissions of lower roles
- Administrators can do everything that Treasurers and Members can do
- Treasurers can do most things that Financial Secretaries can do
- Members have the most limited access

### Security Considerations
⚠️ **Important:** Only give administrative roles to people you trust completely. Administrators can access sensitive member information and make significant changes to your association.

### Role Changes Take Effect Immediately
- When you change someone's role, it applies right away
- They may need to log out and log back in to see all changes
- Test that new officers can access what they need
- Remove access promptly when people leave positions

### Super Administrator Role
- This is a platform-wide role, not association-specific
- Only system administrators should have this role
- Super Admins can access all associations on the platform
- Contact platform support if you need Super Admin assistance

### Audit Trail
- All role changes are logged in the system
- You can see who made changes and when
- This helps with accountability and troubleshooting
- Keep records of why roles were changed

### Best Practices
- Review roles after every election or major leadership change
- Don't leave former officers with administrative access
- Train new officers on their system responsibilities
- Have at least two people with Administrator roles (for backup)
- Document your association's role assignment policies

### Getting Help
- If you're unsure what role someone should have, check your association's constitution or bylaws
- For questions about specific permissions, test with a lower-privilege account first
- Contact platform support if you need help with role-related technical issues
- Consider creating written procedures for role management in your association
