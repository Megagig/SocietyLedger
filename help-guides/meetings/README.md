# Meetings - Organizing and Tracking Association Meetings

## What this module is for

The Meetings module helps you schedule, organize, and track all your association's meetings. You can create meeting agendas, track who attends, record meeting minutes, and send automatic reminders to members. This keeps your association's governance activities organized and documented.

## Who can use it

**Full Access (Create, Edit, Manage):**
- Association Administrators
- Secretaries

**Mark Attendance:**
- Administrators
- Secretaries
- Designated meeting coordinators

**View Meetings and Attend:**
- All Members (can see meetings they're invited to and mark their own attendance)

## What you can do in this module

### Meeting Scheduling
- Create different types of meetings (regular meetings, annual general meetings, emergency meetings)
- Set meeting dates, times, and locations
- Add virtual meeting links for online meetings
- Set how many members need to attend for the meeting to be valid (quorum)

### Meeting Agendas
- Create agenda items with time estimates
- Assign presenters to different agenda topics
- Reorder agenda items as needed
- Add descriptions and notes for each agenda item

### Attendance Tracking
- See who's expected to attend each meeting
- Mark members as present, absent, late, or excused
- Record check-in and check-out times
- Track apologies and reasons for absence

### Meeting Minutes
- Record what happened during the meeting
- Document decisions made and votes taken
- List action items and who's responsible for them
- Schedule the next meeting date

### Meeting Reminders
- Automatic email reminders sent 24 hours before meetings
- Notifications when meetings are scheduled or cancelled
- Alerts for members when their attendance is marked

## Common use cases

### Scheduling a regular monthly meeting
1. Click "Create New Meeting"
2. Choose "Regular Meeting" as the type
3. Set the date, time, and location
4. Add agenda items like "Financial Report," "Old Business," "New Business"
5. Set the quorum (minimum attendees needed)
6. Save and send invitations to all members

### Setting up an Annual General Meeting (AGM)
1. Create a new meeting and select "Annual General Meeting"
2. Schedule it well in advance (usually 30+ days notice)
3. Create detailed agenda including financial reports, elections, policy changes
4. Set a higher quorum requirement for important decisions
5. Add virtual meeting link if some members will join online

### Managing attendance during a meeting
1. Open the meeting from your meetings list
2. Go to the Attendance tab
3. Mark members as "Present" as they arrive
4. For late arrivals, mark as "Late" and record their arrival time
5. Record apologies for members who sent advance notice

### Recording meeting minutes
1. During or after the meeting, go to the Minutes tab
2. Write a summary of what was discussed
3. List all decisions made and any votes taken
4. Record action items with who's responsible and deadlines
5. Set the date for the next meeting
6. Save the minutes for approval at the next meeting

### Handling emergency meetings
1. Create an "Emergency Meeting" with short notice
2. Add a clear agenda explaining the urgent issue
3. Send immediate notifications to all members
4. Consider lowering the quorum if it's truly urgent
5. Document the emergency nature in the meeting description

## Important notes and limitations

### Meeting Types
- **Regular Meeting:** Your standard scheduled meetings
- **Annual General Meeting (AGM):** Yearly meeting for major decisions
- **Emergency Meeting:** Urgent meetings with short notice
- **Committee Meeting:** Smaller group meetings for specific topics
- **Special Meeting:** One-off meetings for specific purposes

### Quorum Requirements
- Quorum is the minimum number of members needed for the meeting to be valid
- Set this based on your association's rules (often 50% of active members)
- Meetings without quorum may need to be rescheduled
- The system will show if you've reached quorum during attendance marking

### Meeting Status
- **Scheduled:** Meeting is planned and invitations sent
- **In Progress:** Meeting is currently happening
- **Completed:** Meeting finished, minutes may be pending
- **Cancelled:** Meeting was cancelled (with reason recorded)
- **Postponed:** Meeting moved to a different date

### Attendance Status
- **Present:** Member attended the full meeting
- **Late:** Member arrived after the start time
- **Absent:** Member didn't attend and didn't send apology
- **Apology:** Member sent advance notice they couldn't attend
- **Pending:** Attendance not yet marked

### Virtual Meetings
- Add meeting links for Zoom, Google Meet, or other platforms
- Include dial-in numbers for members without internet
- Test the link before the meeting to ensure it works
- Consider recording virtual meetings (with member consent)

### Meeting Minutes
⚠️ **Important:** Meeting minutes are legal records of your association's decisions. Write them clearly and accurately, as they may be needed for legal or regulatory purposes.

### Automatic Reminders
- Reminders are sent 24 hours before the meeting start time
- Members receive both email and in-app notifications
- Cancelled meetings trigger immediate notifications to all invitees
- Make sure member email addresses are current for reminders to work

### Attendance History
- The system tracks each member's attendance rate over time
- Use this information to identify members who rarely attend
- Consider reaching out to members with low attendance
- Attendance records may be needed for membership reviews

### Getting Help
- If you can't mark attendance, check that the meeting status is "In Progress"
- For technical issues with virtual meetings, test the links in advance
- If minutes aren't saving, ensure you have the proper permissions
- Contact your administrator if you need help with meeting setup
