# Payments - Processing Member Payments and Billing

## What this module is for

The Payments module handles all money coming into your association from members. You can process different types of payments (dues, fees, donations), track payment methods, generate receipts, and manage member billing. This keeps your association's income organized and helps members stay current with their financial obligations.

## Who can use it

**Full Access (Process, Confirm, Manage):**
- Association Administrators
- Treasurers

**Record Payments:**
- Financial Secretaries (may need approval for large amounts)

**View Own Payments:**
- All Members (can see their payment history and outstanding balances)

## What you can do in this module

### Payment Processing
- Record payments received from members
- Process different payment types (monthly dues, registration fees, special assessments)
- Handle various payment methods (cash, bank transfer, online payments)
- Generate automatic receipts for confirmed payments

### Payment Tracking
- View all payments received by the association
- Track which members have paid and who still owes money
- Monitor payment trends and collection rates
- Search payments by member, date, or amount

### Member Billing
- See each member's outstanding balance
- Track payment due dates and overdue amounts
- Send payment reminders to members
- Set up payment plans for members who need them

### Receipt Management
- Generate receipts automatically when payments are confirmed
- Customize receipt templates with association information
- Resend receipts to members who request them
- Export receipt records for accounting purposes

### Payment Methods Setup
- Configure which payment methods your association accepts
- Set up online payment gateways for convenient member payments
- Manage bank account details for direct transfers
- Handle cash payments with proper documentation

## Common use cases

### Recording a cash payment
1. When a member pays cash in person
2. Go to "Record New Payment"
3. Select the member and what they're paying for
4. Enter the amount and select "Cash" as payment method
5. Generate a receipt and give it to the member
6. Store the cash securely according to your procedures

### Processing bank transfers
1. Check your association's bank account for incoming transfers
2. Match each transfer to a member using reference numbers or amounts
3. Record the payment in the system with the bank reference
4. Confirm the payment to update the member's balance
5. Send receipt confirmation to the member

### Setting up online payments
1. Go to Payment Settings and enable online payment gateway
2. Enter your payment provider account details (like Paystack keys)
3. Test with a small payment to ensure it works
4. Notify members that they can now pay online
5. Monitor online payments and confirm them as they come in

### Managing overdue payments
1. Generate a report of members with overdue balances
2. Send payment reminders via email or text message
3. Contact members personally if payments are significantly overdue
4. Set up payment plans for members experiencing financial difficulties
5. Follow your association's policies for handling persistent non-payment

### Monthly dues collection
1. At the beginning of each month, generate dues statements
2. Send payment reminders to all members
3. As payments come in, record each one against the member's account
4. Follow up with members who haven't paid by the due date
5. Generate a monthly collection report for association records

## Important notes and limitations

### Payment Types
- **Monthly Dues:** Regular membership fees paid monthly or annually
- **Registration Fees:** One-time fees for new members
- **Special Assessments:** Additional fees for specific projects or needs
- **Donations:** Voluntary contributions from members
- **Fines/Penalties:** Charges for late payments or rule violations

### Payment Methods
- **Cash:** Physical money received in person (requires careful handling)
- **Bank Transfer:** Direct transfer to association account (most secure)
- **Online Payment:** Through payment gateways like Paystack (convenient for members)
- **Check:** Paper checks (where applicable, may take time to clear)
- **Mobile Money:** Digital payments through mobile platforms

### Payment Confirmation
⚠️ **Critical:** Only confirm payments after you've actually received the money. Confirming updates the member's balance and generates receipts, so accuracy is essential.

### Receipt Generation
- Receipts are automatically generated when payments are confirmed
- Include payment date, amount, payment method, and what was paid for
- Receipts serve as proof of payment for both member and association
- Keep digital copies for your records even when giving paper receipts

### Payment Security
- Handle cash payments with at least two people present when possible
- Verify bank transfers before confirming payments
- Keep payment records secure and backed up
- Follow your association's financial procedures for all payments

### Partial Payments
- Members can make partial payments toward their dues
- The system tracks remaining balances automatically
- Set clear policies about partial payment acceptance
- Consider payment plans for members who can't pay full amounts

### Payment Disputes
- If a member disputes a payment, investigate thoroughly
- Check bank records and receipt copies
- Document all communications about the dispute
- Follow your association's dispute resolution procedures
- Correct errors promptly and transparently

### Late Payment Handling
- Set clear policies for late payment fees and grace periods
- Send reminders before applying late fees
- Consider individual circumstances when enforcing penalties
- Document all late payment actions for consistency

### Online Payment Considerations
- Online payments may have processing fees (usually paid by association or member)
- Payments may take 1-3 business days to appear in your bank account
- Keep online payment gateway accounts secure with strong passwords
- Monitor for failed or disputed online payments

### Getting Help
- If you're unsure about recording a payment, ask your treasurer before confirming
- For online payment setup issues, contact your payment provider's support
- Keep clear records of all payment-related communications
- When in doubt about payment policies, refer to your association's financial procedures
