# Cooperatives & Loans - Savings and Lending Services

## What this module is for

The Cooperatives & Loans module provides savings and lending services for your association members. Members can open different types of savings accounts, apply for loans, and manage repayments. This helps members save money together and access affordable credit when needed.

**Note:** This is an Enterprise feature that may require a higher subscription plan.

## Who can use it

**Full Management (Approve Loans, Manage Accounts):**
- Association Administrators
- Treasurers

**Process Transactions:**
- Financial Secretaries
- Designated loan officers

**Personal Use (Own Accounts and Loans):**
- All Active Members (can open accounts, apply for loans, make payments)

## What you can do in this module

### Savings Accounts
- Open different types of savings accounts (regular, target savings, fixed deposits)
- Make deposits and withdrawals
- Earn interest on your savings
- Track your savings goals and progress
- Transfer money between your accounts

### Loan Services
- View available loan products with different terms and rates
- Apply for loans for various purposes
- Provide guarantors when required
- Track your loan application status
- Make loan repayments

### Guarantor System
- Guarantee loans for other members
- Review and approve guarantor requests
- Track your guarantor commitments
- Understand your responsibilities as a guarantor

### Account Management
- View all your savings and loan balances
- See transaction history for all accounts
- Download account statements
- Set up automatic savings contributions

## Common use cases

### Opening a savings account
1. Go to "Savings Accounts" and click "Open New Account"
2. Choose account type (Regular, Target Savings, or Fixed Deposit)
3. Set your target amount if opening a target savings account
4. Choose monthly contribution amount for regular savings
5. Make your initial deposit to activate the account

### Applying for a loan
1. Review available loan products to find one that fits your needs
2. Click "Apply for Loan" and select the loan product
3. Enter the amount you want to borrow and repayment period
4. Provide the purpose for the loan
5. Add guarantors if required by the loan product
6. Submit your application and wait for review

### Making a savings deposit
1. Go to your savings account
2. Click "Make Deposit"
3. Enter the amount you want to save
4. Choose payment method (cash, bank transfer, etc.)
5. Add any reference numbers from your payment
6. Confirm the deposit

### Guaranteeing someone's loan
1. Check your pending guarantor requests
2. Review the loan details and borrower information
3. Confirm you understand your commitment as guarantor
4. Accept or decline the guarantor request
5. If accepting, you're committed until the loan is repaid

### Making loan repayments
1. Go to your active loans
2. Select the loan you want to pay
3. Enter the payment amount (can be partial or full)
4. Choose your payment method
5. Add payment reference if applicable
6. Confirm the payment to update your loan balance

## Important notes and limitations

### Savings Account Types

**Regular Savings:**
- Flexible deposits and withdrawals
- Earn interest on your balance
- No minimum balance requirements
- Access your money anytime

**Target Savings:**
- Save toward a specific goal amount
- Higher interest rates for committed saving
- Limited withdrawals before target is reached
- Automatic monthly contributions encouraged

**Fixed Deposit:**
- Lock your money for a specific period
- Highest interest rates
- No withdrawals until maturity
- Penalties for early withdrawal

### Loan Products
Each loan product has different terms:
- **Interest rates:** How much extra you pay for borrowing
- **Repayment period:** How long you have to pay back
- **Maximum amount:** The most you can borrow
- **Guarantor requirements:** Whether you need people to guarantee your loan

### Guarantor Responsibilities
⚠️ **Important:** As a guarantor, you're responsible for the loan if the borrower cannot pay. Only guarantee loans for people you trust and amounts you could afford to pay if necessary.

### Interest Calculations
- **Savings interest:** Usually calculated monthly and added to your account
- **Loan interest:** Added to your loan balance, making your total repayment higher
- **Interest rates:** Shown as yearly percentages but calculated monthly

### Loan Application Process
1. **Application:** Submit your loan request
2. **Review:** Administrators check your eligibility
3. **Approval/Rejection:** Decision made based on association policies
4. **Guarantor Confirmation:** Guarantors must accept their commitments
5. **Disbursement:** Money is released to your account

### Repayment Schedules
- Loans have fixed monthly payment amounts
- Payments include both principal (original amount) and interest
- Late payments may incur additional fees
- Early repayment may be allowed with potential savings on interest

### Account Balances
- **Positive balance:** Money you have saved
- **Loan balance:** Money you still owe
- **Available balance:** Money you can withdraw (may be less than total if some is reserved)

### Fees and Charges
- Account maintenance fees may apply
- Loan processing fees are charged when loans are approved
- Late payment fees for overdue loan payments
- Early withdrawal penalties for fixed deposits

### Eligibility Requirements
- Must be an active member in good standing
- May need minimum membership duration for loans
- Loan amounts may be limited based on savings history
- Some loan products require guarantors

### Getting Help
- Contact your treasurer or loan officer for questions about loan applications
- Check with administrators about guarantor requirements and responsibilities
- Review your association's cooperative policies for detailed rules
- Ask for help calculating loan payments before applying
