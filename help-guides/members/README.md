# Members - Managing Your Association Members

## What this module is for

The Members module is where you manage all the people in your association. You can add new members, update their information, track their membership status, and control what they can do in the system. This is the central place for all member-related activities.

## Who can use it

**Full Access (View, Add, Edit, Remove):**
- Association Administrators

**View and Add Only:**
- Treasurers
- Financial Secretaries

**View Own Profile Only:**
- Regular Members (can only see and update their own information)

## What you can do in this module

### Member Management
- Add new members to your association
- Update member contact information and details
- Change member roles and permissions
- Remove members who are no longer active
- Search and filter members by various criteria

### Member Registration
- Invite new members via email
- Approve or reject membership applications
- Set up member ID numbers (automatic or manual)
- Manage member verification status

### Member Information
- Store personal details (name, phone, email, address)
- Track membership dates (when they joined, renewed, etc.)
- Record emergency contact information
- Add notes about individual members

### Member Status Management
- Mark members as active, inactive, or suspended
- Track membership renewals and expiry dates
- Manage member categories or groups
- Handle member transfers between associations

### Bulk Operations
- Import multiple members from spreadsheet files
- Export member lists for external use
- Send bulk communications to groups of members
- Update multiple member records at once

## Common use cases

### Adding a new member
1. Click "Add New Member" button
2. Fill in their basic information (name, email, phone)
3. Choose their role (usually "Member" for regular members)
4. Set their membership start date
5. Generate or assign a member ID number
6. Send them a welcome email with login instructions

### Inviting someone to join
1. Go to member invitations section
2. Enter their email address and basic details
3. Choose what role they should have
4. Set when the invitation expires (usually 3 days)
5. Send the invitation - they'll get an email with signup instructions

### Updating member information
1. Find the member using search or browse the list
2. Click on their name to open their profile
3. Edit the information that needs updating
4. Save the changes
5. The member will be notified if their contact details changed

### Managing inactive members
1. Use filters to find members who haven't been active recently
2. Review their status and last activity
3. Contact them to confirm if they want to remain members
4. Either reactivate them or mark them as inactive
5. Inactive members can't log in but their records are preserved

### Preparing for elections or meetings
1. Export a list of all active members
2. Filter by members eligible to vote (based on membership duration)
3. Check that all members have current contact information
4. Update any missing email addresses or phone numbers

## Important notes and limitations

### Member Roles and Permissions
- **Administrator:** Can manage all aspects of the association
- **Treasurer:** Handles financial matters and can view member financial records
- **Financial Secretary:** Assists with financial record-keeping
- **Member:** Regular member with basic access to their own information

### Member ID Numbers
- Once assigned, member ID numbers should not be changed
- If using automatic generation, the system creates unique numbers
- Manual ID numbers must be unique within your association

### Data Privacy
- Members can only see their own personal information
- Administrators can see all member information
- Be careful about who has access to member contact details
- Follow your local privacy laws when handling member data

### Membership Status
- **Active:** Member can log in and participate fully
- **Inactive:** Member account is disabled but records are kept
- **Suspended:** Temporary restriction, can be reactivated
- **Pending:** New member waiting for approval

### Removing Members
⚠️ **Caution:** Removing a member deletes their account but may keep some records for financial and legal purposes. Consider marking them as "Inactive" instead of deleting if they might return.

### Bulk Operations
- Always backup your data before doing bulk imports or updates
- Test with a small group first before applying changes to all members
- Bulk operations cannot be easily undone

### Communication
- Members must have valid email addresses to receive system notifications
- Phone numbers are needed for text message notifications
- Keep contact information up to date for effective communication

### Getting Help
If you need to change someone's role or permissions and don't see the option, you may not have sufficient permissions yourself. Contact your association administrator for assistance.
