NEW · AJO / ESUSU / ADASHE THRIFT

Run thrift groups without the stress

Keep the collector — lose the notebook. Every contribution is recorded and receipted, every payout is transparent, and “who paid?” disputes end. Run it inside your association, or as a standalone group.

🧺 Collector thriftContributors pay a fixed amount daily, weekly or monthly. The collector records every collection and the contributor is paid at cycle end.
🔄 Rotating pot (esusu)A fixed group each pays the same amount every period, and one member takes the whole pot each round until everyone has had a turn.
Your rules, enforcedLate fee, grace period, guarantor and reminders set per group — accepted by every member.
Fair payout orderRecorded random draw, approved swaps, and order locked after the first payout.
Collector toolsAssigned contributors, daily targets and an end-of-day cash handover summary.
Receipt for every koboSMS, push or email receipt on each contribution. Corrections are logged, never erased.
Reported vs confirmedA claim of payment is only “confirmed” once matched to the bank account or cash handover.
Two-step verificationGroup approves, then SocietyLedger verifies the member before any payout. Payout accounts shown masked.
Safe payoutsWhoever prepares a payout can't approve it. Changing bank details voids approval.
Defaults & reportsArrears cases, repayment plans, and CSV / PDF reports on contributions, payouts and collectors.
