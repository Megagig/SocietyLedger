This page lists notable changes to SocietyLedger as they ship — new features, meaningful fixes, and anything that changes how an existing screen works. Entries are added here as each release goes out.

## Reference

### Notifications that open the right screen, and officer alerts

- **Tap a notification to open what it's about.** In the app, tapping a ticket, guarantor request, savings-deposit or loan-repayment notification opens that screen. On the web, the notification bell and the Notifications page have a **View details →** link. Notifications from before this change don't point anywhere.
- **Officers are told when something needs them.** Treasurers, financial secretaries and admins get an in-app notification and a push when a member submits a savings deposit, a loan repayment or a loan application.
- **Members hear the decision properly.** Savings-deposit and loan-repayment decisions now arrive as in-app notifications and pushes, follow your notification preferences, and open your savings or loans.
- **Fixed:** admins couldn't see member support tickets — their ticket list was empty and they couldn't reply. Admins now see and answer every member ticket.
- **Fixed:** in the web notification bell, **Mark read** and **Mark all read** did nothing.
- **Fixed:** in the admin console, SocietyLedger's support inbox showed no tickets under **All open** even though the counts included them.
- **Clearer for admins:** the sidebar entry is **Support Tickets** for admins (members keep **My Tickets**), the page is titled **Member Support Tickets** with a count of tickets that need a reply, and the Manage tab in the app refreshes your permissions each time it opens.
- **Corrected:** the manual no longer says notification preferences do nothing. They're applied to the notifications listed in [Choosing which notifications you get](/docs/member-guide/notification-preferences); quiet hours are still not applied.

### Contact SocietyLedger, escalation and the support desk

- **Association admins can contact SocietyLedger support** from **Help & Support → My Tickets → Contact SocietyLedger** on the web, and **Manage → Support tickets → To SocietyLedger** in the app — for billing, bugs, modules, data corrections and feature requests. SocietyLedger's team answers from a new ticket inbox in the admin console, and you're notified of every reply.
- **Escalate a member's ticket** without sharing it: you write a summary in your own words, SocietyLedger sees only that, and the member isn't told. See [Handling support tickets](/docs/help-support/handling-support-tickets).
- **A support desk in the app.** Admins and officers can answer member tickets from **Manage → Support tickets**: reply, leave internal notes, assign, change status and get a suggested reply. The web ticket page gained the same internal notes, status change, **Assign to me** and **Suggest a reply**.
- **Clearer wording.** On the web, members now see **Waiting on you** and **Waiting on support**, as in the app; staff keep **Waiting on User**.
- **Clarified:** [Contacting SocietyLedger support](/docs/help-support/contacting-support) now explains who to contact for what — your association for your own account, SocietyLedger for the platform.

### Support tickets in the app, with attachments

- **Open and follow tickets in the mobile app.** Profile → Help & support → My tickets: your tickets (Open and Closed), a chat-style thread, reply, close and reopen, and a new-ticket form with suggested help articles. See [Opening and following a support ticket](/docs/help-support/support-tickets).
- **Attach files** (up to three photos or PDFs, 5MB each) to a new ticket and to replies — on the web and in the app.
- **You're told about replies.** Your association's reply reaches you by in-app notification, push and email; admins are told when a ticket arrives or you reply.
- **Tickets go to your association's admins.** The support-tickets page now says so — it used to say tickets reach SocietyLedger directly.
- **Fixed (security):** members could list other members' tickets by adding a user filter, and could close or reopen anyone's ticket. Members can now only see and change their own.
- **Fixed:** creating a ticket failed with "Permission denied" because the support permissions were never granted to the default roles.

### Help centre on mobile, and "?" links on more screens

- **Help & support in the app.** Profile → Help & support has search, featured articles, browse by category, popular questions, a full **FAQs** screen with search, and articles you can rate. **User manual** opens this documentation site. See [The in-app help centre](/docs/help-support/in-app-help-center).
- **"?" on the screen you're on.** Screens for savings, loans, guarantor requests, paying, your ledger and your Money overview now have a **?** that opens the matching manual page. The web has the same link on My Savings, My Loans, Guarantor Requests and the officer queues for savings deposits and loan repayments.
- **Fixed:** on the web, choosing a category under **Browse by Category** in the Help Centre led to an empty page. It now lists the articles in that category — including categories such as Governance.

### Guarantor requests: notifications, mobile and My guarantees

- **Guarantors are told straight away.** When someone names you as a guarantor you get an in-app notification, a push on mobile, and an email. Borrowers are notified the same way when a guarantor accepts or declines, including the reason, and when their application moves on to the officers. See [Guarantors](/docs/cooperatives/guarantors).
- **Answer requests in the mobile app.** **Money → Guarantor Requests** has the same Accept and Decline flow as the web, with the liability warning and a required reason to decline.
- **My guarantees.** Web and mobile now show the total you guarantee on active loans and each loan behind it.
- **Fixed:** an officer's view of a member's profile showed blanks for the loans that member guarantees. It now shows the loan number, principal and status.

### Cooperatives: deposits, repayments and savings rules

- **Members can deposit and repay in the app.** Pay the cooperative's bank account, upload the receipt, and an officer confirms it before it counts. See [Depositing into your savings](/docs/cooperatives/depositing-into-your-savings) and [Paying towards your loan](/docs/cooperatives/paying-your-loan). Officers review them under **Savings Deposits** and **Loan Repayments** — see [Approving member savings deposits](/docs/cooperatives/approving-savings-deposits) and [Approving member loan repayments](/docs/cooperatives/approving-loan-repayments).
- **Lend against savings, your cooperative's way.** Each loan product can have a **Savings multiplier** (1×, 2×, 3× …) and hold part of a member's savings while a loan runs. Loan products can now be **edited**. See [Your savings rule and held savings](/docs/cooperatives/savings-rule-and-held-savings).
- **Members see their own cooperative overview** under **Cooperatives → Overview**, instead of an error.
- **Fixed:** members couldn't see or respond to guarantor requests, or search for guarantors when applying, because those screens needed officer-level access. They can now.
- **Security:** members could previously post a deposit or loan repayment straight onto their own balance without an officer confirming the money. Only officers can now record these directly, and each member's cooperative data is visible only to them (and to officers).
