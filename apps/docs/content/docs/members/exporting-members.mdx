Download your complete member list — with balances — for use outside SocietyLedger, from the shared Exports Center.

## Before you start

You need the **export members** permission. Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries all have it by default.

## Export your member list

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Exports"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Under **Quick Exports**, select **All Members**.</Step>
<Step>Your export generates and downloads automatically.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/members/exporting-members-1.webp" alt="Exports Center page with Quick Export cards for Financial Summary, All Members and Member Statement, plus Recent Exports and a Custom Export Builder" />

## What happens next

The export also appears under **Recent Exports** and in your full **Export History**, so you can re-download it later without generating it again.
