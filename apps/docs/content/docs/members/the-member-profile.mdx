Selecting a member from the list opens their profile — everything you have on file about them, plus a quick financial summary.

## How it works

<Screenshot src="/images/members/the-member-profile-1.webp" alt="Member profile for Chiamaka Nwosu showing Total Dues, Total Paid and Balance stat cards, Personal Information, Membership Information and System Information sections" />

The profile is organised into **Personal Information** (contact details, date of birth, address), **Membership Information** (category, join date, how long they've been a member), and — where the member has an emergency contact on file — that too. At the top, three stat cards show **Total Dues**, **Total Paid** and **Balance** for a financial snapshot without leaving the page.

<Callout type="note" title="Not shown here yet">
Attendance history and cooperative details (savings, loans) aren't part of this page today — check [Meetings](/docs/meetings) and [Cooperatives](/docs/cooperatives) directly for those.
</Callout>

## Why it matters

Having the financial snapshot right on the profile means you don't need to cross-reference the finance section just to see whether a member is paid up before, say, approving something on their behalf.
