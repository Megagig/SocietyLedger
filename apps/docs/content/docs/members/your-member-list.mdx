Every member in your association is listed under Members, with search and filters to help you find one quickly.

## Before you start

You need the **view members** permission. Treasurers and Financial Secretaries can view and search the list; only Administrators can add, edit or remove members.

## Find a member

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Members"]} /> from the sidebar.</Step>
<Step>Type a name, email, phone number or member ID into the search box.</Step>
<Step>Narrow the list further with **All Status** (Active, Inactive, Suspended) or **All Categories** (Regular, Executive, Honorary, Student).</Step>
<Step>Use **Previous**/**Next** and the per-page selector to page through results.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/members/your-member-list-1.webp" alt="Members list searched for 'Ngozi', showing the member ID, name, email, phone, status and category columns with pagination" />

## What happens next

Select a member's row to open their [profile](/docs/members/the-member-profile), or use the row's **Edit** or **Disable** actions directly if you have permission.
