People can already register themselves into your association today — this page covers what that looks like, and what the related settings actually control.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission, which only Administrators have by default, to change the settings below (though they currently have no effect — see the callout).

## Self-registration today

<Steps>
<Step>Anyone who reaches your association's subdomain can register themselves from the login page's sign-up link — there's no setting that turns this off.</Step>
<Step>Every self-registered account requires approval and email verification before it can sign in, regardless of what any toggle says.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/members/self-registration-and-approvals-1.webp" alt="Members Access Settings page with Allow Self-Signup, Require Admin Approval, Require Email Verification toggles, Default Role selector, and Member ID settings" />

<Callout type="danger" title="Allow Self-Signup doesn't control this, and there's no approval screen yet">
Self-registration is always open on your subdomain no matter how **Allow Self-Signup** is set — confirmed by tracing the setting through the API, it's never checked. Approval and email verification happen for every self-registration regardless of the other two toggles too. There's also no screen in your own dashboard yet to review a pending registration — only a platform-level screen SocietyLedger staff use, so contact support to approve one in the meantime. See [Members & access settings](/docs/settings/members-and-access) for the rest of this settings page's fields, none of which are enforced yet either.
</Callout>

## What happens next

A self-registered member can't sign in until SocietyLedger support approves them; one who was [invited directly](/docs/members/inviting-members-to-log-in) skips that wait entirely, since an officer already added them.
