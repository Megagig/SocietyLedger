Decide whether people can register themselves into your association, and what happens before they can sign in.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission, which only Administrators have by default.

## Configure self-registration

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Members Access"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Turn **Allow Self-Signup** on to let people register themselves without an invite.</Step>
<Step>Turn **Require Admin Approval** on if new self-registrations should wait for approval before they can sign in, and **Require Email Verification** to make them confirm their email first.</Step>
<Step>Set the **Default Role for New Members** and whether **Member ID**s are generated automatically.</Step>
<Step>Save your changes.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/members/self-registration-and-approvals-1.webp" alt="Members Access Settings page with Allow Self-Signup, Require Admin Approval, Require Email Verification toggles, Default Role selector, and Member ID settings" />

<Callout type="warning" title="No approval screen yet">
There's currently no screen in your own dashboard to review and approve a pending self-registration — only a platform-level screen that SocietyLedger staff use. If you turn on **Require Admin Approval**, contact SocietyLedger support to approve pending members in the meantime.
</Callout>

## What happens next

A self-registered member who needs approval can't sign in until that review happens; one who was [invited directly](/docs/members/inviting-members-to-log-in) skips approval entirely, since an officer already added them.
