People can register themselves into your association. Each new registration waits for an officer to approve it.

## Before you start

- To approve or reject registrations, you need the **edit all members** permission.
- To change the registration settings, you need the **edit settings** permission. Both are Administrator-only by default.

## Turn self-registration on or off

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Members Access"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Switch **Allow Self-Signup** on or off, then save. When it's off, the sign-up link tells people to ask an administrator for an invite instead.</Step>
</Steps>

## Approve or reject a registration

You get a notification when someone registers. On the web it also shows as a count next to **Approvals** on the member list.

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Pending Approvals"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Check the person's name, email and whether they've verified their email.</Step>
<Step>Select **Approve** to let them sign in straight away, or **Reject** and, if you like, give a reason.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Pending approvals"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Check the person's name, email and whether they've verified their email.</Step>
<Step>Select **Approve** to let them sign in straight away, or **Reject** and, if you like, give a reason.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

Use **Approved** and **Rejected** at the top of the screen to look back at earlier decisions.

## What happens next

The person gets an email with your decision, and with your reason if you rejected them. If the email can't be sent, the screen tells you, so you can let them know yourself.

A member you [invited directly](/docs/members/inviting-members-to-log-in) skips this wait entirely, since an officer already added them.
