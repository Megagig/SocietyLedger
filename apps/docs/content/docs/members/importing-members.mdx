Moving many members in at once — when you're switching to SocietyLedger from a spreadsheet, for example — currently needs SocietyLedger support, not a self-service upload.

<Callout type="warning" title="No upload screen yet">
There's no screen in your dashboard to upload a spreadsheet of members. The underlying bulk-import capability exists, but only as something SocietyLedger support can run on your behalf — there's no file-parsing step either, so even support needs your data already broken into individual fields (first name, last name, email, phone, category) rather than a raw file.
</Callout>

## Before you start

If you only have a handful of members to add, it's faster to [add them one at a time](/docs/members/adding-a-member) than to wait on a bulk import.

## Request a bulk import

<Steps>
<Step>Prepare your member list with at least a first and last name for each person, plus email, phone and category where you have them.</Step>
<Step>Contact SocietyLedger support with your list.</Step>
<Step>Support checks for existing members with the same email first — anyone already in your association is skipped automatically, not duplicated.</Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

Each new member gets a member ID assigned automatically, the same as adding one by hand. None of them get a login until you separately [invite them](/docs/members/inviting-members-to-log-in).
