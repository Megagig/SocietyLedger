Update any of a member's details, or change their status if they've stopped participating.

## Before you start

You need the **edit members** permission, which only Administrators have by default.

## Edit a member

<Steps>
<Step>Open the member's [profile](/docs/members/the-member-profile) and select **Edit**, or select the row's edit icon from the [member list](/docs/members/your-member-list).</Step>
<Step>Update any field — personal details, address, category, status or emergency contact.</Step>
<Step>To disable a member without removing their record, set **Status** to **Inactive** or **Suspended**.</Step>
<Step>Select **Update Member**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/members/editing-and-disabling-1.webp" alt="Edit Member form with the Status dropdown set to Inactive" />

## Removing a member

Selecting **Delete** on a member's profile doesn't erase their record — it marks them **Terminated** and keeps everything on file for your financial and legal records. You can't delete a member who still has pending or overdue charges; settle or waive those first.

## What happens next

Status changes take effect immediately — an inactive or suspended member can no longer log in, but their history stays intact everywhere else in the system.
