Add a member's record directly — useful for members who don't need to log in yet, or whose details you already have on hand.

## Before you start

You need the **add members** permission, which only Administrators have by default.

<Callout type="note" title="Adding vs. inviting">
Adding a member only creates their record — it doesn't give them a login. To let them sign in themselves, see [Inviting members to log in](/docs/members/inviting-members-to-log-in) once they're added.
</Callout>

## Add a member

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Members"]} />, then select **Add Member**.</Step>
<Step>Fill in their **First Name** and **Last Name** — everything else is optional at this point.</Step>
<Step>Set their **Category** (Regular, Executive, Honorary or Student), **Status**, and **Join Date**.</Step>
<Step>Select **Create Member**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/members/adding-a-member-1.webp" alt="Add New Member form with First Name, Last Name, Email and Phone fields filled in" />

## What happens next

The member appears in your [member list](/docs/members/your-member-list) immediately, with a member ID assigned automatically.
