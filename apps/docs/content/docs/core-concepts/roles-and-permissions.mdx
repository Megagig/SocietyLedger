What you can see and do in SocietyLedger depends entirely on your role — there's no separate "settings" to unlock features, your menu simply shows what your role allows.

## How it works

Every association starts with four built-in **system roles**:

- **Admin** — full access to everything.
- **Treasurer** — manages finances and payments.
- **Financial Secretary** — records transactions and dues.
- **Member** — sees only their own information.

System roles can't be deleted or renamed, but an admin can create **custom roles** with their own exact mix of permissions — useful for a role like "Communications Officer" that doesn't fit the built-in ones.

Underneath, every permission is a specific capability — things like viewing members, creating transactions, or exporting reports. A role is just a named set of these. When you sign in, SocietyLedger checks your role's permissions and builds your menu from only what you're allowed to see; a menu item you don't have permission for doesn't appear at all, rather than appearing disabled.

## Why it matters

This is why two officers in the same association can see completely different menus — a treasurer sees Finance and Reports, while a financial secretary sees transaction entry but not role management. If you can't see something you expect to, it's almost always a permission your role doesn't have, not a bug.

## What you can do

- [Finishing the setup wizard](/docs/getting-started/onboarding-wizard) — invite officers with a role during setup.
- [Custom roles](/docs/settings/custom-roles) — build a role with exactly the permissions you need.
