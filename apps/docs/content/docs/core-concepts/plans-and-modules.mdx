Your association's plan decides how many members you can have and which parts of SocietyLedger — modules — are available.

## How it works

Every association is on a plan, such as **Starter**, **Pro** or **Enterprise**. The plans on offer are set by SocietyLedger and shown live on the plan page. The plan decides your member limit and which modules are included — things like the financial ledger, welfare funds, voting and elections, meetings, SMS messaging, cooperatives and loans, and reports.

Where a feature belongs to a higher tier than yours, you'll see a small badge next to it in the menu — for example **Enterprise** next to Cooperatives, or **Pro** next to WhatsApp messaging. A **Beta** badge means a feature is newer and still being refined, regardless of plan.

## Why it matters

New associations are given a plan for a fixed starting period automatically — see [Creating your association](/docs/getting-started/create-your-association) — so you can try things out before deciding on a plan. If a module you need isn't included in your current plan, see [what each plan includes](/docs/plan-billing/plans-and-whats-included) or talk to your association's admin about upgrading.
