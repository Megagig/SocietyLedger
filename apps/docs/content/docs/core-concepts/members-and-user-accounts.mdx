Being a member of your association and having a login to SocietyLedger are two separate things — a member doesn't need a login to be tracked in your records.

## How it works

Every member has a **member record**: name, contact details, a **member ID** (like `MEM-0001`), category and status. An officer can add this record — with dues, a ledger, attendance — long before that person ever signs in anywhere.

A member's **login status** tracks whether they can sign in:

<Steps>
<Step><StatusChip status="draft" label="No login" /> — the member exists in your records, but has no SocietyLedger account yet.</Step>
<Step><StatusChip status="pending" label="Invited" /> — an officer sent them an activation link, but they haven't set a password yet.</Step>
<Step><StatusChip status="active" label="Active" /> — they've set a password (or self-registered and been approved) and can sign in.</Step>
</Steps>

Once a member activates their login, their member record and their login account are linked — so what they see when they sign in is their own data.

## Why it matters

This separation is what makes it possible to track dues and attendance for a member before they've ever touched SocietyLedger — useful when you're bringing in your existing member list. It's also why removing someone's login doesn't delete their history: their member record and its financial history stay intact.

## What you can do

- [Joining your association as a member](/docs/getting-started/joining-as-a-member) — the two ways a member gets a login.
- [Inviting members to log in](/docs/members/inviting-members-to-log-in) — send an activation link to an existing member record.
