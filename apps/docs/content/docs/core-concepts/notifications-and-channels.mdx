SocietyLedger can reach you three ways today: inside the app, by email, or by SMS — each notification category has its own setting for which of these it uses.

## How it works

Notifications fall into a few categories, each with its own email / SMS / in-app toggle:

- **Payment due** — a charge is coming up or overdue.
- **Payment received** — a payment was recorded against your account.
- **Approval** — your account or a request was approved (or needs review).
- **Announcement** — a message from your association.
- **System** — everything else (password resets, security alerts).

Your notification preferences page lets you turn any channel on or off per category, and set **quiet hours** — a time range (for example 10pm–8am) during which SocietyLedger is meant to hold off sending anything but in-app notifications.

<Callout type="warning" title="Quiet hours don't take effect yet">
Channel choices are applied to payment, support-ticket, guarantor, savings-deposit and loan-repayment notifications, but meeting reminders and platform announcements don't check them yet, and quiet hours aren't respected. See [Choosing which notifications you get](/docs/member-guide/notification-preferences) for the full picture.
</Callout>

SMS is sent from your association's own **SMS credit balance**, topped up by an admin — if it runs out, SMS notifications pause until it's topped up again, but email and in-app continue as normal.

<Callout type="note">Push notifications (to your phone, even when the app isn't open) arrive with the mobile app.</Callout>

## Why it matters

Not every notification needs to interrupt you the same way — a payment confirmation might only need an in-app note, while an overdue payment might be worth an SMS. Setting these per category, rather than all-or-nothing, means you only get interrupted for what actually matters to you.
