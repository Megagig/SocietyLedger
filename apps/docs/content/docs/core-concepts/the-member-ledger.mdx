Every member has a running ledger — a record of everything charged to them and everything they've paid, in order, with a balance after each entry.

## How it works

Each ledger entry is one of three types:

- **Debit** — something added to what a member owes: a charge like dues, a levy, or a penalty.
- **Credit** — something that reduces it: a payment, a refund, or a waiver.
- **Adjustment** — a manual correction by an officer.

Entries are always in date order, each with the balance owed right after it — so you can see exactly when and why the balance changed, not just what it is today.

A member's **statement** is this ledger filtered to a period (a month, a year, or a custom range) — the same entries, with an opening and closing balance for that period.

## Why it matters

Because every charge and payment is its own entry, a member's balance is never just a single number you have to trust — you can always trace it back to the exact charges and payments that produced it. This is also what a member sees when they check what they owe.
