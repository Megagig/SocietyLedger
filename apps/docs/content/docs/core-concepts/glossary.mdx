Terms used across the SocietyLedger docs and app, in plain language.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Association | Your community's own workspace on SocietyLedger — see [Your association, explained](/docs/core-concepts/your-association). |
| Member ID | A unique code for each member (like `MEM0001`), separate from their login email. |
| Login status | Whether a member has a SocietyLedger account yet: No login, Invited, or Active. |
| Category | Where an officer sets up a type of charge — dues, a levy, a penalty — and its amount. |
| Charge | A transaction that a member owes — created from a category, with a due date. |
| Payment | Money recorded against a charge; a charge can take more than one payment. |
| Dues | A regular charge (usually monthly or annual) that applies to members. |
| Levy | A one-time or campaign charge, often for a specific purpose (like a building fund). |
| Penalty | A charge added for a late payment or a rule violation. |
| Waiver | Closing a charge without full payment, recorded by an officer with a reason. |
| Proof | An uploaded receipt or screenshot supporting a bank transfer payment, pending an officer's verification. |
| Reconciliation | Matching payments recorded in SocietyLedger against a bank statement. |
| Journal entry | A double-entry accounting record (debit and credit to specific accounts), separate from a member's own ledger entry. |
| Member ledger | A member's running record of every debit (charge) and credit (payment) against them, with a balance after each — see [The member ledger and statements](/docs/core-concepts/the-member-ledger). |
| Guarantor | A member who vouches for someone else's cooperative loan application. |
| Quorum | The minimum number of attendees a meeting needs for its decisions to count. |
| Role | A named set of permissions (like Treasurer) assigned to a user — see [Roles & what each person can see](/docs/core-concepts/roles-and-permissions). |
| Permission | One specific capability (like "create transactions") that a role can include. |
| Plan (tier) | Starter, Professional or Enterprise — decides your member limit and which modules are included. |
| Module | A part of SocietyLedger (like Welfare or Cooperatives) that may or may not be included in your plan. |
| SMS credits | A balance your association tops up to send SMS notifications; email and in-app notifications don't use it. |
