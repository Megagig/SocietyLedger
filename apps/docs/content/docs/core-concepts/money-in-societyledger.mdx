Every naira SocietyLedger tracks follows the same three-step path: a category, a charge, and one or more payments against it.

A **category** (like Monthly Dues or a one-time Building Levy) is where an officer sets the amount and how often it applies. From a category, SocietyLedger creates a **charge** for each member — a transaction with an amount and a due date. Members then make one or more **payments** against that charge, by card, bank transfer, or recorded manually by an officer.

A charge moves through these statuses as payments come in:

<StatusChip status="pending" /> not yet paid, not yet due → <StatusChip status="overdue" /> past its due date, nothing paid → <StatusChip status="partial" /> some paid, not all → <StatusChip status="paid" /> fully paid

A charge can also be <StatusChip status="waived" label="waived" /> by an officer, closing it without full payment.

### Worked example

Tunde Bakare's monthly dues charge is ₦5,000, due 1 March.

| Date | Event | Amount | Charge status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Mar | Charge created | ₦5,000 due | Pending |
| 5 Mar | No payment yet | — | Overdue |
| 10 Mar | Partial payment | ₦2,000 paid | Partial (₦3,000 remaining) |
| 15 Mar | Final payment | ₦3,000 paid | Paid |

## Why it matters

Because a charge can take more than one payment, your reports always show exactly how much of a charge is still outstanding — not just whether it's "done." This is also what feeds [the member ledger](/docs/core-concepts/the-member-ledger): every charge and payment becomes an entry there.
