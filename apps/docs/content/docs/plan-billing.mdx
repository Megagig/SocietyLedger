Plan & billing covers choosing a plan, your 14-day trial, paying with Paystack or Nomba, changing or cancelling your subscription, and what actually happens — today — when one lapses.

<Callout type="warning" title="Billing tracks correctly; access doesn't change with it yet">
Subscribing, paying and cancelling all work for real against Paystack/Nomba. But whether a module is available to your association is still decided separately, and that check doesn't currently block anything — see [What happens when a subscription ends](/docs/plan-billing/when-a-subscription-ends) before assuming a plan or trial status restricts what you can do.
</Callout>
