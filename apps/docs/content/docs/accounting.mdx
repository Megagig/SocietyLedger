Accounting gives your association real double-entry bookkeeping — a chart of accounts, journal entries with a maker-checker approval step, an income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement, and budgets you can track against actual spending.

<PlanNote />

<Callout type="note" title="Not the same as Reports">
[Reports → Financial Summary](/docs/reports/financial-summary) reads your Finance transactions directly, so it reflects everything whether or not it's synced here. Accounting's statements only reflect what's been posted to the ledger — see [What happens automatically when money moves](/docs/accounting/what-happens-automatically) for the gap between the two.
</Callout>
