## Can I edit a meeting after I've scheduled it?

Not today — there's no edit screen for a scheduled meeting. If the details need to change, cancel it with a reason and schedule a new one instead. See [Cancelling or postponing a meeting](/docs/meetings/cancelling-or-postponing).

## How do I mark a meeting as in progress or completed?

There's currently no button for this — a meeting stays Scheduled until you cancel it. See [Scheduling a meeting](/docs/meetings/scheduling-a-meeting) for what the lifecycle looks like today.

## How do I add candidates to an election?

Adding a candidate isn't self-service yet — contact SocietyLedger support with the member's name and the position, and your platform contact can add them directly. See [Nominations and candidates](/docs/elections/nominations-and-candidates).

## Why is there no "Start Voting" button on my election?

Closing nominations currently leaves an election showing as Draft, which has no Start Voting option in the interface. Contact SocietyLedger support to open voting once your candidates are approved; see [Opening and closing voting](/docs/elections/running-the-vote).

## I published election results — where can members see who won?

There's no results screen yet showing vote counts or the winner, even though the votes themselves are counted and stored correctly. Announce the outcome to your members outside the app — an announcement, a notice, or at your AGM — until a results view is added. See [Publishing results](/docs/elections/publishing-results).

## How many members actually voted, and who's eligible?

Every active member is automatically eligible once voting opens — there's no separate list to manage. Turnout isn't shown as a report yet, so you'll need your own count of who voted rather than a number in the app; see [Who can vote, and tracking turnout](/docs/elections/voter-eligibility-and-turnout).
