## Can I edit a meeting after I've scheduled it?

Not today — there's no edit screen for a scheduled meeting. If the details need to change, cancel it with a reason and schedule a new one instead. See [Cancelling or postponing a meeting](/docs/meetings/cancelling-or-postponing).

## How do I mark a meeting as in progress or completed?

There's currently no button for this — a meeting stays Scheduled until you cancel it. See [Scheduling a meeting](/docs/meetings/scheduling-a-meeting) for what the lifecycle looks like today.

## Why can't I create or run an election?

Elections are run by the Election Committee, not by the officers. Ask your Administrator to give you the **Election Committee** role from Settings → Users. See [The Election Committee](/docs/elections/election-committee).

## How do I add candidates to an election?

Open the election and select **Add Candidate** under the position (**Add a candidate** on the phone). Another member of the committee then approves them. See [Nominations and candidates](/docs/elections/nominations-and-candidates).

## Why can't I open voting on my election?

Voting opens only when every position has an approved candidate, no nomination is still waiting for a decision, and the voting end date hasn't passed. The election page says which of these is missing; see [Opening and closing voting](/docs/elections/running-the-vote).

## Where can I see who won, and when can members?

The Election Committee can open **View Results** as soon as voting closes; members see the results once the committee publishes them. A tie has no winner. If the election needed a minimum turnout and too few members voted, the results can't be published. See [Publishing results](/docs/elections/publishing-results).

## How many members actually voted, and who's eligible?

Every active member is automatically eligible once voting opens — there's no separate list to manage. The election page shows how many members have voted, and the results show the turnout; see [Who can vote, and tracking turnout](/docs/elections/voter-eligibility-and-turnout).
