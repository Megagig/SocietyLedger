## Does the free trial really not need a card?

In practice, no — the only way to start a trial today is the checkout page's pay button, which redirects to a real payment page for the full plan price, even though the Plans page says no card is required. The trial itself does genuinely begin in the background regardless. See [Your free trial](/docs/plan-billing/free-trial).

## I downgraded my plan — why did it change immediately instead of waiting until my period ends?

A downgrade's success message says the change is "scheduled" for your current period's end date, but it actually applies the moment you confirm it, the same as an upgrade. See [Upgrading or downgrading](/docs/plan-billing/changing-your-plan).

## Can I cancel anytime, and what happens to my association's data?

Cancelling doesn't end your subscription immediately — it stops it from renewing, so your plan keeps working until the period you've already paid for finishes. You can reactivate any time before that period actually ends. See [Cancelling and reactivating](/docs/plan-billing/cancelling-and-reactivating).

## What actually happens to my association when a subscription expires?

See [What happens when a subscription ends](/docs/plan-billing/when-a-subscription-ends) for exactly what changes (and what doesn't) once your subscription lapses.

## Where can I see my past invoices and payments?

Your full billing history is in one place. See [Billing history](/docs/plan-billing/billing-history).

## Will modules or features actually lock if I don't upgrade my plan?

Not today — enforcement isn't switched on yet, so don't rely on your subscription status as an access control. See [What happens when a subscription ends](/docs/plan-billing/when-a-subscription-ends) and [Modules by plan](/docs/reference/modules-by-plan) for the tiering this will eventually enforce.
