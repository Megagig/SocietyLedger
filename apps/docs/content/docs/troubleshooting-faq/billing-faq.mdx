## Do I need to pay to start?

Not unless SocietyLedger is asking new associations to pay first. A new association is given a plan for a fixed period, as a free trial or as complimentary access, starting when it is approved, and you don't need a card for it. The pricing page always states the current offer. See [Your starting period](/docs/plan-billing/free-trial).

## I chose a cheaper plan — why hasn't it changed yet?

A move to a cheaper plan is scheduled for the end of the period you've already paid for, and nothing is charged now. Until then you keep your current plan. The subscription page shows the switch and its date. See [Upgrading or downgrading](/docs/plan-billing/changing-your-plan).

## Why was I credited when I upgraded?

When you move to a higher plan, you aren't charged twice for the days you had left. The unused part of what you paid is taken off the new plan's price. If your period was free, there is nothing to credit.

## I paid but my subscription isn't active. What do I do?

Don't pay again. Your subscription is activated only when Paystack confirms the payment for the exact amount shown at checkout. A payment that is still processing activates by itself as soon as it's confirmed. If the amount that arrived doesn't match, the payment is marked **Being checked** on your subscription page, and SocietyLedger contacts you. Contact support and quote the Paystack reference. See [Subscribing and paying](/docs/plan-billing/subscribing-and-paying).

## Can I cancel anytime, and what happens to my association's data?

Cancelling doesn't end your subscription immediately — it stops it from renewing, so your plan keeps working until the period you've already paid for finishes. You can reactivate any time before that period actually ends. See [Cancelling and reactivating](/docs/plan-billing/cancelling-and-reactivating).

## What actually happens to my association when a subscription expires?

See [What happens when a subscription ends](/docs/plan-billing/when-a-subscription-ends) for exactly what changes (and what doesn't) once your subscription lapses.

## Where can I see my past invoices and payments?

Your full billing history is in one place. See [Billing history](/docs/plan-billing/billing-history).

## Will modules or features lock if my plan doesn't include them?

Yes. A module or feature that isn't in your plan, or in an [add-on](/docs/plan-billing/add-ons) you hold, isn't available, and SocietyLedger enforces that on the server, not just in the menu. See [Understanding your plan](/docs/plan-billing/plans-and-whats-included) and [Modules by plan](/docs/reference/modules-by-plan).

## Can I add extras without changing my whole plan?

Yes, with an add-on, if SocietyLedger offers one for your plan. You pay only for the days left until your renewal date, and it renews with your plan. See [Add-ons](/docs/plan-billing/add-ons).
