## Can people register themselves, or do I have to add every member manually?

People can already register themselves from your association's sign-up page today — there's no setting that actually turns this off, regardless of what the Allow Self-Signup toggle says. See [Self-registration and approving accounts](/docs/members/self-registration-and-approvals).

## How do I approve a member who registered themselves?

There's no screen in your own dashboard for this yet — only a platform-level screen SocietyLedger staff use. Contact SocietyLedger support to approve a pending self-registration in the meantime; see [Self-registration and approving accounts](/docs/members/self-registration-and-approvals).

## What's the difference between inviting a member and letting them register themselves?

An invited member skips approval and email verification entirely, since an officer already vouched for them by adding them first — they can sign in immediately. A self-registered member always waits for both. See [Inviting members to log in](/docs/members/inviting-members-to-log-in).

## Can I add a lot of members at once instead of one by one?

Yes — bulk import from a spreadsheet is built for exactly this. See [Importing members](/docs/members/importing-members).

## Can I export my member list?

Yes, from the members list itself. See [Exporting members](/docs/members/exporting-members).

## How do I give someone extra permissions without making them a full Administrator?

Create a custom role with just the permissions they need, rather than assigning the built-in Administrator role. See [Custom roles](/docs/settings/custom-roles) and [Assigning roles](/docs/settings/assigning-roles).
