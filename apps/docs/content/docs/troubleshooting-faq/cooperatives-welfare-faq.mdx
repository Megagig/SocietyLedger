## Can a member repay their own loan in the app?

Yes, with an officer's confirmation. The member pays the cooperative's bank account, then selects **Pay Now** on their loan and uploads the receipt. The repayment is recorded when an officer confirms the money arrived — until then it shows as **Awaiting confirmation**. See [Paying towards your loan](/docs/cooperatives/paying-your-loan) and [Approving member loan repayments](/docs/cooperatives/approving-loan-repayments).

## How does a member add money to their savings?

They pay into the cooperative's bank account, then select **Make a Deposit** on their savings account and upload the receipt. It's added to their savings once an officer confirms the payment. See [Depositing into your savings](/docs/cooperatives/depositing-into-your-savings).

## I paid, but my savings or loan balance hasn't changed. Why?

Your payment is waiting for an officer to confirm it. Under **My Deposits** (savings) or **My Payments** (loans), it shows as **Awaiting confirmation**. If it was **Not approved**, the officer's reason is shown beside it, and you can submit a corrected payment.

## Why can't I borrow as much as the loan product's maximum?

Some products only lend a multiple of your savings (for example, up to twice what you have saved), and savings already held against another loan don't count. The Apply for Loan page shows your savings, what's available, and the most you can borrow. See [Your savings rule and held savings](/docs/cooperatives/savings-rule-and-held-savings).

## Why can't I withdraw some of my savings?

Part of your savings is held against an active loan. It stays in your account but can't be withdrawn until it is released as you repay the loan. See [Your savings rule and held savings](/docs/cooperatives/savings-rule-and-held-savings).

## Why does a member say "Failed to fetch cooperative dashboard"?

That message came from an older version, where members who opened **Cooperatives** landed on an officer-only dashboard. Members now see their own **My Cooperative** overview instead. If you still see the message after an update, refresh the page.

## What happens if a guarantor declines to back my loan application?

The borrower sees the guarantor's reason for declining and can look for another guarantor instead. See [Guarantors: requesting and responding](/docs/cooperatives/guarantors).

## Can I see everything about a member's savings and loans in one place?

Not yet — there's no single officer-facing profile screen. Check their savings, loans and applications as three separate searchable lists instead. See [A member's cooperative profile](/docs/cooperatives/member-cooperative-profile).

## Is there a dedicated form for bereavement support requests?

No — bereavement goes through the same request form as any other welfare category, with no separate fields for the deceased's name or date. Put those details in the Reason field instead. See [Bereavement support](/docs/welfare/bereavement-support).

## Who can approve or reject a welfare request?

Administrators and Treasurers can review, approve (at the requested amount or a different one) or reject a request with a reason. See [Handling support requests](/docs/welfare/payout-requests).

## How do I set up a welfare fund for something like bereavement or medical support?

Create a fund and choose its type — Bereavement, Medical, Education, Emergency, Maternity or Wedding — which then appears as a request option for members. See [Setting up welfare funds](/docs/welfare/welfare-funds).
