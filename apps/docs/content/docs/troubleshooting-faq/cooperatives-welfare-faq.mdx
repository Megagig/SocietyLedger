## Can a member repay their own loan directly in the app?

Not yet — repayments are recorded by an officer, not self-service. A member can watch their own repayment progress, but paying it outside the app (by transfer or cash) only reflects once an officer records it. See [Repayments and repayment schedules](/docs/cooperatives/repayments-and-schedules).

## What happens if a guarantor declines to back my loan application?

The borrower sees the guarantor's reason for declining and can look for another guarantor instead. See [Guarantors: requesting and responding](/docs/cooperatives/guarantors).

## Can I see everything about a member's savings and loans in one place?

Not yet — there's no single officer-facing profile screen. Check their savings, loans and applications as three separate searchable lists instead. See [A member's cooperative profile](/docs/cooperatives/member-cooperative-profile).

## Is there a dedicated form for bereavement support requests?

No — bereavement goes through the same request form as any other welfare category, with no separate fields for the deceased's name or date. Put those details in the Reason field instead. See [Bereavement support](/docs/welfare/bereavement-support).

## Who can approve or reject a welfare request?

Administrators and Treasurers can review, approve (at the requested amount or a different one) or reject a request with a reason. See [Handling support requests](/docs/welfare/payout-requests).

## How do I set up a welfare fund for something like bereavement or medical support?

Create a fund and choose its type — Bereavement, Medical, Education, Emergency, Maternity or Wedding — which then appears as a request option for members. See [Setting up welfare funds](/docs/welfare/welfare-funds).
