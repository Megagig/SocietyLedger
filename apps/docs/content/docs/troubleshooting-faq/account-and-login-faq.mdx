## Why can't I sign in even though I'm sure my password is correct?

SocietyLedger blocks sign-in for four separate reasons that look similar but need different fixes: your email isn't verified yet, your account is still awaiting approval, it was rejected, or it (or your whole association) was suspended. See [Understanding your account's approval status](/docs/account-security/account-approval-status) for how to tell which one applies to you.

## I just signed up — why do I have to wait before I can sign in?

Every self-registered account needs both email verification and approval before it can sign in, no matter what your association's settings say. If an officer added you directly instead of you registering yourself, you skip this wait entirely — see [Inviting members to log in](/docs/members/inviting-members-to-log-in).

## I never received my verification email — what do I do?

Check your spam folder first, then use the resend option on the sign-in page. See [Verifying your email](/docs/account-security/verifying-your-email) for the full steps.

## I forgot my password — how do I get back in?

Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page to get a reset link by email. See [Resetting your password](/docs/account-security/resetting-your-password).

## The app says my whole association is suspended — what does that mean?

That's different from your individual account being suspended: it means a SocietyLedger platform administrator has suspended your association itself, not just your login. Contact SocietyLedger support directly rather than your association's own admin, since they can't lift this. See [Understanding your account's approval status](/docs/account-security/account-approval-status) for how to tell the two apart.

## Can I belong to more than one association with the same login?

Yes — if you're a member of more than one association, you can switch between them without signing out. See [Switching between associations](/docs/account-security/switching-associations).
