## Why did my payment show as "paid" right away, before anyone checked it?

Today, choosing Bank Transfer, Cash, Card or POS when you pay yourself marks the transaction paid immediately on your own say-so — there's no gateway charge or officer confirmation behind those options yet. See [Paying online](/docs/member-guide/paying-online) and [Paying by bank transfer](/docs/member-guide/paying-by-bank-transfer) for what each method actually does today.

## I don't want to pay by card — can I pay by bank transfer instead?

Yes — record the transfer yourself as a member, or have an officer record it on your behalf, then it goes through verification against your bank's actual statement. See [Paying by bank transfer](/docs/member-guide/paying-by-bank-transfer) and, for officers, [Recording bank transfers](/docs/payments/recording-bank-transfers).

## I sent a bank transfer — why hasn't it shown up against my balance yet?

A transfer needs to be matched against your association's bank statement and verified by an officer before it's applied — this doesn't happen automatically the moment you send the money. See [Matching and reconciliation](/docs/payments/matching-and-reconciliation) and [Verifying payment proofs](/docs/payments/verifying-payment-proofs).

## Can I pay several dues or charges in one checkout?

Not today — online checkout handles one item at a time, so paying multiple charges means paying them one after another rather than in a single transaction. See [Paying online](/docs/member-guide/paying-online).

## Where can I see my payment history and get a receipt?

Your full payment history and downloadable receipts are on your own statement page. See [Your payment history and receipts](/docs/member-guide/payment-history-and-receipts).

## As a member, why can't I verify or approve my own payment proof?

Verifying a payment proof is an officer-only task by design, so a different pair of eyes confirms it against the bank statement. See [Verifying payment proofs](/docs/payments/verifying-payment-proofs) and [Roles and permissions](/docs/core-concepts/roles-and-permissions) for who can do this.
