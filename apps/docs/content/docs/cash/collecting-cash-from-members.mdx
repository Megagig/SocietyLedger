Use a collection whenever you take cash from members — at a meeting, or on any day.

## Before you start

- You need the **collect cash** permission.
- **Cash** must be enabled under **Enabled Channels** in [Payment settings](/docs/settings/payment-settings).
- The member must be on the system. Their charges, savings account, welfare funds and loans are what you can take cash against.

## Open a collection

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Finance", "Cash Collections"]} />, then select **Open a collection**.</Step>
<Step>Name it, for example *October general meeting*. Pick the meeting if it was one (optional), then select **Open collection**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Cash", "Collections"]} />, then select the **+** button.</Step>
<Step>Name it, pick the meeting if there was one (optional), then select **Open collection**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## Write a receipt

<Steps>
<Step>In the collection, select **Collect cash**.</Step>
<Step>Choose the **Member**. Everything they could pay appears: what they owe, loan repayments, savings, welfare funds, and a donation.</Step>
<Step>Type the amount they hand over against each item. **Fill** puts in the full balance or the usual amount for you.</Step>
<Step>Check the **Total received** against the cash in your hand, then select **Record receipt**.</Step>
</Steps>

The member is credited at once and gets a notification (and an email if they have one). One receipt can cover several items.

<Callout type="warning" title="If it says “Not saved”">
The receipt didn't reach the server. Select **Not saved — Retry**. Retrying is safe: it never credits the member twice. If it says *part of this receipt was saved*, open the collection and check which payments are listed before taking more.
</Callout>

## Void a mistake

While the collection is still open, another officer with the **verify** permission can open the receipt and select **Void**, giving a reason. The member's balance goes back as it was and they are told why. You can't void a receipt you took yourself, and a loan repayment can't be voided here.

## Close the collection

When you have finished taking cash, select **Close collection**. This fixes the receipts so the total can be lodged. You can't add or void receipts afterwards.

## What happens next

[Lodge the cash](/docs/cash/lodging-cash-into-the-bank) into the bank.
