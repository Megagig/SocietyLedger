Cash is easy to take and easy to lose track of. SocietyLedger follows it through four steps, so at any moment you can see who holds how much.

## The four steps

1. **Collect.** An officer opens a **collection** — one meeting, or one day — and writes a **receipt** for each member who pays. The member is credited straight away and told, so their balance is right even before the cash reaches the bank.
2. **Close.** When the officer has finished taking cash, they close the collection. After that, receipts in it can't be added or voided, so the total is fixed.
3. **Lodge.** The officer pays the cash into one of the association's bank accounts and records the **lodgment** with a photo of the deposit slip.
4. **Verify.** A **different** officer checks the lodgment against the bank statement. Only then does the cash move from "cash in hand" to the bank in your accounts.

## Who holds the cash

The **Cash** page shows each officer's position:

- **In hand** — collected and not yet lodged.
- **Ready to lodge** — on closed collections.
- **Lodged, awaiting check** — paid into the bank, waiting for a second officer.

Officers who can view cash see everyone's position. Everyone else sees their own.

## What it covers

One receipt can include any mix of dues and levies, cooperative savings, welfare contributions, donations and loan repayments. Each is recorded by the same rules as when it is entered any other way — a charge can't be overpaid, a savings account must be active, and so on.

## Safeguards

- You can't verify your own lodgment.
- Lodging less than you collected needs a reason, and is flagged for the treasurer.
- A receipt can be voided only while its collection is open, with a reason, by an officer other than the one who took the cash.
- If cash stays unlodged past your deadline (3 days unless you change it in [Payment settings](/docs/settings/payment-settings)), the officer and the officers who watch cash are reminded.

<Callout type="note" title="Cash is one option among several">
Nothing changes for members who pay by card or bank transfer. Cash collections only handle cash that an officer takes in person. **Cash** must be switched on under **Enabled Channels** in Payment settings.
</Callout>

## What you need

Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries can collect, lodge, verify and view cash by default. See [Permissions](/docs/reference/permissions) to give the same to a custom role.
