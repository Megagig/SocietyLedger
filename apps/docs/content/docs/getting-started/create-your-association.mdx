Creating an association gives your community its own SocietyLedger workspace — members, dues, meetings and everything else live under your own address.

## Before you start

You don't need anything ready in advance — no bank details, no member list. You can add those later.

## Sign up for an association

<Steps>
<Step>Go to the SocietyLedger signup page and fill in your **Association Name** (for example, "Yaba Tech Alumni Association").</Step>
<Step>A **Subdomain** is suggested from your association's name — lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens only. Edit it if you want a shorter or different address; SocietyLedger checks it's available as you type.</Step>
<Step>Fill in your own **Email Address**, **Password** and **Confirm Password** for the admin account you'll sign in with.</Step>
<Step>Select **Create Association**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/create-your-association-1.webp" alt="Create Your Association signup form with Association Name, Subdomain, Email Address, Password and Confirm Password fields" />

Your association starts on a free trial automatically — there's no plan to choose at this step.

## What happens next

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/create-your-association-2.webp" alt="Email Verified confirmation screen showing the account is pending admin approval" />

You'll see a confirmation that your association was created, along with its address and a 6-digit verification code sent to your email. From here:

1. Verify your email with the code — see [Verifying your email](/docs/account-security/verifying-your-email).
2. Wait for a SocietyLedger platform admin to approve your account.
3. Once approved, sign in and the [setup wizard](/docs/getting-started/onboarding-wizard) opens automatically.

<Callout type="warning" title="Approval takes a little time">
New associations are reviewed by a SocietyLedger platform admin before you can sign in — this usually takes 1–2 business days. You'll get an email once your account is approved.
</Callout>
