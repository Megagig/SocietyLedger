Creating an association gives your community its own SocietyLedger workspace — members, dues, meetings and everything else live under your own address.

## Before you start

You don't need anything ready in advance — no bank details, no member list. You can add those later. Have your phone number and an email address you can open ready, because we send a code to that email.

<Callout type="warning" title="Choose the name carefully">
Your association's name becomes its web address (for example `yaba-tech-alumni.societyledger.com.ng`) once your account is approved, and it can't easily be changed afterwards. Pick a name members will recognise and a short, clear address.
</Callout>

## Sign up for an association

<Steps>
<Step>On the web, go to the SocietyLedger signup page. In the app, tap **Create an association** on the welcome screen. Enter your **Association Name** (for example, "Yaba Tech Alumni Association").</Step>
<Step>Choose the **Kind of Association**. If yours isn't listed, choose **Other** and type what kind of association it is.</Step>
<Step>**Your Address** is suggested from the name — lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens only. Edit it if you want a shorter or different address; SocietyLedger checks it's available as you type and shows how members will sign in. In the app this is step 1 of 2, and you tap **Continue** to move on.</Step>
<Step>Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, **Password** and **Confirm Password** for the admin account you'll sign in with. In the app you also agree to the terms.</Step>
<Step>Select **Create Association** (**Create association** in the app).</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/create-your-association-1.webp" alt="Create Your Association signup form with association details and admin account fields" />

Your association starts on a free trial automatically — there's no plan to choose at this step. See [Your free trial](/docs/plan-billing/free-trial).

## What happens next

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/create-your-association-2.webp" alt="Email Verified confirmation screen showing the account is pending admin approval" />

You'll see a confirmation that your association was created, along with its address and a 6-digit verification code sent to your email. From here:

1. Verify your email with the code — see [Verifying your email](/docs/account-security/verifying-your-email). If the email never arrives, ask for a new code, or contact support and we can verify it for you.
2. Wait for SocietyLedger to approve your account.
3. Once approved, your free trial starts, you can sign in, and the [setup wizard](/docs/getting-started/onboarding-wizard) opens automatically.

<Callout type="info" title="Approval takes a little time">
New associations are reviewed by SocietyLedger before you can sign in — this usually takes 1–2 business days. You'll get an email once your account is approved, and your free trial only starts then, so the wait never uses it up.
</Callout>
