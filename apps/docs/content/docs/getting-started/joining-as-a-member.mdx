There are two ways to get a login for your association: an officer invites you by email, or you register yourself if your association allows it.

## Before you start

You need your association's web address (for example, `yourassociation.societyledger.com.ng`) — ask an officer if you're not sure.

## If you were invited by email

<Steps>
<Step>Open the invitation email and select the activation link. It opens your association's **Activate your account** page.</Step>
<Step>Choose a **Password** and confirm it.</Step>
<Step>Select **Activate Account**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note">Invitation links expire after a few days. If yours has expired, ask the officer who invited you to resend it.</Callout>

## If you're registering yourself

Some associations let anyone with the web address create their own account, instead of waiting for an invite.

<Steps>
<Step>Go to your association's web address and select <UiLabel>Register here</UiLabel> on the sign-in page.</Step>
<Step>Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email Address**, **Password** and **Confirm Password**.</Step>
<Step>Select **Create Account**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/joining-as-a-member-1.webp" alt="Create your member account form with First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Password and Confirm Password fields" />

<Callout type="note">If self-registration isn't offered on your association's sign-in page, ask an officer to invite you instead.</Callout>

## What happens next

Either way, you'll need to verify your email with a 6-digit code, then wait for your account to be approved — see [Pending, rejected or suspended accounts](/docs/account-security/account-approval-status). Once approved, [sign in](/docs/getting-started/signing-in) with your association's web address.
