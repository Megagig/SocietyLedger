Every association has its own web address, and you sign in there rather than on the main SocietyLedger site.

## Before you start

You need your association's web address (for example, `yourassociation.societyledger.com.ng`) and an account that's already been created — see [Joining your association as a member](/docs/getting-started/joining-as-a-member) if you don't have one yet.

## Find your association's sign-in page

If you go to the main SocietyLedger sign-in page without your association's address, you'll see a reminder to use it instead:

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/signing-in-1.webp" alt="Sign in to your association page showing a reminder to access your association via its subdomain" />

## Sign in

<Steps>
<Step>Go to your association's own web address.</Step>
<Step>Enter your **Email Address** and **Password**.</Step>
<Step>Select **Sign In**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/signing-in-2.webp" alt="Association-branded sign-in page with Email Address and Password fields" />

If you forgot your password, select **Forgot your password?** — see [Resetting a forgotten password](/docs/account-security/resetting-your-password).

<Callout type="warning" title="Email not verified yet">
If you see "Please verify your email before logging in", you haven't finished verifying the email you signed up with — check your inbox for the 6-digit code.
</Callout>

## What happens next

Where you land depends on your role and account status: founders and admins who haven't finished setup see the [setup wizard](/docs/getting-started/onboarding-wizard), everyone else goes straight to their dashboard.
