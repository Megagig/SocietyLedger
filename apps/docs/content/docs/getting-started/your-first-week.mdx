The setup wizard covers the basics — this is a suggested order for the rest, once you're signed in and looking at your dashboard.

## Before you start

You've finished (or skipped) the [setup wizard](/docs/getting-started/onboarding-wizard) and can see your dashboard.

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/your-first-week-1.webp" alt="Empty dashboard welcome state with Add Members and Set Up Finance buttons" />

## A suggested order

<Steps>
<Step>**Set up your financial categories** — dues, levies and any other charges your association collects, under **Finance**.</Step>
<Step>**Add your association's bank account** — so members know where to send bank transfers, under **Settings**.</Step>
<Step>**Invite your officers** — treasurer, financial secretary or other admins, under **Members**.</Step>
<Step>**Add your members** — one by one or by importing a CSV, under **Members**.</Step>
<Step>**Send your first announcement** — let members know the association is live, under **Communication**.</Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

Each of these has its own detailed guide once you need it — this checklist is just a suggested order to work through them.
