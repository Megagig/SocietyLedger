Once your association is approved and you sign in for the first time, a five-step setup wizard opens to help you get the basics in place.

## Before you start

You need to be signed in as your association's admin. Every step can be skipped and finished later — nothing here is required before you can use SocietyLedger.

## The five steps

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/onboarding-wizard-1.webp" alt="Welcome to SocietyLedger onboarding overview showing five steps: Profile, Team, Members, Finance and Complete" />

<Steps>
<Step>**Profile** — set your association's name, logo, and primary color (used throughout your dashboard), plus an optional description.</Step>
<Step>**Team** — invite other officers by email and assign them a role: **Admin**, **Treasurer** or **Financial Secretary**. Select **Add another team member** to invite more than one.</Step>
<Step>**Members** — bring in your existing members, either by uploading a CSV file or adding them one by one with **Add Manually**.</Step>
<Step>**Finance** — add your dues, levies and penalties. Quick-add templates are offered (for example, Monthly Dues or a Building Levy) or select **Custom Category** to create your own.</Step>
<Step>**Complete** — a summary screen confirms you're set up, with quick links to add your first member, record a transaction, or open your dashboard.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/onboarding-wizard-2.webp" alt="Step 1 of 5, Association Profile form with Association Name, Primary Color and Description fields" />

## Skipping and resuming

Select **Skip this step** on any step to move on without filling it in. If you leave the wizard before finishing, signing back in takes you straight back to the overview, with completed steps marked and the next one ready to continue.

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/onboarding-wizard-3.webp" alt="You're All Set completion screen with quick actions to add a member, create a transaction, or view the dashboard" />

## What happens next

Whatever you skipped can be finished later from the normal dashboard screens — adding members from **Members**, dues and levies from **Finance**, and so on. See [Your first week](/docs/getting-started/your-first-week) for a suggested order.
