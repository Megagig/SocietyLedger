Once your association is approved and you sign in for the first time, a five-step setup wizard opens to help you get the basics in place. It works the same on the web and in the app, and your progress is saved, so you can start on one and finish on the other.

## Before you start

You need to be signed in as your association's admin. Every step can be skipped and finished later — nothing here is required before you can use SocietyLedger.

## The five steps

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/onboarding-wizard-1.webp" alt="Welcome to SocietyLedger onboarding overview showing five steps: Profile, Team, Members, Finance and Complete" />

<Steps>
<Step>**Profile** — set your association's name, logo, brand colour, and contact details (phone, email and address). Everything you save here can be changed later in **Settings**.</Step>
<Step>**Team** — invite other officers with their name and email, and give each a role: **Admin**, **Treasurer** or **Financial Secretary**. Each person gets an email to set their password and arrives with the role you chose. Add as many people as you need in one go.</Step>
<Step>**Members** — bring in your existing members. On the web, upload a CSV file or add them manually. In the app, add them one at a time; for a long list, skip this step and import it on the web.</Step>
<Step>**Finance** — pick starter dues, levies and penalties (you can change each amount), and add the bank account members pay into. All of it is optional.</Step>
<Step>**Complete** — a summary shows what you set up and what you skipped, with a **Finish now** link on anything skipped.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/onboarding-wizard-2.webp" alt="Step 1 of 5, Association Profile form" />

## Skipping and resuming

Select **Skip this step** on any step to move on without filling it in. A skipped step is marked **Skipped**, not **Done**, so the summary always tells you the truth. You can also leave the whole wizard with **Skip setup for now**; it won't appear again, and you can set everything up later.

If you close the app or leave the page halfway, signing back in takes you straight back to the overview, with finished and skipped steps marked and **Continue** going to the first step still open.

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/onboarding-wizard-3.webp" alt="You're All Set completion screen" />

## What happens next

Whatever you skipped can be finished later from the normal screens — adding members from **Members**, dues and levies from **Finance**, and so on. See [Your first week](/docs/getting-started/your-first-week) for a suggested order.
