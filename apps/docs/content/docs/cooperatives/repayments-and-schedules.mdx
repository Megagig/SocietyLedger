Once a loan is disbursed, it has an outstanding balance that goes down as repayments are recorded against it.

## How it works

<RoleTabs roles={["Officers", "Members"]}>
<RoleTab>
From <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Loans"]} />, select the cash icon on an active loan to **Record Loan Repayment** — enter the **Amount**, a **Payment Method**, and an optional reference. The loan's **Outstanding** balance updates immediately. When a member declares a payment with a receipt instead, approve it from [Approving member loan repayments](/docs/cooperatives/approving-loan-repayments).

<Screenshot src="/images/cooperatives/repayments-and-schedules-1.webp" alt="Record Loan Repayment modal showing the loan's outstanding balance and an Amount field" />
</RoleTab>
<RoleTab>
Under <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "My Loans"]} />, each loan shows a **Repayment Progress** bar, how much you've paid against the total, and the **Maturity Date**. To pay, transfer to the cooperative's bank account and select **Pay Now** to upload your receipt — an officer confirms it before it counts. See [Paying towards your loan](/docs/cooperatives/paying-your-loan).
</RoleTab>
</RoleTabs>

## Why it matters

A repayment only counts once an officer has seen the money arrive. Members can't record a repayment on their own loan — they declare a payment with a receipt, and an officer approves it, or an officer records it directly. A confirmed repayment also releases the matching share of any savings held against the loan.
