Once a loan is disbursed, it has an outstanding balance that goes down as repayments are recorded against it.

## How it works

<RoleTabs roles={["Officers", "Members"]}>
<RoleTab>
From <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Loans"]} />, select the cash icon on an active loan to **Record Loan Repayment** — enter the **Amount**, a **Payment Method**, and an optional reference. The loan's **Outstanding** balance updates immediately.

<Screenshot src="/images/cooperatives/repayments-and-schedules-1.webp" alt="Record Loan Repayment modal showing the loan's outstanding balance and an Amount field" />
</RoleTab>
<RoleTab>
Under <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "My Loans"]} />, each loan shows a **Repayment Progress** bar, how much you've paid against the total, and the **Maturity Date**. This view is read-only — repayments are recorded by an officer.
</RoleTab>
</RoleTabs>

## Why it matters

Only officers can record repayments — there's no self-service "pay my loan" action for members on this screen. If a member pays outside the app (bank transfer, cash), it only reflects here once an officer records it.
