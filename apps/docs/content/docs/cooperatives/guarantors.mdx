When another member names you as a guarantor on their loan application, you'll see the request here and decide whether to accept it.

## Before you start

Read the loan details carefully — guaranteeing a loan is a real commitment.

<Callout type="danger" title="You're liable if they default">
Accepting a guarantor request means you agree to be responsible for the guaranteed amount if the borrower doesn't repay. Don't accept unless you understand and accept that risk.
</Callout>

## Respond to a request

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Guarantors"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Review the borrower's name, the loan product, the loan amount, and the amount you'd be guaranteeing.</Step>
<Step>Select **Accept** to confirm, or **Decline** and give a reason.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/cooperatives/guarantors-1.webp" alt="Accept Guarantor Request dialog showing the borrower's name, loan amount, your guarantee amount, and a warning about liability" />

## What happens next

If you accept and the loan product's minimum guarantor count is now met, the application automatically moves to **Pending Review** for an officer to approve — see [Reviewing, approving and disbursing loans](/docs/cooperatives/reviewing-and-disbursing-loans). If you decline, the borrower sees your reason and can look for another guarantor.
