Members ask to withdraw; two different officers decide. The member, the reviewer and the approver are always three different people, and the balance moves only at final approval.

## Before you start

Each stage has its own permission, which your association assigns to whichever roles it chooses:

- **Review** (`cooperatives.withdrawals.review`): by default the Treasurer and the Financial Secretary.
- **Approve** (`cooperatives.withdrawals.approve`): by default the Administrator.

## Stage one: review

<Steps>
<Step>Open <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Withdrawals"]} /> (on the phone: **Manage → Cooperatives → Withdrawals**) and the **To review** tab.</Step>
<Step>Check the request: the amount, the account, and the member's reason.</Step>
<Step>Select **Pass on for approval**, or **Reject** and give a reason the member will read.</Step>
</Steps>

## Stage two: approve and pay

<Steps>
<Step>Open the **To approve** tab. You'll only see **Approve and pay** if you hold the approve permission and you didn't review this request.</Step>
<Step>Select **Approve and pay**, say how it was paid out (bank transfer, cash, cheque or other), and add a reference.</Step>
<Step>Confirm. The balance, any savings held for a loan, and the monthly limit are checked once more, then the withdrawal is recorded and the member is told.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="The system records the payout, it doesn't send money">
You pay the member out of the cooperative's bank account yourself; approving records it in the member's savings and the books.
</Callout>

## If your cooperative has only one or two officers

An administrator can switch off two-stage approval in [Cooperative setup](/docs/cooperatives/cooperative-setup). Then one officer decides each request. A request already waiting keeps the rule it was made under.
