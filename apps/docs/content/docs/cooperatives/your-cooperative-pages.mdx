If your association's plan includes the Cooperatives module, your menu has a cooperative section: **Cooperative Dashboard**, **Savings**, **Transactions**, **Loans** and **Cooperative Bank Accounts**. On the phone it is under **Money**.

## Cooperative Dashboard

One tile for every savings type and loan category your cooperative offers, each with its balance in full (₦5,000.00) and the last thing that moved. Because the officers create the types, a new type appears on its own. **Hide** covers the amounts; **Refresh** reloads them.

The **Contribution table** shows, for each month, what you paid *for* that month against what was expected. Each month shows one of: **Paid** (the expected amount or more), **Part paid** (something, but less than expected), **Defaulted** (the month is over and nothing was paid), or **Due** (this month or later, not paid yet). A month with a part payment is never marked Defaulted, so you can see at a glance how much of it is still owed.

## My Transactions

Four views: **Overview** (everything since you joined), **Loans**, **Contributions** and **Shares**. The last three follow the dates you choose. **Excel** (web) or the export button (phone) saves what you see.

## My Cashbook

Your statement in bookkeeping form: shares, savings, loans and loan interest, each with debit, credit and balance. Loan interest is shown the way a loan book does: the whole interest of a loan is **charged** on the day it is paid out, each repayment marks part of it **paid**, and what is left is **Interest still to pay**. A date range starts from your true opening balance, not from zero. **Print PDF** (web) saves it from your browser; on the phone, **PDF** makes the statement to share or save, and **Spreadsheet** gives you the same figures to open in a spreadsheet app.

## My Pending Payments

Every payment you have declared, savings or loan repayment, with its status. A rejected one says why. Select **View proof** to open the receipt you uploaded.

## Cooperative Bank Accounts

Where to send your payments, with a **Copy** button for the account number. Only active accounts are shown.
