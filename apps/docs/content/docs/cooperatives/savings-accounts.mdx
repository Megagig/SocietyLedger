Open a savings account for a member before they can start saving or apply for a loan that requires one.

## Before you start

You need the **Administrator**, **Treasurer** or **Financial Secretary** role.

## Open an account

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Savings"]} /> and select **Open Account**.</Step>
<Step>Choose the **Member**.</Step>
<Step>Choose an **Account Type** — Regular, Thrift, Target or Fixed Deposit.</Step>
<Step>Add optional **Notes**, then select **Create Account**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Cooperatives", "Savings accounts"]} /> and tap **+**.</Step>
<Step>Choose the **Member** and the **Kind of account** — Regular, Thrift, Target or Fixed Deposit.</Step>
<Step>Optionally add a savings target, monthly contribution, interest rate or note, then tap **Open account**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

<Screenshot src="/images/cooperatives/savings-accounts-1.webp" alt="Open Savings Account modal with a member selected, Account Type set to Regular Savings, and a Notes field" />

## What happens next

The account appears in the Savings list with a ₦0 balance, account number (e.g. `SAV-26-000002`) and **Active** status. [Record a deposit](/docs/cooperatives/deposits-and-withdrawals) to start building the balance.
