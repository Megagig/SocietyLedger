Record a deposit or withdrawal against a member's savings account — the balance updates immediately.

## Before you start

You need the **Administrator**, **Treasurer** or **Financial Secretary** role. The account must be **Active**.

## Record a transaction

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Savings"]} /> and find the member's account.</Step>
<Step>Select the deposit (down arrow) or withdrawal (up arrow) icon on their row.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Amount**, choose a **Payment Method**, and add an optional **Reference**.</Step>
<Step>Select **Record Deposit** or **Process Withdrawal** to confirm.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/cooperatives/deposits-and-withdrawals-1.webp" alt="Record Deposit modal for a savings account with an Amount field showing ₦15,000" />

## What happens next

The account's balance, **Total Deposits** or **Total Withdrawals**, updates immediately. There's no separate approval step — a recorded deposit or withdrawal takes effect as soon as you confirm it, so only record one you've actually verified.
