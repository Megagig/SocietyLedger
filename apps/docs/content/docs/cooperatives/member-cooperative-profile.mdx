A member sees their own full cooperative picture — savings, loans, and loans they're guaranteeing — in one place. Officers don't have that same single view for another member yet.

## How it works

<Callout type="note" title="Officers: use the separate lists">
There's currently no single screen for an officer to pull up one member's complete cooperative profile. Find their savings accounts on <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Savings"]} />, their loans on <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Loans"]} />, and their applications on <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Applications"]} /> — each searchable by member name.
</Callout>

A member's own view (under **My Savings** and **My Loans**) shows their account balances, active loans with repayment progress, and any loans they've agreed to guarantee — this is the real data a future officer-facing profile would draw from.

## Why it matters

If you're reviewing a loan application, it's worth checking the applicant's savings balance and any loans they're already guaranteeing separately, since there's no single summary that surfaces all of it together yet.
