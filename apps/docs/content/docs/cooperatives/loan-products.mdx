Create a loan product — a named loan type with its own amount range, interest rate and guarantor requirement — before members can apply for it.

## Before you start

You need the **Administrator**, **Treasurer** or **Financial Secretary** role.

## Create a loan product

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Loan Products"]} /> and select **New Product**.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Product Name** and optional **Description**.</Step>
<Step>Set **Minimum** and **Maximum Amount**, and the **Interest Rate** — Flat or Reducing Balance.</Step>
<Step>Set **Min** and **Max Tenure** in months.</Step>
<Step>Optionally set a **Processing Fee**, **Late Fee** and **Grace Period**.</Step>
<Step>Turn on **Requires Guarantor** if applicable, and set **Minimum Guarantors**.</Step>
<Step>Under **Savings rule**, set the **Savings multiplier** if your cooperative lends against savings — 2 means a member can borrow up to twice their available savings. Leave it empty for no savings requirement.</Step>
<Step>If you set a multiplier, choose whether to turn on **Hold savings while the loan is running**. It is on by default.</Step>
<Step>Select **Create Product**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/cooperatives/loan-products-1.webp" alt="Create loan product form with Product Name, Description, Minimum and Maximum Amount, and Interest Rate fields filled in" />

<Callout type="note" title="Match your constitution">
Cooperatives differ: some lend up to what a member has saved (1), others twice (2) or three times (3). Set the multiplier on each product to match your own rules. [Your savings rule and held savings](/docs/cooperatives/savings-rule-and-held-savings) explains exactly how it works, with examples.
</Callout>

On your phone, go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Cooperatives", "Loan products"]} />, tap **+**, and fill in the same sections — **Amount and time**, **Interest and fees**, **Guarantors and savings** and **Late repayment**. The largest loan can't be less than the smallest, and the longest tenure can't be shorter than the shortest.

## Edit a product

<Steps>
<Step>On <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Loan Products"]} />, select **Edit** on the product's card.</Step>
<Step>Change any field — including the **Savings multiplier** and the hold switch. Set the multiplier to empty to remove the savings requirement.</Step>
<Step>Use **Product is active** to stop members applying for it, then select **Save Changes**.</Step>
</Steps>

On your phone, tap a product to change it, or switch it off with **On**. Changes apply to new applications, and to applications still waiting for approval or payout, which are re-checked against the current rule. Savings already held against a loan that has been paid out stay as they were.

## What happens next

The product appears in your Loan Products list and immediately becomes available for members to select on [Applying for a loan](/docs/cooperatives/applying-for-a-loan). Deactivate a product instead of deleting it once members have applied against it.
