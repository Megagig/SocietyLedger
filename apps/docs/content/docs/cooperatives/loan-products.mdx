Create a loan product — a named loan type with its own amount range, interest rate and guarantor requirement — before members can apply for it.

## Before you start

You need the **Administrator**, **Treasurer** or **Financial Secretary** role.

## Create a loan product

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Loan Products"]} /> and select **New Product**.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Product Name** and optional **Description**.</Step>
<Step>Set **Minimum** and **Maximum Amount**, and the **Interest Rate** — Flat or Reducing Balance.</Step>
<Step>Set **Min** and **Max Tenure** in months.</Step>
<Step>Optionally set a **Processing Fee**, **Late Fee** and **Grace Period**.</Step>
<Step>Turn on **Requires Guarantor** if applicable, and set **Minimum Guarantors**.</Step>
<Step>Select **Create Product**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/cooperatives/loan-products-1.webp" alt="Create loan product form with Product Name, Description, Minimum and Maximum Amount, and Interest Rate fields filled in" />

## What happens next

The product appears in your Loan Products list and immediately becomes available for members to select on [Applying for a loan](/docs/cooperatives/applying-for-a-loan). Deactivate a product instead of deleting it once members have applied against it.
