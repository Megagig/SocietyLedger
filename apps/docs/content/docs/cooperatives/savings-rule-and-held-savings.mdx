Many cooperatives only lend a member a multiple of what they have saved — twice their savings, three times, or exactly what they've saved. Each cooperative sets its own rule, on each loan product, to match its constitution.

## How it works

### The savings multiplier

On a loan product, the **Savings multiplier** says how many times their **available savings** a member can borrow.

| Multiplier | A member with ₦100,000 available can borrow up to |
|---|---|
| 1 | ₦100,000 |
| 2 | ₦200,000 |
| 3 | ₦300,000 |
| empty | No savings requirement — only the product's own maximum applies |

A member's limit on a product is the smaller of this figure and the product's **Maximum Amount**.

### Held savings

When **Hold savings while the loan is running** is on, paying out a loan puts part of the member's savings on hold: the **loan amount ÷ the multiplier**. A ₦200,000 loan at a multiplier of 2 holds ₦100,000.

The held money is **not spent or debited**. It stays in the member's savings account and still counts in their balance, but:

- it can't be withdrawn,
- it can't be counted again to borrow a second loan, and
- it is released as the loan is repaid — in proportion to the principal repaid, and all of it once the loan is fully paid off.

Example: on that ₦200,000 loan, when ₦50,000 of principal has been repaid, ₦25,000 of the hold is released.

### Balance, held and available

Members see three figures on **My Savings**: their **total balance**, how much is **held against their loan**, and how much is **available**. Available is the total balance minus anything held, and it is what the borrowing limit is based on.

## Why it matters

The rule is checked when a member applies, again when an officer approves the application, and again when the loan is paid out. A member who withdrew savings — or took another loan — after applying can't slip past it, and applications that no longer fit are refused with a message showing the new limit.

## What you can do

- **Officers:** set the multiplier and the hold switch in [Setting up loan products](/docs/cooperatives/loan-products).
- **Members:** see your limit and what will be held in [Applying for a loan](/docs/cooperatives/applying-for-a-loan).
