Pay into your cooperative's bank account first, then tell the app what you paid. One bank transfer is one payment with one receipt, and it can cover several savings types at once, for example your monthly savings and your shares.

## Before you start

You need the transfer receipt (a photo or PDF, up to 5MB). The cooperative's account details are under **Cooperative Bank Accounts**.

## Submit a payment

<Steps>
<Step>Open <MenuPath items={["Savings", "Savings"]} /> (on the phone: **Money → Savings → Pay into savings**).</Step>
<Step>Under **Payment confirmation**, choose the **Savings type**. If your cooperative has a usual monthly amount for it, the amount fills in.</Step>
<Step>Check the **Amount** and the **Month-Year** the payment is for, then select **Add**. Repeat for each savings type you paid in the same transfer. The same type for the same month is added together.</Step>
<Step>Check the **Total** matches your transfer, choose the date you paid and the account you paid into, and attach your **evidence of payment**.</Step>
<Step>Select **Submit payment**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="One transfer, one receipt">
If you made two separate transfers, submit them as two payments, each with its own receipt. A payment never takes more than one receipt.
</Callout>

## What happens next

Nothing is added to your savings yet. Your payment shows under **My Pending Payments** as *Awaiting confirmation*. When an officer confirms the money arrived, every line is added to your savings at once; if they can't, you are told why so you can fix it. See [Your cooperative pages](/docs/cooperatives/your-cooperative-pages).
