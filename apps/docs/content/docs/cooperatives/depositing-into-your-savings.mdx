Save money with your cooperative by paying into its bank account, then telling the app about the payment with your receipt. Your savings go up once an officer confirms the money arrived.

## Before you start

- An officer must have [opened a savings account](/docs/cooperatives/savings-accounts) for you, and it must be **Active**.
- Make the bank transfer first, and keep the receipt as a photo or PDF (up to 5MB).

<Callout type="note" title="Nothing is added until it's confirmed">
Submitting a deposit does not change your balance. It shows as **Awaiting confirmation** until an officer checks the cooperative's bank statement and approves it.
</Callout>

## Declare a deposit

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "My Savings"]} /> and find the account you're saving into.</Step>
<Step>Select **Make a Deposit**. The cooperative's bank account details are shown, with a button to copy the account number.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Amount you paid** and the **Date of payment** (it can't be in the future).</Step>
<Step>Attach your **Bank receipt** — a JPEG, PNG, GIF or PDF.</Step>
<Step>Optionally add the **Transfer reference** from your receipt and a **Note** for the officer.</Step>
<Step>Select **Submit Deposit**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot pending="My Savings page with the Make a Deposit form open: the cooperative's bank account, Amount, Date of payment, Bank receipt and Submit Deposit" />

## What happens next

Your deposit appears under **My Deposits** on the same page, marked **Awaiting confirmation**.

- **Confirmed** — the amount is added to your balance, and you get a notification.
- **Not approved** — nothing is added. The officer's reason is shown next to the deposit, and you also get a notification. You can submit a corrected deposit.

You can open the receipt you uploaded from the **Receipt** link beside each deposit.

<Callout type="tip" title="Withdrawals">
Members can't withdraw from the app. Withdrawals are recorded by an officer — see [Recording deposits and withdrawals](/docs/cooperatives/deposits-and-withdrawals). Savings held against an active loan can't be withdrawn at all until the loan is repaid, see [Your savings rule and held savings](/docs/cooperatives/savings-rule-and-held-savings).
</Callout>
