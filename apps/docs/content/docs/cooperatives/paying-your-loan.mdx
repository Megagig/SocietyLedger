Repay your loan by paying into the cooperative's bank account and telling the app about the payment with your receipt. Your loan balance goes down once an officer confirms the money arrived.

## Before you start

- You need an **Active** or **Overdue** loan under <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "My Loans"]} />.
- Make the bank transfer first, and keep the receipt as a photo or PDF (up to 5MB).

<Callout type="note" title="Your balance doesn't change until it's confirmed">
A submitted payment shows as **Awaiting confirmation**. It is only recorded against your loan when an officer approves it.
</Callout>

## Declare a payment

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "My Loans"]} /> and find the loan. Select **Pay Now** (on the next-payment banner, or on an overdue loan).</Step>
<Step>Check the amount you still owe, and the cooperative's bank account shown in the form.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Amount you paid** — it can't be more than you still owe — and the **Date of payment**.</Step>
<Step>Attach your **Bank receipt** — a JPEG, PNG, GIF or PDF.</Step>
<Step>Optionally add the **Transfer reference** and a **Note**, then select **Submit Payment**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot pending="My Loans page with the Pay Towards Your Loan form open: loan summary, You still owe, bank account, Amount, Date, Bank receipt" />

## What happens next

Your payment appears under **My Payments** on the same page.

- **Confirmed** — the repayment is recorded on your loan and your outstanding balance goes down. If part of your savings is held against the loan, a matching share is released. You get a notification.
- **Not approved** — nothing is recorded. The officer's reason is shown next to the payment, and you get a notification.

See [Your savings rule and held savings](/docs/cooperatives/savings-rule-and-held-savings) for how savings are released as you repay.
