Apply for a loan in four steps — choose a product, set the amount and tenure, state your purpose, and add guarantors if the product requires them.

## Before you start

Any member can apply. If the loan product requires guarantors, line up willing members before you start — they'll need to accept before your application can be reviewed.

## Apply for a loan

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Apply for Loan"]} />.</Step>
<Step>**Select Product** — choose from the loan products your association offers. If a product lends against savings, its card shows how much you can borrow on it.</Step>
<Step>**Loan Details** — enter the **Loan Amount** and **Tenure (months)**, within the product's limits. For a product with a savings rule, you'll see your savings, how much is available, and the most you can borrow; the amount can't go above that.</Step>
<Step>**Purpose & Guarantors** — describe what the loan is for, and choose guarantors if the product requires them. Search for guarantors by name or member number; only names are shown.</Step>
<Step>**Review & Submit** — check the details, then select **Submit Application**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/cooperatives/applying-for-a-loan-1.webp" alt="Select a Loan Product step showing available products with amount range, interest rate and tenure" />

<Callout type="note" title="Part of your savings will be held">
If the product holds savings, the amount that will be held is shown on the **Loan Details** and **Review & Submit** steps. It stays in your savings account but can't be withdrawn until you've repaid the loan. See [Your savings rule and held savings](/docs/cooperatives/savings-rule-and-held-savings).
</Callout>

## What happens next

If the product requires guarantors, the application starts as **Pending Guarantors** until enough of them accept. Each guarantor is notified as soon as you submit, and you're notified when they answer — see [Guarantors](/docs/cooperatives/guarantors). Once guarantors are in place (or if none are required), it moves to **Pending Review** for an officer to approve.
