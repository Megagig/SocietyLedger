When a member saves by paying the cooperative's bank account, they upload their receipt and the deposit waits here. Approving it is what adds the money to their savings.

## Before you start

You need the **Administrator** or **Treasurer** role. Keep the cooperative's bank statement open so you can match each deposit to a real credit.

<Callout type="warning" title="Only approve money you can see">
Approving takes effect immediately and can't be undone from this screen. A receipt can be wrong or edited, so match the amount and date to your own bank statement, not just the uploaded image.
</Callout>

## Review a deposit

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Savings Deposits"]} />. The **Awaiting** tab shows deposits that need a decision.</Step>
<Step>Read the member, amount, payment date, account and any reference or note. Select **View receipt** to open the uploaded file.</Step>
<Step>Select **Approve** if the money is in the cooperative's account — confirm with **Yes, add to savings**.</Step>
<Step>Or select **Reject**, give the member a **Reason**, and confirm.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Cooperatives", "Deposit requests"]} />. The **Waiting** tab shows deposits that need a decision.</Step>
<Step>Read the member, amount, payment date, account and any reference or note. Tap **View receipt** — an image opens in the zoom viewer, a PDF in your phone's viewer.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Approve** if the money is in the cooperative's account, and confirm.</Step>
<Step>Or tap **Reject**, give the member a **Reason**, and confirm.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## What happens next

- **Approved** — the amount is added to the member's savings account straight away and recorded as a deposit in its history (and in your accounting, if integration is on). The member is notified.
- **Rejected** — nothing changes in the account. The member is notified and shown your reason.

**You can't decide your own deposit.** If you are also a member and the deposit is yours, another officer has to approve or reject it; you'll see a note instead of the buttons. A deposit can only be reviewed once. If two officers open the same deposit, the second one is told it has already been reviewed. The **Approved** and **Rejected** tabs keep the history.

You can still record a deposit directly, without a member's receipt, as described in [Recording deposits and withdrawals](/docs/cooperatives/deposits-and-withdrawals).
