A member who has paid into the cooperative's bank account submits a **payment request**: one transfer, one receipt, and one or more lines (a savings type, an amount, and the month it is for). The balance doesn't move until you approve it.

## Before you start

You need the **ledger.create** permission (Treasurer, Financial Secretary, Administrator). You can't decide your own payment; another officer has to.

## Decide a payment

<Steps>
<Step>Open <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Payment Requests"]} /> (on the phone: **Manage → Cooperatives → Payment requests**). The **Awaiting** tab lists what is waiting.</Step>
<Step>Select **View proof** and check the receipt against the bank statement: the amount, the date and the account.</Step>
<Step>Check each line. The total is the member's transfer; the lines say which savings types and months it covers.</Step>
<Step>Select **Approve**, or **Reject** and give a reason the member will read.</Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

Approving adds every line to the member's savings **in one step, or none of them**: if one line can't be posted (for example the member's account for that type is closed), nothing is added and the request stays in the queue. The member is told either way, and the payment shows in their cashbook and contribution table under the month it was for.
