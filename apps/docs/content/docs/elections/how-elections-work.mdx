Every election moves through the same stages, in order. A member of the [Election Committee](/docs/elections/election-committee) takes each step themselves — nothing advances on its own.

| Stage | What the committee does | What members see |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | Create the election and its positions. | Nothing yet. |
| Nominations Open | Members nominate themselves; the committee adds and approves candidates. | The election, open for nominations. |
| Nominations Closed | Approve or reject the nominations still waiting. | Nominations are over. |
| Voting Open | Open voting once every nomination has been decided. | The ballot, ready to vote. |
| Voting Closed | Close voting once the window ends. | The election shows as closed; no results yet. |
| Results Published | Publish the results. | The results — see [Publishing results](/docs/elections/publishing-results). |

An election can also go **straight from Draft to Voting Open** when the committee has put the candidates in itself and approved them, skipping the nominations stages. It can be **cancelled** from any stage before the results are published — with a reason, which members can read.

<Callout type="note" title="Each step happens once">
If two committee members tap the same step at the same moment, or you tap twice, only the first one counts. The other is told the election has already moved on, and the screen refreshes to show where it is now.
</Callout>

## What each step checks

- **Opening voting** needs an approved candidate for every position, no nomination left waiting, and a voting end date that has not already passed.
- **Publishing results** is refused if the election needed a minimum turnout and too few members voted. Cancel the election and run it again instead.
- **Candidates** can be approved or rejected until voting opens, even after nominations close; they cannot withdraw once voting has opened.

## Why it matters

Each stage is a deliberate, one-way gate — once voting closes, no more votes count, and once results are published the election is on record. Plan your dates before you open nominations; the election's details can only be changed while it is still a Draft.
