Once voting closes, the committee can read the results straight away. Members see them only when the committee publishes them.

## Before you start

Voting must be **Closed** first — see [Opening and closing voting](/docs/elections/running-the-vote). Reading the results before they are published needs the committee's **see results early** power, and publishing needs the **publish results** power.

## Read and publish

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Open the election from <MenuPath items={["Governance", "Elections"]} /> and select **View Results**.</Step>
<Step>Check the count. Back on the election, select **Publish Results**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Open the election from <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Voting", "Elections"]} /> and tap **See the results**.</Step>
<Step>Check the count. Back on the election, tap **Publish results** and confirm.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## What the results show

- **Turnout** — how many members voted out of how many could, counting each member once however many positions they voted in.
- **Minimum turnout** — whether the election needed one and whether it was met.
- **Each position** — every approved candidate with their votes and share, how many abstained, and the winner.

<Callout type="note" title="A tie has no winner">
If two or more candidates finish level at the top, none is marked as the winner and the screen says who is level. The committee decides what happens next — for example, cancelling and holding a run-off.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Results below the minimum turnout can't be published">
If the election needed a minimum turnout and too few members voted, **Publish Results** is refused. Cancel the election and run it again; the reason you give is visible to members.
</Callout>

Before they are published, the results are hidden from everyone but the committee — including officers who are not on it — and nobody at all can see a running count while voting is still open.

## What happens next

The election becomes **Results Published**, every member can see the result, and it stays in the elections list as a record.
