Once voting closes, publish the results to mark the election as officially decided.

## Before you start

You need the **Administrator** role. Voting must be **Closed** first — see [Opening and closing voting](/docs/elections/running-the-vote).

## Publish results

<Steps>
<Step>Open the election from <MenuPath items={["Governance", "Elections"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Select **Publish Results**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/elections/publishing-results-1.webp" alt="Election page showing Results Published status after publishing, with the candidate list still visible" />

<Callout type="warning" title="Vote counts aren't shown anywhere yet">
Publishing results changes the election's status to **Results Published**, but there's currently no screen — not even this one — that shows how many votes each candidate received or who won. The votes are counted and stored correctly; announce results to your members outside the app for now (a notice, an announcement, or at your AGM) until a results view is added.
</Callout>

## What happens next

The election's status becomes **Results Published**, and it stays available in your elections list as a historical record.
