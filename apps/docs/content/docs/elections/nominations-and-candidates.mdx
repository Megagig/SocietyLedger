Before voting can open, every position needs at least one approved candidate. A candidate gets there in two steps: they are put forward, then another member of the [Election Committee](/docs/elections/election-committee) approves them.

## Before you start

You need the committee's **manage candidates** power. Candidates can be added while the election is a **Draft** or **Nominations Open**; members can only nominate themselves while nominations are open.

## Put a candidate forward

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Open the election from <MenuPath items={["Governance", "Elections"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Under the position, select **Add Candidate**, search for the member, and add an optional manifesto.</Step>
<Step>Members who want to stand themselves select **Nominate Yourself** on the position while nominations are open.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Open the election from <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Voting", "Elections"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Under the position, tap **Add a candidate**, search by name or member number, pick the member, and add an optional manifesto.</Step>
<Step>Members who want to stand themselves do it from <MenuPath items={["Community", "Elections"]} /> while nominations are open.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

The committee can search every active member from here, even though committee members don't have the full members list.

## Approve or reject

Every nomination starts as **Waiting**. A committee member selects **Approve**, or **Reject** with a reason that the member can read. Only **Approved** candidates appear on the ballot.

<Callout type="note" title="You can't decide your own nomination">
If you are standing yourself, another member of the committee has to approve or reject you. You'll see a note instead of the buttons.
</Callout>

You can keep deciding nominations after **Close Nominations**, right up until voting opens. Voting cannot open while a nomination is still waiting, so the election page tells you how many are left.

If your association has rules for who can stand, someone who doesn't meet them can't nominate themselves or be added, and is told why — the committee can make an exception for one member. See [Who can vote and who can stand](/docs/elections/voting-rules).

A candidate can withdraw themselves until voting opens. After that, nobody can be removed from the ballot.

## What happens next

When every position has an approved candidate and none is waiting, open voting — see [Opening and closing voting](/docs/elections/running-the-vote).
