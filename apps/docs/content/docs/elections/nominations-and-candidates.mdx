Once nominations open, candidates need to be added to each position before voting can start — but adding them isn't currently a self-service task.

## How it works today

<Callout type="warning" title="No screen adds candidates yet">
Selecting **Add Candidate** on an open election currently goes nowhere — there's no page behind it yet. There's also no button for a member to nominate themselves, and no screen to approve, reject or withdraw a candidate. If your association needs candidates added to a real election, contact support with the member's name and the position — your platform contact can add them directly.
</Callout>

Once a candidate has been added, you'll see them listed under their position with their name, manifesto (if given), and a status — **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected** or **Withdrawn**. Only **Approved** candidates appear on the ballot when voting opens.

<Screenshot src="/images/elections/nominations-and-candidates-1.webp" alt="Election detail page with nominations open, showing one approved candidate under Welfare Chair with their manifesto and an Approved badge" />

## Why it matters

Don't open voting until every position has at least one approved candidate — a position with no candidates will show as empty on the ballot, with nothing for members to vote on.
