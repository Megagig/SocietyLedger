By default, every **active** member of your association can vote once voting opens — there is no separate eligibility list to manage, and the number of members who could vote is fixed the moment voting opens.

If your association has its own rules — only members in good financial standing vote, for example — see [Who can vote and who can stand](/docs/elections/voting-rules). With rules set, eligibility is checked at the moment someone votes, and the number of members who can vote is counted as it stands now.

## How it works

Turnout is the share of those members who voted, and each member counts once, however many positions they voted in.

**Every member can follow it live.** While voting is open, My Voting (and the election's own page, on the web and on the phone) shows how many members have voted, a bar against the minimum turnout if there is one, a chart of how it built up over the voting window, and how many have voted on each position. It refreshes every few seconds and marks the election **Live**.

It shows turnout only. Nobody — members, officers or the committee — can see who is ahead or who anyone voted for until the results are published, so watching it can't sway the vote.

## A minimum turnout

When you create an election you can require a minimum share of members to vote for the result to count. The comparison is exact — 49.6% never counts as meeting a 50% minimum. If it is not met, the results can still be read by the committee, but **Publish Results** is refused: cancel the election and run it again.

## Why it matters

If a member says they can't see an election to vote, check they are an **active** member — someone marked inactive or terminated can't vote, and they are not counted in the turnout either.
