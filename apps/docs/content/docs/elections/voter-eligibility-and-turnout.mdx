Every **active** member of your association can vote once voting opens — there is no separate eligibility list to manage. The number of members who could vote is fixed the moment voting opens.

## How it works

Turnout is the share of those members who voted, and each member counts once, however many positions they voted in. You can see it:

- on the election's page, as soon as voting is open — a count of members who have voted, never who they voted for;
- on the results, once voting has closed.

## A minimum turnout

When you create an election you can require a minimum share of members to vote for the result to count. The comparison is exact — 49.6% never counts as meeting a 50% minimum. If it is not met, the results can still be read by the committee, but **Publish Results** is refused: cancel the election and run it again.

## Why it matters

If a member says they can't see an election to vote, check they are an **active** member — someone marked inactive or terminated can't vote, and they are not counted in the turnout either.
