Every active member of your association is eligible to vote once an election's voting window opens — there's no separate eligibility list to manage.

## How it works

<Screenshot src="/images/elections/voter-eligibility-and-turnout-1.webp" alt="Voting & Elections hub showing Total Elections, Active Elections, Total Polls and Active Polls stat cards, plus active elections and polls lists" />

The <MenuPath items={["Governance", "Voting & Elections"]} /> hub is where you manage both elections and polls from one place — total and active counts, your currently open items, and quick links to create a new election or poll.

<Callout type="note" title="Turnout isn't a report yet">
How many eligible voters an election has, and how many actually voted, aren't currently shown anywhere in the interface — the numbers are tracked, but there's no turnout screen to view them on. Once voting closes, you'll know how many votes were cast from your own count of members you spoke to, rather than from a report in the app.
</Callout>

## Why it matters

If a member says they can't see an election to vote, it's worth confirming they're an **active** member — someone marked inactive or terminated won't see it.
