Create a poll for a quick decision — a venue change, a meeting time, anything that doesn't need a full election.

## Before you start

You need the **manage polls** permission. The Administrator and the [Election Committee](/docs/elections/election-committee) have it by default.

## Create the poll

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Governance", "Polls"]} /> and select **New Poll**.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Poll Question** and an optional description.</Step>
<Step>Choose a **Poll Type** — Single Choice, Multiple Choice or Yes/No. Yes/No fills in its two options automatically.</Step>
<Step>For the other types, enter each **Option** — select **Add Option** for more.</Step>
<Step>Set the **Start Date** and **End Date**, choose whether responses are **Anonymous** and whether members can see results while it is open, then select **Create Poll**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/elections/creating-a-poll-1.webp" alt="Create New Poll form with a Yes/No question, pre-filled Yes and No options, and start/end date fields" />
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Voting", "Polls"]} /> and tap **+**.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Question**, choose **How members answer** — pick one, pick several, or yes or no — and set when it starts and ends.</Step>
<Step>Add the answers to choose from, and for a multiple-choice poll the most answers a member can pick.</Step>
<Step>Choose whether answers are secret and whether results show while it is open, then tap **Add poll**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

A poll can be changed until it starts. The end date has to be after the start date, and must still be in the future when you start it.

## What happens next

The poll is created as a **Draft**. Start it when you're ready for members to respond — see [Poll results](/docs/elections/poll-results) for what members and officers see while it's running.
