Create a poll for a quick decision — a venue change, a meeting time, anything that doesn't need a full election.

## Before you start

You need the **Administrator** role.

## Create the poll

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Governance", "Polls"]} /> and select **New Poll**.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Poll Question** and an optional description.</Step>
<Step>Choose a **Poll Type** — Single Choice, Multiple Choice, Yes/No, or Ranked Choice. Yes/No fills in its two options automatically.</Step>
<Step>For other types, enter each **Option** — select **Add Option** for more.</Step>
<Step>Set the **Start Date** and **End Date**.</Step>
<Step>Choose whether responses are **Anonymous** and whether members can **change their response**, then select **Create Poll**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/elections/creating-a-poll-1.webp" alt="Create New Poll form with a Yes/No question, pre-filled Yes and No options, and start/end date fields" />

## What happens next

The poll is created in **Draft**. Select **Start Poll** on its page when you're ready for members to respond — see [Poll results](/docs/elections/poll-results) for what members and officers see while it's running.
