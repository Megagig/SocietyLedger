Once a poll is active, members respond from its own page — officers can close it and see the final breakdown at any time.

## How it works

While a poll is **Active**, a member sees the options and a **Submit Vote** button; after responding, they see "Your response has been recorded" — but not the running tally, unless the poll was explicitly set to show results before it ends.

<Screenshot src="/images/elections/poll-results-1.webp" alt="Closed poll showing Results with Yes at 100% (1 vote) and No at 0%" />

An officer selects **Close Poll** when the response window should end. The Results panel then shows each option's vote count and percentage, visible to anyone who opens the poll.

## Why it matters

Responses are final — a member can't take back a submitted vote unless the poll was created with **Allow members to change their response** turned on.
