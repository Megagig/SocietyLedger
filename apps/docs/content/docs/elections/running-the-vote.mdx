Open voting once every position has at least one approved candidate, and close it when your voting window ends.

## Before you start

You need the **Administrator** role. Every position should have an approved candidate before you open voting — see [Nominations and candidates](/docs/elections/nominations-and-candidates).

<Callout type="danger" title="Contact support to open voting today">
Closing nominations currently leaves the election showing as **Draft**, and there's no **Start Voting** button on a Draft election in the interface — only **Open Nominations** or **Cancel**. Until this is fixed, ask your platform contact to open voting for you once your candidates are approved.
</Callout>

## Vote and close

<Steps>
<Step>Once voting opens, every eligible member sees the election's positions and their approved candidates.</Step>
<Step>A member selects a candidate for each position (or **Abstain**, if the election allows it) and selects **Submit Vote** — one submission per position, and it can't be changed afterward.</Step>
<Step>When your voting window ends, an officer selects **Close Voting** on the election's page.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/elections/running-the-vote-1.webp" alt="Member's view of an open election after voting, showing a green checkmark and 'You have voted for this position' under each position" />

## What happens next

The election shows as **Voting Closed**. No more votes are accepted. See [Publishing results](/docs/elections/publishing-results) for the next step.
