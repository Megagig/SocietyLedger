A collector goes to the savers, takes their daily amount, and brings it to the circle. SocietyLedger replaces the notebook: each payment is recorded the moment you take it, the saver is told, and at the end of the day you hand the cash over and an officer counts it.

This page is the whole day in one place. The two pages after it cover the details of [recording cash](/docs/ajo-thrift/recording-cash) and [handing over](/docs/ajo-thrift/handing-over-at-the-end-of-the-day).

## Before you start

- You need the **collector** role, and the circle's officers must have **assigned** savers to you. You can only record cash from people assigned to you.
- **You need internet.** Cash is recorded straight away and nothing is saved on your phone for later, so a payment is never in the app twice or missing. When your phone is offline, the screen says so and the buttons wait.
- Your Ajo tier must include collector thrift (**Growth** and above).

## What the four figures mean

- **Expected today:** what your savers' daily targets add up to.
- **Collected today:** what you have recorded so far today.
- **Carried over:** cash from an earlier day that you have not yet handed in. For example, a saver's ₦1,000.00 you were short on yesterday.
- **Cash in your hand:** what you have recorded and not yet handed over. At the end of the day, this is what you should have to give the office.

## What savers see

As soon as you record a payment, the saver sees it in their own savings as **Cash received, awaiting confirmation**, and is told. It becomes **Confirmed**, and counts towards their savings, only after an officer counts your handover. Savers can always see where their cash is: with you, handed to the office, or confirmed.

## Good habits

- **Record cash straight away**, in front of the saver, not at the end of the day from memory.
- **Never let a saver's payment go unrecorded.** If it is not in the app, the saver cannot prove they paid.
- **Hand over every day.** A handover covers one day, and you cannot record cash for a day after you have handed it over.
- **Keep carried-over cash separate.** It shows up in your handover so the count can match it.

<Callout type="note" title="You cannot confirm your own cash">
Someone else counts your handover. That is not a sign of distrust. It protects you: when the count matches, the record shows you handed in everything.
</Callout>
