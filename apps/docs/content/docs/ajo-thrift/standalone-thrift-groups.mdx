Thrift runs in two kinds of account. An **association with the Thrift add-on** is an association (with dues, finances, meetings and so on) that also runs savings circles. A **standalone thrift group** signed up only for Thrift. It has no association side at all, so everything you see is about circles. The circles themselves work the same in both: the same screens for paying, confirming, payouts, rules, the draw and reports.

## What a standalone group sees

**On the web**

- **Thrift Home** is your dashboard. An admin or officer sees how many circles are running and open, how many people are in them, and shortcuts. A member sees their circles, what they are behind by, and the next payment.
- The menu has **Thrift Home**, **My Thrift** (your circles, one entry per circle, the Circle board of each rotating pot, your payout account and circle notifications), **My Payments**, and for those who run things **Thrift Circles**, **Payments**, **Collect**, **Thrift Reports** and **People**, then **My Profile**, **Notifications**, **Help & Support** and **Settings**.
- There is no Finances, Governance, Meetings, Elections, Cooperatives or Welfare. If you type an association address, SocietyLedger sends you back to Thrift Home.
- **Settings** keeps only what a group has: the group's profile, roles, users, notifications, the audit log, data and privacy, and the plan.
- **People** (the members list) shows contact details, when each person joined, their status, whether they can sign in, and the circles they are in. It has no categories, occupations or dues, because those belong to associations.

**On the phone**

- You land on the **Thrift** tab. There is no association Home, Community or Money ledger.
- A **Circle** tab appears for people in a rotating pot, and for the admin and officers of one. It is the circle's board.
- A **Manage** tab appears for admins, officers and collectors, with the circles, payments, collection, people and reports they are allowed to use.
- **Profile** has your details, security, notifications and help. "Switch group" replaces "Switch association".

## Words

A standalone group calls its savings groups **circles**, and the account itself a **group**. Where an association says "association", a standalone group says "group". The roles are **Administrator**, **Officer**, **Collector** and **Member**. See [Roles in a thrift group](/docs/ajo-thrift/roles-in-a-thrift-group).

## Plans and billing

A standalone group pays only for Thrift: one Ajo tier, chosen by how many people save with you. There is no association plan. See [Plans, billing and limits](/docs/ajo-thrift/plans-billing-and-limits).

## If something looks missing

The most common reasons are your role (you only see what you may use), a circle that has not started yet (payments open when it does), or a setting such as the Circle board waiting for its notice period. See the [FAQ](/docs/ajo-thrift/troubleshooting-and-faq).
