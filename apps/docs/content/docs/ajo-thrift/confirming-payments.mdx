Until an officer confirms it, a payment is only a claim. Confirming is how a transfer becomes money in the circle, and it is the step that gives the member a receipt. This page covers confirming a transfer, turning one down, and reversing a payment that should not have been confirmed.

## Before you start

- You need the **reconcile** permission. A circle's officers usually have it.
- Have the circle's **bank statement** open. SocietyLedger never sees your bank account, so the check is yours.
- Cash recorded by a collector is not confirmed here. It is confirmed when the collector's handover is counted. See [Handovers and counting cash](/docs/ajo-thrift/handovers-and-counting-cash).

## Confirm a transfer

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Thrift Circles"]} />, open the circle, and choose the <UiLabel>Payments</UiLabel> tab. The list opens on **Transfers to confirm**, oldest first.</Step>
<Step>Look at a payment's amount, name, reference, and when it was reported. Select <UiLabel>See slip</UiLabel> to open the proof the member attached.</Step>
<Step>Find the same amount in the circle's bank statement.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Confirm</UiLabel>. Check the amount, add a short note under **How you checked** (for example "seen in the GTBank statement, 30 Oct"), and select <UiLabel>Confirm payment</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Thrift circles"]} />, open the circle, and tap **Payments**.</Step>
<Step>Tap a payment to see its details and open the slip.</Step>
<Step>Check the circle's bank statement for the same amount, then tap **Confirm payment**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## Warnings you may see

- **A possible duplicate.** SocietyLedger noticed that the same proof file, or the same bank reference, was used on another payment in your association. Look again. If it really is a different payment, tick **I checked, and this is a different payment** to go on. This catches a slip used twice.
- **"I am the only person who can confirm here."** You cannot normally confirm a payment you recorded, or your own. A circle with one person who can confirm may confirm its own, but only by ticking this box and writing a note. It is flagged on the [accountability page](/docs/ajo-thrift/officer-accountability) as **confirmed by the person who recorded it**.

## What happens next

The payment becomes **Confirmed**, is counted towards what the member owes, and is shown in the circle's figures. The member gets a receipt and a message, and the instalments it covers are marked paid, oldest first. If a late fee was waiting to be paid, it is paid after the instalments.

## Turn a payment down

If the money is not in the statement, or the slip does not match, select <UiLabel>Turn down</UiLabel>, write a reason, and save. The reason is shown to the member. A turned-down payment is never counted, and nothing needs undoing.

## Reverse a payment

If a payment was confirmed by mistake, for example it was entered twice, you can reverse it.

<Steps>
<Step>Under **Show**, choose <UiLabel>Confirmed</UiLabel>, and find the payment.</Step>
<Step>Select **Reverse**, write the reason, and save.</Step>
</Steps>

A reversal never deletes anything. It writes a new entry that cancels the first, with your name and your reason, and the amount comes off the member's record. The member's instalments go back to unpaid. A payment can be reversed once, and a reversal cannot itself be reversed: to put the money back, record the payment again.

<Callout type="warning" title="Reversing a payment that funded a payout">
If a payout has been prepared from a round this payment funded, reversing the payment leaves the round short, and that payout cannot go on until the round is funded again. Speak to your admin before reversing a payment from a round that has a payout in progress.
</Callout>
