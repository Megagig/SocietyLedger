A **guarantor** is someone who agrees to stand behind a member: a sister, a brother, a friend or an employer. In SocietyLedger, a guarantor is a **recorded agreement and nothing more**. It is an honest record that a named person was told what the circle expects and agreed. It does not create a legal guarantee, SocietyLedger does not enforce one, and **nothing is ever taken from a guarantor automatically**.

Whether a guarantor is needed is a circle rule: **Not needed**, **Optional** or **Required before joining**. See [Rules, grace and late fees](/docs/ajo-thrift/rules-grace-and-late-fees).

## Before you start

- You need the **manage** permission.
- If the circle requires a guarantor, the member stays in **Waiting for approval** until one is recorded. The **Waiting for** column on the Members tab says so.
- Have the guarantor's **name**, **phone number** and **relationship to the member**.

## Record an agreement in person

Use this when the guarantor is with you, or you have spoken to them by phone and they agreed.

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Thrift Circles"]} />, open the circle, and choose the <UiLabel>Members</UiLabel> tab.</Step>
<Step>On the member's row, select <UiLabel>Guarantor</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Show the wording</UiLabel> and read it to the guarantor. This is exactly what they are agreeing to, and it is the text that is saved.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Guarantor's name**, their **Phone**, and the **Relationship to the member**.</Step>
<Step>Under **How they agreed**, write how it happened, for example "Read the wording to her on the phone on 29 August, and she agreed."</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Record agreement</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Thrift circles"]} />, open the circle, and tap **Members**.</Step>
<Step>Tap the member, then tap **Guarantor**.</Step>
<Step>Read the wording to the guarantor, enter their details and how they agreed, and tap **Record agreement**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## Or send the guarantor a link

Use this when the guarantor is not with you. They read the wording and accept it themselves, on their own phone.

<Steps>
<Step>Open the same **Guarantor** window.</Step>
<Step>Enter the guarantor's name, phone and relationship, and select <UiLabel>Create the link</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Copy the **Link for the guarantor** and send it to them by WhatsApp or SMS. The window shows the name of the person they will be agreeing for.</Step>
<Step>The guarantor opens the link in their browser, reads the wording and accepts or declines. They do not need an account.</Step>
</Steps>

A few things to know about the link:

- It can be used **once**, and it expires after **7 days**.
- **Creating another link replaces the first**, so an old link in someone's messages cannot be used.
- You are told when the guarantor accepts, and the member is activated if nothing else is missing.

<Callout type="note" title="Guarantor consent opens in a browser">
On a phone, you create and share the link from the app, and the guarantor opens it in their browser. They never need the SocietyLedger app.
</Callout>

## What happens next

The member's row shows **guarantor on file**. If a new agreement is recorded later, it replaces the earlier one, and the earlier one stays on file, so the history is complete.

If someone later falls behind, a guarantor is **not** charged. The circle can contact the guarantor, but SocietyLedger will only record that it did. See [Late fees, default cases, repayment plans and disputes](/docs/ajo-thrift/late-fees-defaults-and-disputes).
