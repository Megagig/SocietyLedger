Sometimes a payment you made does not show: your transfer was never matched, or a collector forgot to record your cash. When that happens, raise a **query** (the app also calls it a claim). It puts your case in front of an officer, protects you from being marked as behind while it is looked at, and leaves a written record.

## Before you start

- It must be for **one payment that is unpaid and past its due date** on your schedule. You cannot raise a query about a payment that is already paid or waived, and the button does not appear for a day that is not yet due.
- You need to be allowed to make changes in the circle. If your circle has been switched to read-only, you will not see the button.
- Have your **evidence** if you have it: a screenshot of the transfer, or the bank's receipt. A photo or PDF, up to 5 MB. It is optional, but it makes a decision much quicker.
- Your circle must be **running**.

## What happens next

1. **That payment shows as Under review.** It is **never** shown as Missed while your query is open. You are **not charged a late fee or chased** about it, and it is left out of your [trust score](/docs/ajo-thrift/your-trust-score) until it is decided.
2. **The circle's officers are told**, and can see what you said and your evidence. They find it under **Arrears and cases**, on the **Claims of "I paid"** tab, and select **Decide**.

<Screenshot
  src="/images/ajo-thrift/pot-arrears-claims-1.webp"
  alt="The Claims of 'I paid' tab on an officer's Arrears and cases page: the member's name, what they said, Waiting for a decision, and a Decide button."
  caption="What the officer sees: your claim, your words and your evidence, waiting for someone other than you to decide."
/>
3. **A different person decides.** The officer who raised a query for someone cannot decide it, and you cannot decide your own. They check the bank statement or the collector's record, then either **accept** your claim or **do not accept** it, and they must write a reason. **You are told the decision and the reason.**
4. **If it is accepted**, the officer then finds and confirms the payment as normal, and you get a receipt. A query does not add money by itself.
5. **A decision is not changed.** If you think something was missed, you can raise it again with more evidence.

You can see your queries and their decisions on your circle page.

## Good to know

- **One query at a time** can be open for each payment.
- **A query does not stop everything.** It covers that one payment only.
- **Do not raise a query to delay a late fee.** An officer can see what you paid and when, and a claim that does not match the record is simply not accepted.
- **If you have not yet reported the payment at all**, report it first. See [Paying and sending proof](/docs/ajo-thrift/paying-and-sending-proof). A query is for when you have paid and it still does not appear.

<Callout type="tip" title="Evidence helps both sides">
A clear screenshot with the date, amount and the circle's account name lets an officer decide in minutes. A decision with no evidence has to rest on memory.
</Callout>
