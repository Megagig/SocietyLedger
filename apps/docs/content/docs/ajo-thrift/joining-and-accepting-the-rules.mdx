When a circle's officers add you, the circle appears in **My Thrift**. You do not need to do anything to be added, but you do need to **read and accept the circle's rules** before you can start. The rules are the agreement between you and everyone else in the circle: how much you pay, when, how long you have after the due date, and what happens if someone falls behind.

## Before you start

- You need a SocietyLedger login. If an officer added you without one, they may have emailed you a link, or they can record on your behalf that they explained the rules and you agreed.
- Make sure the circle has your correct **phone number**. It is where you will get texts about your payments.

## Accept the rules

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["My Thrift"]} />. Your circle is listed. Select <UiLabel>Open</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Circle rules</UiLabel>. You will see the **version** of the rules and the date it is in force from.</Step>
<Step>Read every rule. They are written in plain words: the late fee and its cap, the grace period, whether a guarantor is needed, what happens if someone stops paying after being paid, whether reminders are sent, and what other members can see about you.</Step>
<Step>Tick <UiLabel>I have read these rules and I agree to them.</UiLabel>. You can add a **Note (optional)**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Accept the rules</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Money", "My thrift"]} /> and open your circle.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Circle rules** and read them.</Step>
<Step>Agree, and tap **Accept the rules**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## If the circle asks you to confirm your phone

Some circles ask everyone to confirm their phone number. You will see a card called **Confirm your phone number**: "We text a code to the number on file."

<Steps>
<Step>Wait for the text. The card says **Code sent to** your number.</Step>
<Step>Type the **Code** and select <UiLabel>Confirm your phone</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>

The code stays valid for a short time. **Never share it with anyone.** An officer or a collector never needs it. If it expires, ask for another.

## What you are waiting for

Until you are **Running**, your circle page says "Before you can start, the circle is waiting for:" and lists what is outstanding. It could be that an officer still has to approve you, that you have to accept the rules, confirm your phone, or that a guarantor has to be recorded.

When everything is done, you become **Running** by yourself, and you can see your schedule and start paying.

## What happens next

- **Your schedule appears.** It shows each payment, its amount and its due date.
- **Your payout account.** Add it now, so you are ready when it is your turn to collect. See [Your payout account and verification](/docs/ajo-thrift/your-payout-account-and-verification).
- **If the rules change.** The circle must give at least 7 days' notice and you must accept the new version. You are only ever bound by the version you accepted.

<Callout type="note" title="You are agreeing to a record, not signing a contract">
Accepting the rules records that you read them and agreed, with the version and the date. It is the circle's evidence that you knew what you were agreeing to. Read them carefully, and ask an officer before you accept if anything is unclear.
</Callout>
