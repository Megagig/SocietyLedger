A collector-thrift circle runs in **cycles**: a fixed run of days (for example, 30) in which every saver saves the same amount each time. At the end, each saver chooses to **withdraw** (their savings are paid to them, less the collector's fee) or **continue** into the next cycle. This page covers how a cycle works and how to run the end of one.

## Before you start

- This page is for **collector thrift**. A rotating pot has one cycle, its rounds.
- You need the **approve payouts** permission to start the next cycle. Officers with **manage** can see the page and record a choice for a saver.

## How a cycle works

- **Each saver has their own amount.** It must be at least the circle's minimum, and the saver (or an officer for them) sets it **before the cycle begins**. It then **cannot change within the cycle**, which is what makes a saver's schedule and their final payout certain.
- **A late joiner's cycle ends with everyone else's.** If someone joins on day 10, they save for the days that remain, and their cycle ends on the same day.
- **The collector's fee** is set when the circle is created (for example, one day's contribution). It comes out of each saver's savings when the cycle ends.
- **Cash is counted day by day.** See [Handovers and counting cash](/docs/ajo-thrift/handovers-and-counting-cash).

## The end of a cycle

Savers are asked **three days before the cycle ends** whether to withdraw or continue. In the demo above, the cycle ends on 17 November, so savers are asked from 14 November.

- **Withdraw.** The saver's savings, less the collector's fee, are paid to their payout account. They need a verified payout account before then.
- **Continue.** The saver stays in the circle for the next cycle, and chooses what to save each time. Any money that has not been paid out is **brought forward**.
- **No answer means withdraw.** If a saver does not answer, their savings are paid out to them. This is the safe default: nobody's money is held longer than they asked.
- **An officer can record a choice** for someone who has no login, and the page shows "(recorded by an officer)".

Savers who withdraw are paid like any payout: an officer [prepares it](/docs/ajo-thrift/payouts), an admin approves it, and it is recorded as paid.

## Start the next cycle

You can start the next cycle only when:

- **the current cycle is over**, and
- **every saver who is withdrawing has been paid** (their payout is recorded as paid).

<Steps>
<Step>On the **Savings cycle** tab, select <UiLabel>Start the next cycle</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Confirm. A new cycle begins, with a fresh schedule for every saver who continued, at the amount they chose.</Step>
</Steps>

If a withdrawing saver has not been paid yet, SocietyLedger says who, and will not start the next cycle until they have been.

## What happens next

Savers see their new cycle on their own page, with their calendar of payments. Past cycles stay under **My record**, with their totals. Nothing from an earlier cycle is erased.

<Callout type="note" title="A saver's amount is a promise to themselves">
Fixing the amount for the whole cycle protects savers from slipping: a collector cannot talk a saver into a smaller amount halfway through, and a saver cannot lose track of what they agreed. If a saver needs to change their amount, it changes at the start of the next cycle.
</Callout>
