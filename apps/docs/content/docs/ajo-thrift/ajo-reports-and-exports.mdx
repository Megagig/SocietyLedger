Ajo has eight reports. Each one is worked out from the circle's ledger and schedule, the same way on the web, on the phone and in every file, so a figure never differs between them. This page covers what each report counts and how to read and download it.

## Before you start

- You need the **Ajo reports** permission. Officers have it. A collector who has it sees only their own people and their own cash.
- Which reports you can open depends on your Ajo tier. Basic reports come with every tier. A report your tier does not include is still listed, with the reason, so you know what an upgrade would add. See [Plans, billing and limits](/docs/ajo-thrift/plans-billing-and-limits).

## The eight reports

| Report | What it shows | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| **Contributions** | What was expected, paid, outstanding, overdue or missed, one row for each instalment. | Basic |
| **Member ledger** | Every payment, fee, payout, reversal and adjustment for each person, with a running balance. | Basic |
| **Payouts** | Scheduled, waiting, paid and reversed payouts, with their beneficiaries. | Basic |
| **Group financial summary** | Money in, money out, what is owed and the cash counted, by circle and month. | Basic |
| **Collector performance** | Cash each collector recorded, handed in and had counted, against their targets. | Advanced |
| **Defaults and arrears** | Who is behind, their unpaid late fees, default cases, repayment plans and disputes. | Advanced |
| **Fees and revenue** | Late fees assessed, paid and waived, the collector fee, and the platform levy. | Advanced |
| **Audit and activity** | Approvals, reversals, waivers and rule and order changes, with who, when and why. | Audit |

## Open and read a report

<Step>Choose the **Circle** (or leave it on all circles), and the **From** and **To** dates. If you choose no dates, the report shows the running month. You can ask for up to 12 months.</Step>
<Step>For some reports you can also choose a **Person**, once you have chosen a circle, or a **Collector**.</Step>

Under every report's title is **How this is counted**. It says what the figures include, which date each is counted on, and what is left out. Read it the first time you open a report.

## What the figures mean

- **Only confirmed money is received.** A payment that is only reported is shown separately as **Reported, awaiting confirmation**. It is never added to what was received.
- **Money is counted on the day it was recorded**, in the circle's own timezone. A payment recorded at 23:30 Lagos time on 31 March is a March payment, even if it is April somewhere else.
- **Expected, outstanding and overdue are counted on the due date.**
- **Circles are never blended.** With more than one circle, you get a block of figures for each. Circles of the same kind (two rotating pots, say) also get a total. A rotating pot and a collector-thrift group are never added together, because their money works differently.
- **A dash is not a zero.** A figure that does not apply shows "—".
- **The totals match the ledger.** Contributions, payouts and the balance in a report equal the group ledger's totals for the same dates, to the kobo.

## Download a report

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
Choose **CSV** or **PDF** at the top right of the report. The file uses the same filters you chose. CSV opens in Excel and in Google Sheets: money is a plain number under a "(₦)" heading, so a column adds up, and phone numbers keep their leading zero. The PDF writes money as "NGN 5,000.00".
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
Tap **Share PDF** or **Share CSV**. The phone's share sheet opens, so you can send the file on WhatsApp or email, or save it to Files.
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

A report can have up to **100,000 rows** to view or as a CSV, and up to **5,000 rows** as a PDF. If it has more, SocietyLedger says so and tells you what to do: choose fewer dates or one circle, or download the CSV instead of the PDF.

## What happens next

Each file you download is recorded in the audit log: who made it, which report, which dates and which filters. The [Audit and activity report](/docs/ajo-thrift/officer-accountability) lists these, so the circle can see who took a copy of what.

## What is left out, on purpose

Reports show names, and phone numbers for people who are behind, so you can chase them. They never show a bank account number, an email address, a home address, a proof file, a guarantor's details, or a private note.

<Callout type="note" title="If your tier changes">
If your Ajo add-on expires, the circle becomes read-only and you can still open and download the reports your tier included. Reports from a higher tier stay locked until you renew with a tier that includes them.
</Callout>
