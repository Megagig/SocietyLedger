When you sign up a thrift group and sign in for the first time, SocietyLedger walks you through five short steps. Each step saves on its own, so you can stop and come back. Nothing here moves money: the only bank details you give are your group's own account, and SocietyLedger never holds your money.

## Before you start

- Your group must have been approved by SocietyLedger and your email verified.
- You start with a **14-day free trial** of Ajo, with collector thrift and reminders included and room for **30 contributors**. After the trial you pay only for the Ajo tier that fits your number of contributors.
- Have your group's bank account details to hand.

## The five steps

<Steps>
<Step>**Group profile.** Check your group's name and contact phone. You can add an email, the area and state where you meet, and a logo. Changing the name does not change your group's web address.</Step>
<Step>**Your first circle.** Choose **Collector thrift** (a collector gathers a fixed amount each time and everyone gets their savings back at the end) or **Rotating pot** (everyone pays the same each round and one person takes the whole pot, in a drawn order). Then set the amount, how often people pay, the start date, the collector's fee, your group's bank account and the rules. The circle is saved as a **draft**: nobody can see it yet.</Step>
<Step>**Contributors.** Add the people who will pay in. Type them one by one, or upload a CSV list (up to 200 people; download the template to see the columns). You can approve them for the circle right away and record that you explained the rules and they agreed.</Step>
<Step>**Team and collectors (optional).** Invite a co-admin, Ajo officers and collectors by email. One person prepares a payout and another approves it, so a second admin helps.</Step>
<Step>**Review and open.** Check each part, then **Open** the circle so your contributors can see it, or keep it as a draft and open it later.</Step>
</Steps>

## How often people pay

Choose **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, **Annually**, or **Custom**. With Custom you set your own step, such as every 2 weeks or every 3 days. For a twice-a-month circle, choose 15 days or 2 weeks. The schedule cannot be changed once the circle has started.

## Adding people from a CSV

The first row names the columns: `first_name`, `last_name`, `phone`, `address`, `email` and `shares`. Only the name and phone are required. Each row is checked on its own. A row with a problem is listed with the reason, and the good rows are still added. Sending the same list again adds nobody twice.

<Callout type="note" title="Why people may show as “0 active”">
A person becomes **active** once the circle's approval, their acceptance of the rules, any guarantor and any phone confirmation are done, **and the circle is open**. People you add to a draft stay approved and waiting, and become active the moment you open the circle. Before anyone can be **paid out**, SocietyLedger also checks their name, phone, address and payout account.
</Callout>

## Skipping and coming back

**Skip for now** takes you straight to your circles. A banner there reminds you to finish, and **Setup guide** lets you go through the steps again. Nothing you set up is lost or deleted.

## What happens next

Open the circle, then ask each person to accept the rules and add their payout account. When you are ready, start the circle on its start date and record contributions as they come in. Every contributor gets a receipt.
