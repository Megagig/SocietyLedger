Everyone in a thrift group holds a role, and a role decides what they can see and do. A standalone group has four:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Administrator** | Runs the group: circles, people, payments, payouts and settings. Approves payouts, and is the only one who can set a circle's payout order by hand. |
| **Officer** | Runs circles day to day: adds and approves people, confirms payments, records payments for members, manages rules, reports. Does not approve payouts. |
| **Collector** | Collects contributions from the people assigned to them and records cash. A collector does not confirm payments: the officer's handover count does. |
| **Member** | Pays into a circle and sees their own records. Everyone is a member as well as anything else. |

A person can hold more than one role. The roles that exist only for an association (Treasurer, Financial Secretary, Election Committee) are not offered in a thrift group.

## Give someone a role

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "User Management"]} />, find the person, and give them the role. See also [Assigning roles](/docs/settings/assigning-roles).
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Settings"]} />, open the person under user roles, and choose the role under **Give a role**.
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

The person must already have a login and be approved. To invite someone who does not have one yet, invite them from the people list first.

## Good to know

- **You cannot change your own roles.** Ask another Administrator. A group always keeps at least one Administrator.
- **You can only give a role whose permissions you hold yourself.** The roles a thrift group offers are built so that its Administrator holds all of them, so you will always see Officer and Collector in the list.
- **Taking a role away** is recorded, with who did it and when, in the audit log.
- **Keep the jobs apart.** The person who collects cash should not be the only person who confirms it. That is why a collector cannot confirm a transfer and an officer cannot approve a payout. See [Officer accountability](/docs/ajo-thrift/officer-accountability).
