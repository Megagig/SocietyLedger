When someone falls behind, SocietyLedger gives you three tools and one firm rule. The tools are **late fees**, **default cases with repayment plans**, and a way to **decide a claim** that someone did pay. The rule is that **nothing is ever taken from other members or from a guarantor**, and nobody is removed automatically.

All three live on the circle's **Arrears and cases** tab.

## Before you start

- You need the **manage** permission. Closing a case asks for your password.
- Late fees only exist if the circle's rules have one. See [Rules, grace and late fees](/docs/ajo-thrift/rules-grace-and-late-fees).

## Late fees

<Screenshot
  src="/images/ajo-thrift/pot-arrears-fees-1.webp"
  alt="The Late fees tab showing two ₦500.00 fees for one member, each with how it was worked out, a status, and buttons I told them and Waive."
  caption="Late fees, each with how it was worked out in words."
/>

A late fee is worked out **once for each missed payment**, the day after the grace period ends, and only if the circle's rules have a fee. In a pot where each person pays **₦5,000.00** a week with a fixed fee of **₦500.00** and 3 grace days, someone who has still not paid three days after the due date gets one ₦500.00 fee for that round. Two missed rounds is two fees.

- **The rules the member accepted decide.** The fee comes from the version of the rules the member accepted that was in force on the due date. If they never accepted a version, there is no fee.
- **They are told first.** The fee becomes payable only **48 hours after the member has been told**, and only if no query is open about that payment.
- **Someone who cannot be reached.** A member with no login and no way of being messaged stays "waiting" until an officer records that they were told. Select <UiLabel>I told them</UiLabel>, write how you told them (for example "told in person at the market on Saturday"), and record it. The 48 hours then begin.
- **The fee belongs to the circle.** It is never part of a payout and never SocietyLedger's.
- **How it is paid.** When the member pays, the money clears their instalments first, then any payable fees, oldest first. The fee is shown as a separate part of the payment.

### Waive a fee

Select <UiLabel>Waive</UiLabel>, write a reason, and select <UiLabel>Waive the fee</UiLabel>. The member is told. A waiver is undone only by a recorded step: select <UiLabel>Undo waiver</UiLabel> and write why. Every waiver and undo is on the [accountability page](/docs/ajo-thrift/officer-accountability).

## Claims that someone paid

A member who believes their payment is not shown can raise a **claim**, and the officer decides it. See [Raising a query about a payment](/docs/ajo-thrift/raising-a-query-about-a-payment) for the member's side.

<Screenshot
  src="/images/ajo-thrift/pot-arrears-claims-1.webp"
  alt="The Claims of I paid tab showing one open claim with what the member said, a link to see their evidence, and a button to decide it."
  caption="An open claim, with what the member said and their evidence."
/>

<Steps>
<Step>Open the **Claims of "I paid"** tab. Each claim shows **The member says:** and, if they attached a slip, <UiLabel>See evidence</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Check it against the bank statement or the collector's record.</Step>
<Step>Choose the **Decision**: <UiLabel>Accept the claim</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Do not accept the claim</UiLabel>. Write the reason. The member is told it.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Record the decision</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>

Two rules apply. **The person who raised a claim cannot decide it**, so an officer who raised one for someone with no login needs another officer to decide. And **a decision is never changed**: if something was missed, the member can raise it again.

A claim does not add any money. Accepting it records that you agree the member paid. You then [confirm the payment](/docs/ajo-thrift/confirming-payments) or record it, as you would any other. While a claim is open, the round shows as **Under review**, and no late fee, reminder or default case is raised for that member over it.

## Default cases and repayment plans

A **default case** is for one specific situation: a member of a **rotating pot** who has **already been paid** and then falls behind past the grace period. Everyone else in the pot is still paying for them, so the circle needs a record and a way forward. It does not apply to collector thrift, where each saver's money is their own.

What happens automatically depends on the rule the member accepted for this:

- **Tell the admins.** The admins are told, and nothing else happens.
- **Offer the member a repayment plan.** A plan is drawn up.
- **Open a case and offer a plan.** A case is opened and a plan is drawn up. This is the default.

### The case file

Each case keeps **the file**: what was owed when it opened (frozen, never edited), what is owed now, and a list of every step, with who did it and when. You can **Add a note to the file**, and the member can add their own response.

### A repayment plan

A plan is a **promise about timing**. It splits what is owed into instalments on dates.

<Steps>
<Step>In the case, a plan is **Proposed** until the member agrees. Select <UiLabel>Draft a new plan</UiLabel> to change how it is split.</Step>
<Step>When the member agrees, select <UiLabel>Record agreement</UiLabel> and write how they agreed (for example "agreed in person at the meeting on 28 October"). Or the member accepts it themselves in the app.</Step>
<Step>The plan becomes **active**. While it is kept, **no new late fee** is charged on the instalments it covers.</Step>
</Steps>

If the member misses a plan date, the miss is recorded once and the admins are told. A plan can be cancelled (<UiLabel>Cancel the plan</UiLabel>, with a reason).

### Close a case

When the matter is over, select <UiLabel>Close this case</UiLabel>, choose the **Outcome**: **Resolved** or **Closed, not resolved**, write why, and enter your password.

## What happens next

A case never removes anyone and never takes money from anyone else. If someone is **Closed, not resolved**, the record stays and the circle decides what to do. It can see the member's [trust score](/docs/ajo-thrift/your-trust-score) and the file, but neither blocks them by itself.

<Callout type="note" title="The words">
SocietyLedger says "Behind by", Overdue and Missed. It never says "defaulter". A default case describes a situation, not a person.
</Callout>
