A payout is the moment a circle pays a person. **SocietyLedger never sends the money.** It works out how much is owed, makes sure more than one person agrees, and keeps the record. Your circle pays from its own bank account, then records the bank's reference here.

There are three steps, and they are done by different people:

1. **Prepare.** An officer works out the payout and checks that it can go ahead.
2. **Approve.** An admin approves it. The person who prepared it cannot approve it, and nobody can approve a payout to themselves.
3. **Record as paid.** After your circle has paid from its account, an officer records the bank's reference.

Only step 3 writes anything to the circle's ledger. Preparing and approving change nothing about the money.

## Before you start

- You need the **manage** permission to prepare, **approve payouts** to approve, and **reconcile** to record. An **admin** usually has all three.
- Payouts are made **in the drawn order**. A round cannot be prepared until the one before it has been recorded as paid.
- The person being paid must have a payout account entered and **verified**, and must not have changed it in the last **72 hours**. See [Your payout account and verification](/docs/ajo-thrift/your-payout-account-and-verification).

## Prepare a payout

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Thrift Circles"]} />, open the circle, and choose the <UiLabel>Payouts</UiLabel> tab.</Step>
<Step>Under **Prepare a payout**, choose the **Round**.</Step>
<Step>Read the preview: **To pay out**, the **Collector fee** (if any), **Confirmed so far**, who is **Short by** how much, and the account it will go to.</Step>
<Step>If it can go ahead, select <UiLabel>Prepare this payout</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Thrift circles"]} />, open the circle, and tap **Payouts**.</Step>
<Step>Choose the round and read the preview.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Prepare this payout**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

If the preview says it cannot go ahead, it says why: the previous round is unpaid, the person has no verified account, they changed their account recently, or an open payout already exists for this round.

**If someone has not paid.** If one person is short, the preview names them and shows how much. You can still pay out what has been **confirmed**. For example, in a pot of ten people paying **₦5,000.00**, if one person has not paid, **₦45,000.00** can be paid out now with a note that **₦5,000.00** is short. If that money arrives later, it is paid as a **second payout** to the same person. Nothing is taken from anyone else.

## Approve a payout

<Steps>
<Step>On the **Payouts** tab, find the payout marked **Waiting for approval**, and select <UiLabel>Approve</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Check the amount, the person and the account. The window reminds you that approving does not send any money.</Step>
<Step>Type **Your password** again, to be sure it is you, and approve.</Step>
</Steps>

If your circle has only one admin, you can approve a payout you prepared yourself, but you must tick **I am the only admin, and I checked this myself** and write a note. It is flagged, and every officer is told.

## Pay it, then record it

Pay the person from your circle's bank account, outside SocietyLedger. Then:

<Steps>
<Step>On the **Payouts** tab, find the payout marked **Approved, waiting to be paid** and select <UiLabel>Record as paid</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Bank reference** from the transfer. You can attach the **Slip** (optional) and add a **Note**.</Step>
<Step>Record it. The payout becomes **Paid**.</Step>
</Steps>

The person who is being paid cannot record their own payout. A bank reference that has already been used on another payout is refused, and a reference typed again is treated as the same payout, so it can never be counted twice.

## If something goes wrong

- **The transfer failed.** Select <UiLabel>Transfer failed</UiLabel>. Nothing is written to the records, the round goes back to waiting, and the admins are told. Prepare it again once it is fixed.
- **You do not want to go ahead.** Select **Cancel**. The officer who prepared a payout can cancel it. An admin can cancel any payout that has not been recorded as paid.
- **A paid payout was recorded by mistake, or the money came back.** An admin can **Reverse** it: choose the payout, write the reason, enter your password, and select <UiLabel>Reverse it</UiLabel>. The ledger gets an entry that cancels it, with the reason and your name, and the payout shows as **Reversed**. This is the only way to undo a recorded payout.

If the payout account was changed after a payout was prepared, SocietyLedger cancels the prepared payout, because the money would go to a different account from the one that was checked.

## What happens next

After a payout is recorded, the person is told, and it appears in the circle's activity. When every round has been paid and nothing is open, select <UiLabel>Finish the circle</UiLabel> to close it. A finished circle stays readable and can still be reported on.

<Callout type="warning" title="Approving does not pay">
Approving only records that two people agree the payout is right. No money moves until your circle pays the person from its own account. Do not tell the person it has been paid until you have recorded it.
</Callout>
