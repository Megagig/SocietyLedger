## I paid, but it still shows as unpaid. What do I do?

Only a **Confirmed** payment counts as paid. If you reported a transfer, it is waiting for an officer to find it on the bank statement. If you gave cash, it is waiting for your collector to record it and hand it in. Open the payment to see which step it is at. If it has been more than a day or two and you are sure you paid, use **I already paid this**. See [Raising a query about a payment](/docs/ajo-thrift/raising-a-query-about-a-payment).

## What do the payment statuses mean?

There are nine, and the main ones are **Pending**, **Reported**, **Confirmed**, **Under review** and **Missed**. Only Confirmed counts toward what you have paid. See [Payment statuses](/docs/ajo-thrift/payment-statuses).

## Why was I charged a late fee?

A late fee applies only when the rule version you **accepted** includes one, and only after the grace period has passed. If you never accepted a version, no fee is charged. A fee is payable **48 hours** after you are told about it. See [Rules, grace and late fees](/docs/ajo-thrift/rules-grace-and-late-fees).

## Can the rules change after I joined?

Yes, but never suddenly. A new version of the rules gives members **7 days' notice**, and you accept it before it governs you. Your earlier acceptance stays on record. See [Joining a circle and accepting the rules](/docs/ajo-thrift/joining-and-accepting-the-rules).

## Why can't a payout be paid yet?

A payout goes through three steps: an officer **prepares** it, an admin **approves** it (with their password), and it is **recorded as paid** once the money has gone. The admin who approves cannot be the officer who prepared it, nor the person being paid. In a rotating pot, the previous round must be recorded as paid first. See [Payouts](/docs/ajo-thrift/payouts).

## Why is my payout account not accepted?

A new or changed payout account has a **72-hour cool-off**, to stop someone redirecting your money. It is then checked: the name must match your own, your phone must be confirmed, and your address must be on file. If it is **Turned down**, the page says why. See [Your payout account and verification](/docs/ajo-thrift/your-payout-account-and-verification).

## How is the order of payouts decided?

By **draw** or by the admin's own order, set before the circle starts. Each draw is recorded and cannot be redone. A change after the start is an **emergency change**, and it is recorded with a reason. See [The payout order and the draw](/docs/ajo-thrift/the-payout-order-and-draw).

## What is the difference between a rotating pot and collector thrift?

In a **rotating pot**, everyone pays in each round and one member takes the pot each time. In **collector thrift**, a collector visits savers daily, and each saver gets their own savings back at the end of a cycle. See [Choosing a circle type](/docs/ajo-thrift/choosing-a-circle-type).

## My collector says they handed in the cash, but I cannot see it.

Open **Where my cash is** on your savings page. Your payment moves through three steps: recorded by your collector, handed in by your collector, and confirmed by the office. If a step is overdue, the page says so. See [Cash reconciliation](/docs/ajo-thrift/cash-reconciliation).

## The handover count does not match. What now?

The cash counted must equal the payments ticked. If a payment is not there, **untick** it and the count then matches. That payment stays unconfirmed, and the missing cash is carried into the next handover so nothing disappears. See [Handovers and counting cash](/docs/ajo-thrift/handovers-and-counting-cash).

## Can I leave a circle?

Before it starts, yes. In collector thrift, yes, and what you have saved is still yours. In a rotating pot that has started, you cannot simply leave, because other members are relying on your payments. Speak to an admin. See [Leaving a circle](/docs/ajo-thrift/leaving-a-circle).

## Who can see my details?

Your own details are private to you and the circle's officers. Other members see only what the **Circle board** shows, and names follow the circle's name rule. A statement or board **never** includes phone numbers, emails, addresses or bank accounts. See [What members can see in a circle](/docs/ajo-thrift/what-members-can-see-in-a-circle).

## Why can't I start a new circle or add people?

Usually one of three things: your plan's limit for circles or people has been reached, your **levy** has been unpaid for more than 60 days, or your Ajo add-on has expired, which makes everything read-only. See [Plans, billing and limits](/docs/ajo-thrift/plans-billing-and-limits).

## Can an officer take or change money on their own?

No. Every confirmation, payout and reversal is recorded with who did it and when, and some steps need a second person. See [Officer accountability](/docs/ajo-thrift/officer-accountability).

## How do I know a message or call is really from my circle?

Be careful. SocietyLedger never asks for your password or a phone code, and an officer will never ask you to pay into a personal account that is not on the circle page. See [Staying safe](/docs/ajo-thrift/staying-safe).
