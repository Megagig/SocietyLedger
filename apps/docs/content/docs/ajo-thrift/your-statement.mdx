A statement is a record you can keep, print or send. SocietyLedger makes two kinds, depending on how your circle works: your own **savings statement** if you save through a collector, and a **circle statement** if you are in a rotating pot.

## Before you start

You must be in the circle. A statement shows only what you are allowed to see: your own payments if you save through a collector, or what the Circle board shows if you are in a rotating pot.

## Your savings statement (collector thrift)

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["My Thrift"]} />, open your circle, and select <UiLabel>My savings</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Open the <UiLabel>Statements</UiLabel> tab.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Download PDF</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Download CSV</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Money", "Thrift"]} />, open your circle, and tap **My savings**, then **Statements**.</Step>
<Step>Tap the PDF or CSV. When it is ready, your phone's share sheet opens, so you can send it on WhatsApp or save it to Files.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

It covers **the cycle you are in**: what fell due, every payment, your savings and what you will receive. It says that it is from SocietyLedger's records and that **your group's bank statement is the final word**. Earlier cycles appear under **My record** with their totals.

A **receipt** for one confirmed payment is separate. Open the **Payments** tab and select **Receipt** next to a confirmed payment.

## The circle statement (rotating pot)

<Steps>
<Step>Open your circle and choose <UiLabel>Circle board</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Download circle statement</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>

It is a PDF built from the same board you see on screen, so it matches the circle on the day it is made, and shows **nothing the board does not show**. Names follow the circle's name rule, and there is never a phone number, email, address, bank account, guarantor detail, note or proof.

## How anyone can check a statement is genuine

Every circle statement carries a short **code** (for example `K7M2P-9XQ4R`). Anyone who holds the statement, including a person who does not use SocietyLedger, can check it.

<Steps>
<Step>Open the verification page that is printed on the statement.</Step>
<Step>Type the code. Letters can be any case, and the dash is optional.</Step>
<Step>The page says **when the statement was made** and **whether the circle's figures still match it**.</Step>
</Steps>

The page shows circle totals only, never a name. It is how a circle can send a statement to a bank, a landlord or the group chat, and the person who gets it can tell it was not edited.

## A note on the naira sign in a PDF

PDFs write money as **NGN 5,000.00**, because the fonts built into PDFs have no naira sign. A CSV and every screen use **₦5,000.00**. They are the same figures.

## What happens next

A statement is a snapshot. If a payment is confirmed after you make it, make a new one. A statement made today does not change what your circle's figures are.

<Callout type="note" title="Share a statement, not your login">
Send the PDF or its code, never your password or a phone confirmation code. Nobody who needs to check a statement needs to sign in.
</Callout>
