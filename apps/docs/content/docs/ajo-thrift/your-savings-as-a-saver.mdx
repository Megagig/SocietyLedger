If you save with a collector, **My savings** is your page. It is private: only you and your circle's officers can see it. It answers three questions: how much have I given, where is my cash, and what will I get back.

## Before you start

Your circle must have added you, and you must have accepted its rules. See [Joining a circle and accepting the rules](/docs/ajo-thrift/joining-and-accepting-the-rules).

## The tabs

**My savings** shows **Saved so far** (confirmed money only), your progress (for example "30 payments in this cycle · 10 made · 0 missed · 19 to go"), and **what you will receive**: your savings less the collector's fee. For example, **₦10,000.00 saved − ₦1,000.00 collector fee = ₦9,000.00**. Under it is a calendar of every day, with its status. Money you have given that is not counted yet is shown as **Reported, awaiting confirmation**, and the page says "You're behind by ₦2,000.00" until the office counts it.

**Payments** lists every payment: the date, the amount, how you paid (for example **Cash to collector**), who recorded it, its status, and its receipt number. Confirmed payments have a **Receipt** you can open.

**Where my cash is** follows each cash payment through three steps: **your collector recorded it**, **your collector handed it in**, and **the office confirmed it**, with who and when. If a step takes too long (a day for your collector, two for the office), it says so, and you can ask about it. This is how you can always answer "did my money get there?".

**Collector and fees** shows your collector's name and phone, since when, your daily target and when they last took cash, and the **fees and charges**: the collector's fee and how it is worked out.

**My record** shows how many of your payments were on time ("10 of your last 11 payments were on time (91%)"), your streak, and your past cycles with their totals.

**Statements** lets you download your statement. See [Your statement](/docs/ajo-thrift/your-statement).

## When the cycle ends

Three days before your cycle ends, you are asked: **What would you like to do with your savings?**

- **Withdraw them:** they are paid to your payout account.
- **Continue into the next cycle:** they stay in the group, and you choose **What will you save each time next cycle?**

Select <UiLabel>Save my choice</UiLabel>. **If you do not answer, your savings are paid out to you.** Once your payout has started, your choice can no longer change. If you will withdraw, make sure your [payout account](/docs/ajo-thrift/your-payout-account-and-verification) is added and verified before the cycle ends. The page reminds you if it is not.

## If something looks wrong

If a payment you made is not shown, or a day shows as behind when you paid, use **I already paid this** next to that day on your circle page, or **Ask about this payment** on the Payments tab. See [Raising a query about a payment](/docs/ajo-thrift/raising-a-query-about-a-payment).

<Callout type="tip" title="Check the same day">
Open **Where my cash is** after your collector visits. If your payment shows as recorded, you have proof that you paid. If it does not, tell an officer straight away, while you both remember.
</Callout>
