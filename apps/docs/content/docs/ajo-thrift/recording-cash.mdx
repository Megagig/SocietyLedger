Recording cash is the collector's main job in the app. You do it once for each payment, as you take the money. It takes a few seconds, and it is what gives the saver proof that they paid.

## Before you start

- You must be **assigned** to the saver. You can only record cash from your own people.
- You need **internet**. If your phone is offline, the button is replaced with a note that this needs internet, and nothing is queued.
- The circle must be **running**, and its Ajo add-on must be active. While the add-on is expired, nothing can be recorded.

## Record a payment

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Open <MenuPath items={["Collect"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Find the saver under **Your contributors** and select <UiLabel>Record cash</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Amount received (₦)**. Write it in full, for example 1,000 or 2,000.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Record cash</UiLabel> again to save. The saver's row now shows <UiLabel>Paid today</UiLabel>, and the figures at the top update.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Thrift circles", "Today's collection"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Tap the saver's name under **Your contributors**.</Step>
<Step>Enter the amount you received and save.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## What happens next

- The payment is recorded as **Cash received, awaiting confirmation**. It is **not** counted towards the saver's savings yet.
- The saver sees it in their own savings the same day, and is told.
- A saver's payment is applied to their oldest unpaid day first.
- If you press the button twice by accident, or your connection drops and tries again, the payment is recorded **once**. Each attempt carries its own key, so a repeat can never count twice.

It is confirmed only when an officer counts your handover at the end of the day. See [Cash reconciliation](/docs/ajo-thrift/cash-reconciliation).

## If you record the wrong amount

You cannot edit or delete a payment. Tell an officer straight away. They can **reverse** it with a reason, and you record it again correctly. The reversal and the reason stay in the history, so nobody can say it was hidden.

If you recorded **too little** or **too much**, say which when you tell them, so they can explain it in the note.

## If you cannot record

| What you see | Why, and what to do |
|---|---|
| "This needs internet" | Your phone is offline. Connect, then record. Do not write it down and enter it later from memory if you can avoid it. |
| You cannot record for this saver | They are not assigned to you. Ask an officer to assign them. |
| You cannot record, the day has been handed over | You already handed over for this day. Ask an officer. |
| Handovers or recording are switched off | The circle's Ajo add-on has expired. The circle is read-only until it is renewed. |

<Callout type="warning" title="Never record cash you have not been given">
A payment recorded without cash behind it will be unticked when the office counts your handover, and flagged. Savers are told when you record, so they will see it.
</Callout>
