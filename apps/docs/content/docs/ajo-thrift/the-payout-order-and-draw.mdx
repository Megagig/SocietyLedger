In a rotating pot, the order in which people are paid is the most sensitive decision in the circle. SocietyLedger makes it by a **recorded random draw**, so nobody, including the admin, can arrange it, and everyone can see afterwards that it was fair. This page covers the draw, swapping places, and what to do in an emergency.

## Before you start

- This page is for **rotating pots**. A collector-thrift circle has no order, because everyone is paid at the end of the cycle.
- You need the **manage** permission to draw and to request swaps. An **admin** (the **approve payouts** permission) approves swaps and makes emergency changes.
- Everyone in the circle must be added and approved first. See [Adding and approving people](/docs/ajo-thrift/adding-and-approving-people).

## Make the draw

The draw is made while the circle is **open for joining**, before it starts.

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Thrift Circles"]} />, open the circle, and choose the <UiLabel>Payout order</UiLabel> tab.</Step>
<Step>Check that the list of people is final. If you add or remove someone, or change anyone's shares, after the draw, you will need to draw again.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Draw the order</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>The order appears at once, with the time of the draw and its **seed**. The seed is a long code that records exactly how the order was made.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Thrift circles"]} />, open the circle, and tap **Payout order**.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Draw the order**. The order appears with the time of the draw and its seed.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

A few things to know:

- **You cannot re-roll.** If you ask for the draw again, SocietyLedger refuses unless the list of people or their shares has changed since the last draw. This stops anyone redrawing until they like the result.
- **You cannot choose a seed.** The server makes it. The same seed always gives the same order, so the draw can be checked.
- **Shares.** A person with two shares has two places in the order, and takes two rounds. They pay the contribution for each share every round.
- **Optional rule.** The circle can keep people whose [trust score](/docs/ajo-thrift/your-trust-score) is **At risk** out of the first payout places. It is applied when the draw is made.

## Swap two places

Before any payout has been prepared, two people can swap places by agreement.

<Steps>
<Step>On the **Payout order** tab, under <UiLabel>Swap two places</UiLabel>, choose the **First place** and the **Second place**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Request the swap</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>An admin sees it under **Swap requests** and selects <UiLabel>Approve</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Turn down</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>

Swaps are allowed **until the first payout is prepared**. Once a round's payout has been prepared, approved or paid, that place is held still and cannot be swapped or moved.

## An emergency change

Sometimes the person due next cannot be paid: they are ill, or away, or in need of a later turn. After the first payout has been recorded as paid, an admin can move one person who has **not yet been paid** to a different place, with a written reason.

<Steps>
<Step>On the **Payout order** tab, choose <UiLabel>Emergency change</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Choose the person, the **place to move them to**, and write the **reason**. The reason is shown to the whole circle.</Step>
<Step>Save it. Everyone else between the old and new place moves up or down by one, and the people whose places changed are told.</Step>
</Steps>

Only places that have not been paid, and have no payout in progress, can move. Before the first payout is recorded, use an ordinary swap or draw again instead.

## What happens next

Every draw, swap and emergency change is kept under **History of changes** at the bottom of the page, with who made it and, for emergencies, the reason. They also appear on the [accountability page](/docs/ajo-thrift/officer-accountability), and members of the circle see the order itself on their Circle board.

<Callout type="note" title="Why a swap needs an admin">
A swap changes when someone gets money. Asking an admin to approve it means two people agree to every change, and the reason is on record if anyone asks later.
</Callout>
