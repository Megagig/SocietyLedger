An expense is money the association has paid out: hall hire, stationery, a welfare cost. You record it **after** it is paid, with the receipt. A different officer checks the receipt and approves it, and only then does it count in the books. You never write debits and credits: the books are updated for you.

## Before you start

- To record, change and send expenses you need the **manage finances** permission (Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries by default).
- To approve, reject or void you need **approve payments**, which only Administrators and Treasurers have by default.
- The **category** is one of your expense accounts in the [chart of accounts](/docs/accounting-guide/chart-of-accounts), so add an account there if the one you need is missing.
- The **paid from** account is one of your cash or bank accounts, including petty cash.

## The receipt rule

Spending **above the receipt limit** cannot be sent without its receipt. At or below the limit you may send it with a reason instead, such as "bus fare, no receipt". The limit starts at ₦5,000.00. An officer with the **edit settings** permission can change it under <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Financial"]} /> (**A receipt is needed above**). Set it to 0 to always ask for a receipt.

## Approve, reject or void

- **Approve.** Open the expense, check the receipt against the amount, then approve with your password. It is posted to the books straight away: the category is debited and the account it was paid from is credited. **You cannot approve an expense you recorded.**
- **Nobody to approve?** An officer who cannot approve payments sees the expense and a note saying so. If no one but the officer who recorded it can approve, the expense says that too: give the Treasurer role to someone, or let another officer approve payments, under Settings → Roles.
- **Reject.** Give a reason. The officer who recorded it sees the reason, changes the expense and sends it again.
- **Take it back.** The officer who recorded an expense that is still waiting can take it back to change it.
- **Void.** If an approved expense was wrong (paid twice, wrong amount), an approver can void it with a reason and their password. The books get a reversing entry dated today, and the expense stays on the list marked **Voided**. Nothing is deleted. A date in a closed accounting period cannot be voided until the period is reopened.

## Budget

If the category has a line in the budget in force, the form and the approver both see where it stands. A voucher that takes the line over budget is **not refused**: the approver decides, and the approval records that it went over.

## Where it shows

Every approved expense appears in the books as a journal entry. Open an expense category on the chart of accounts or the income statement, and select a line: its entry has a **Where this came from** card that opens the expense and its receipt. See [Opening a figure to see the entries behind it](/docs/accounting-guide/opening-a-figure).

An expense waiting for approval shows on the Accounting dashboard and on Home for officers who can approve it.
