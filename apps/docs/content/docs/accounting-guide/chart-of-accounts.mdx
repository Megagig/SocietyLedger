Every journal entry, statement and budget in Accounting posts against an account from this list — set it up before you do anything else in the module.

## Before you start

You need the **manage finances** permission, which Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries have by default. Do this once, before creating any journal entry.

## Set up your accounts

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Accounting", "Chart of Accounts"]} />.</Step>
<Step>If the list is empty, select <UiLabel>Initialize Standard Accounts</UiLabel> — this creates 50+ accounts across Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Income and Expenses, including Member Dues Receivable (1121) and Membership Dues (4110), the pair most journal entries use.</Step>
<Step>To add your own account, select <UiLabel>Add Account</UiLabel> and set a unique **Code**, **Name**, **Type** (Asset, Liability, Equity, Income or Expense) and **Normal Balance** (Debit or Credit).</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/accounting-guide/chart-of-accounts-1.webp" alt="Chart of Accounts list grouped by type, with Initialize Standard Accounts and Add Account buttons" />

<Callout type="note" title="System accounts are protected">
Accounts created by **Initialize Standard Accounts** can't have their code or type changed, and can only be deactivated, not deleted, once they have entries against them. Accounts you add yourself can be edited or deleted freely until something posts to them.
</Callout>

## What happens next

New accounts appear immediately in the account picker on the journal entry form, and in every financial statement once something posts to them.
