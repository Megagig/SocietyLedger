Every journal entry, statement and budget posts against an account from this list. Set it up before money starts to move, so the app can enter it in the books for you.

## Before you start

You need the **manage finances** permission to add or change accounts, and **reverse ledger entries** to delete one. Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries have the first by default.

Accounts are grouped as Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Income and Expenses, with what each holds beside it. Select an account to see every entry behind its balance, and a sub-account list if it has any. To change an account, use **Edit** in its menu on the website, or the pencil beside it on the phone. You can rename or describe any account, including a built-in one, and move it under another account of the same type. See [Opening a figure to see the entries behind it](/docs/accounting-guide/opening-a-figure).

## The rules

- **Normal balance follows the type.** Assets and expenses grow with debits; liabilities, equity and income grow with credits. A new account gets the right one automatically. A contra account, such as accumulated depreciation, is the usual reason to choose the other. On the phone, **Grows with** lets you choose it when you add the account.
- **Group on the statements.** Each kind has its own groups, the same as on the website: assets are current, fixed or other; liabilities are current or long term; income and spending are operating, program or other. The group decides where the account shows on the balance sheet and how cash movements are sorted, so give every account one.
- **Groups of accounts.** An account can sit under another of the **same type**, and never under itself or under one of its own sub-accounts. Choose **None** to take it out from under its parent again.
- **Built-in accounts** can be renamed, described and moved, but keep their code and type, and cannot be switched off or deleted.
- **Used accounts are switched off, not deleted,** so their history stays. An account with sub-accounts, or in a budget, cannot be deleted at all.
- **A switched-off account still shows in the statements** while it holds money, so the totals stay true. You just cannot post to it.
