A period is a stretch of dates, usually a month or a year. Once your books for it are final, you close it, and nothing more can be posted to a date inside it.

## Before you start

You need the **approve payments** permission, which Administrators and Treasurers have by default.

## Add, close and lock a period

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Accounting", "Periods"]} /> and select <UiLabel>New Period</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Give it a name, choose month, quarter or year, and set the first and last day.</Step>
<Step>When the books for it are settled, select <UiLabel>Close</UiLabel> on the period.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Accounting", "Accounting periods"]} /> and tap **+**.</Step>
<Step>Name the period, choose what it covers, and pick its first and last day.</Step>
<Step>Tap a period, then **Close this period**. Tap **Open it again** or **Lock it for good** on a closed one.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## The three states

- **Open.** Entries can be posted to any date in it.
- **Closed.** Nothing can be posted to a date in it. You can open it again if you must, and each reopening is recorded in the [audit log](/docs/settings/audit-log).
- **Locked.** Final. A locked period can never be opened again by anyone.

A period runs from the first moment of its first day to the last moment of its last day, in Lagos time. Two periods cannot share a day.

## What closing checks

- **Entries still waiting.** A period cannot be closed while entries in it are waiting to be approved or posted. Post or reject them first.
- **Posting is checked again.** An entry written before a period was closed cannot be posted into it afterwards.
- **Automatic entries too.** If money moves with a date in a closed period, the payment goes through, but its entry waits in the list of things that could not be entered until you fix it.

Dates that no period covers are not restricted. Add a period for every month or year you want to be able to close.
