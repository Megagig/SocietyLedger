In most accounting systems, a period you close or lock stops new entries from being backdated into it. SocietyLedger has the underlying data model for this, but no screen or action to use it yet.

## How it works

<Callout type="note" title="Not available today">
There's no page to open, close or lock a period, and no option anywhere in Accounting that checks whether a period is open before letting you post an entry. Every journal entry can be dated into any past or future period, closed or not, because nothing enforces a period's status.
</Callout>

If this changes in a future release, the concept is: a period (typically a month or a fiscal year) starts **Open**, moves to **Closed** once your books for it are final, and can be **Locked** to prevent even an administrator from reopening it.

## Why it matters

Until period locking exists, your only real safeguard against an old journal entry being backdated into a period you consider "closed" is reviewing recent entries yourself — see [Journal entries: draft, approve, post](/docs/accounting-guide/journal-entries) for the approval step that at least ensures a second person saw it first.
