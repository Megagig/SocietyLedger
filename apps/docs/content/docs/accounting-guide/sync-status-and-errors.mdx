Sync status tells you how much of your financial activity has actually reached Accounting — and for two of the three modules it tracks, the honest answer is "none of it, and nothing you click will change that yet."

## Before you start

You need the **manage settings** permission — this screen lives under Settings, not Accounting itself.

## Check and fix sync status

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Accounting Integration"]} />.</Step>
<Step>**Overall Sync Status** shows Total Records, Synced, Unsynced and an overall Sync Rate across every module.</Step>
<Step>Under **Finance Module**, each of Transactions and Payments has its own **Sync Now** button — select it to sync everything currently unsynced for that type, up to 100 records per click. Repeat if Unsynced is still above 0 after it finishes.</Step>
<Step>**Welfare Module** and **Cooperatives Module** show the same Total / Synced / Unsynced breakdown, but have no **Sync Now** button — see the callout below for why.</Step>
<Step>If **Recent Sync Errors** appears, each row names the record and the error returned when syncing it failed.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/accounting-guide/sync-status-and-errors-1.webp" alt="Accounting Integration page showing Overall Sync Status, Finance Module sync rates with Sync Now buttons, and Welfare and Cooperatives modules with no sync button" />

<Callout type="warning" title="Welfare and Cooperatives can't be synced at all yet">
Only Finance transactions and payments can ever be synced — there's no **Sync Now** button for Welfare or Cooperatives because the feature to sync them doesn't exist yet, not because there's nothing to sync. Their 0% sync rate will never move.
</Callout>

A sync error is rare in practice — the most common cause is syncing before the chart of accounts has been initialized, which is why [setting up the chart of accounts](/docs/accounting-guide/chart-of-accounts) has to come first. If you see "Required accounts not found," that's what happened; initialize the standard accounts (or re-add whichever one was removed) and try **Sync Now** again.

## What happens next

A successful sync posts a journal entry per record immediately — there's no approval step for synced entries, unlike one you create by hand. See [How charges and payments post to accounts](/docs/accounting-guide/how-transactions-post) for which accounts it uses.
