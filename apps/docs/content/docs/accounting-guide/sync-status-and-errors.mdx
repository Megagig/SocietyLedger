Money that moves in the app is entered in the books automatically. Occasionally an entry fails, and the record waits until you fix the cause and catch it up.

## Before you start

To catch up you need the permission to change finances (Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries have it). The web page lives under Settings and needs the **manage settings** permission.

## See what is missing and catch it up

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Accounting Integration"]} />.</Step>
<Step>**Overall Sync Status** shows the total records, how many are in the books, and how many are not.</Step>
<Step>Under **Finance Module**, **Welfare Module** and **Cooperatives Module**, select <UiLabel>Sync Now</UiLabel> on a kind of record to enter up to 100 missing ones. Repeat if some remain.</Step>
<Step>If **Recent Sync Errors** appears, each row names the record and why it could not be entered.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Accounting"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Sync status** (or **Not in the books yet** under **Needs attention**) to see each kind of record, grouped as Finance, Welfare and Cooperatives, with how many are in the books.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Catch up** beside a kind to enter its missing records, or **Catch up everything** at the top. It enters up to 100 of each kind at a time.</Step>
<Step>Anything that still fails is listed under **Could not be entered**, with the reason.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

<Screenshot src="/images/accounting-guide/sync-status-and-errors-1.webp" alt="Accounting Integration page showing Overall Sync Status, Finance Module sync rates with Sync Now buttons, and Welfare and Cooperatives modules" />

The two screens show the same numbers: charges and payments under Finance; contributions and payouts under Welfare; savings, loans paid out and loan repayments under Cooperatives.

## The commonest causes

- **The chart of accounts is not set up.** Initialize the standard accounts (see [The chart of accounts](/docs/accounting-guide/chart-of-accounts)), then catch up.
- **The date is in a closed period.** Open the period again, or write a manual entry dated in an open period. See [Accounting periods](/docs/accounting-guide/accounting-periods).
- **A required account was switched off or removed.** Switch it back on.

The reason shown is plain words only. Nothing technical is shown.
