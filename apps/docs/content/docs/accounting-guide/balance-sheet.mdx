The full balance sheet lists every account that has a balance, grouped under Assets, Liabilities and Equity.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Accounting", "Balance Sheet"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Pick an **As of Date** — it defaults to today.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/accounting-guide/balance-sheet-1.webp" alt="Balance Sheet report showing Assets, Liabilities and Equity sections with a green Balanced indicator" />

Worked example, as of today, with one posted ₦50,000 dues entry:

| Section | Account | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Current Assets | Member Dues Receivable | ₦50,000 |
| **Total Assets** | | **₦50,000** |
| Current Liabilities | — | ₦0 |
| **Total Liabilities** | | **₦0** |
| Equity | Retained Earnings | ₦50,000 |
| **Total Equity** | | **₦50,000** |

**Retained Earnings** is calculated, not posted directly — it's what carries your accumulated net income from past periods into equity.

## Why it matters

A balance sheet that doesn't balance (Assets ≠ Liabilities + Equity) means something was posted outside the normal double-entry flow — this should never happen through the journal entry and sync workflows alone, so treat it as a bug rather than something to correct manually.
