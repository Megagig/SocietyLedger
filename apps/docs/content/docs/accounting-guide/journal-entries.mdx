An entry you write by hand needs two people: one to write it, and a different one to approve it. Only then can it be posted to the books.

Open any entry to see its lines; select an account on a line to see that account's ledger. An entry the app made has a **Where this came from** card that opens the charge, payment, loan or other record behind it. See [Opening a figure to see the entries behind it](/docs/accounting-guide/opening-a-figure).

## Before you start

- To write and send entries you need the **manage finances** permission (Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries by default).
- To approve, reject or post you need **approve payments**, which only Administrators and Treasurers have by default.

## Write and send an entry

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Accounting", "Journal Entries"]} /> and select <UiLabel>New Entry</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Set the **Entry Date**, a **Description**, and an optional **Reference**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>+ Add Line</UiLabel> for each account the entry touches, with a **Debit** or a **Credit** on each line. It can be saved only when it reads **Balanced**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Save as Draft</UiLabel>, then open the draft and select <UiLabel>Submit for Approval</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Accounting", "Journal entries"]} /> and tap **+**.</Step>
<Step>Set the **Date**, say what it is for, and add a reference if you have one.</Step>
<Step>For each line, choose the account and enter a **Debit** or a **Credit**. The panel underneath says whether they match, and by how much they differ if not.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Save draft**, or **Send for approval** to do both in one go.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

Debits must equal credits **to the kobo**. An entry that is out by even ₦0.01 cannot be saved, and amounts can have at most two decimal places.

## Approve and post

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>A **different** officer opens the waiting entry and selects <UiLabel>Approve</UiLabel>, or <UiLabel>Reject</UiLabel> with a reason.</Step>
<Step>Once it is approved, select <UiLabel>Post Entry</UiLabel> and enter your password.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>A **different** officer opens the entry and taps **Approve**, or **Reject** and gives a reason.</Step>
<Step>Once it is approved, tap **Post to the books**, enter your password and tap **Post entry**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## The rules

- **Not your own.** You cannot approve or reject an entry you wrote. This applies even to a small correction.
- **Decided once.** If two officers open the same entry, only the first decision counts.
- **Rejected entries come back.** Change a rejected entry to make it a draft again, then send it again. The reason stays visible while you do.
- **Checked again at posting.** The entry must still balance, its accounts must still be switched on, and its date must not be in a [closed period](/docs/accounting-guide/accounting-periods).
- **Posted for good.** A posted entry can never be changed. To correct a mistake, write a new entry that reverses it.
- **Your password, and a limit on guesses.** Posting asks for your password every time. Five wrong passwords in 15 minutes lock posting (and voiding a charge) for that officer until the 15 minutes are up, even if the next one is right. Right passwords never count against you.
- **Recorded.** Writing, approving, rejecting and posting an entry are all in the [audit log](/docs/settings/audit-log), with who did them.

Entries the app makes when money moves are marked **Automatic**. They are posted straight away and need no approval. See [What happens automatically](/docs/accounting/what-happens-automatically).
