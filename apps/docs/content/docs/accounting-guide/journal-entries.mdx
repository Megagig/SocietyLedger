A journal entry you create by hand goes through the same maker-checker approval every synced entry skips — someone other than you has to approve it before it can post.

## Before you start

You need the **manage finances** permission to create and submit entries (Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries by default); approving or posting needs **approve payments**, which Financial Secretaries don't have — only Administrators and Treasurers can approve.

## Create and post an entry

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Accounting", "Journal Entries"]} /> and select <UiLabel>New Entry</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Set the **Entry Date**, a **Description**, and an optional **Reference**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>+ Add Line</UiLabel> for each account the entry touches, entering a **Debit** or **Credit** amount per line. The **Balanced** / **Not Balanced** badge must read **Balanced** — total debits have to equal total credits — before you can save.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Save as Draft</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Open the draft entry and select <UiLabel>Submit for Approval</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>A **different** officer with the approve-payments permission opens the pending entry and selects <UiLabel>Approve</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Reject</UiLabel> (with a reason).</Step>
<Step>Once approved, select <UiLabel>Post Entry</UiLabel> — this is final; a posted entry can no longer be edited.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/accounting-guide/journal-entries-1.webp" alt="Journal Entries list with entry numbers, descriptions, debit and credit amounts, status badges, and a New Entry button" />

<Callout type="danger" title="You can't approve your own entry">
The same login that created or submitted an entry is blocked from approving or rejecting it — you need a second person with the right permission, even for a small correction.
</Callout>

## What happens next

A posted entry's amounts appear immediately in the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow for its date, and can never be edited again — to correct a mistake, reverse it with a new entry rather than trying to change the posted one.
