A budget only tracks variance once it's Active — creating one is just the first of three steps.

## Before you start

You need the **manage finances** permission to create a budget; approving and activating it needs **approve payments** (Administrators and Treasurers by default).

## Create, approve and activate a budget

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Accounting", "Budgets"]} /> and select <UiLabel>New Budget</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Set a **Name**, **Fiscal Year**, **Start Date** and **End Date**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>+ Add Item</UiLabel> for each account you want to budget, picking the **Account** and an **Annual Amount** — for example, ₦1,200,000 for Membership Dues.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Create Budget</UiLabel>. It starts as **Draft**.</Step>
<Step>Open the budget and select <UiLabel>Approve</UiLabel>, then <UiLabel>Activate</UiLabel>. Activating closes any other budget already Active for the same fiscal year.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/accounting-guide/budgets-and-variance-1.webp" alt="Budgets list showing a 2026 Annual Budget with its fiscal year, period, total budget and Active status, and a New Budget button" />

## Read the variance report

<Steps>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>View Variance</UiLabel> on any budget.</Step>
<Step>Each account shows **Budget Amount**, **Actual Amount**, **Variance** and a status of **Under**, **On Track** or **Over**.</Step>
</Steps>

Worked example, partway through the year: a ₦1,200,000 annual budget for Membership Dues against ₦50,000 posted so far shows a variance of ₦1,150,000 and a status of **Under** — expected this early in the year, not necessarily a problem.

## What happens next

Variance recalculates every time you open it — there's nothing to refresh or re-save. It only reflects **posted** journal entries, same as every other statement in this module.
