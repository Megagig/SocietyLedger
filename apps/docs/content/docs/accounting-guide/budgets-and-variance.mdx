A budget only tracks how you are doing once it is in force. Creating one is the first of three steps.

## Before you start

You need the **manage finances** permission to plan a budget. Approving and putting it in force needs **approve payments** (Administrators and Treasurers by default), and you cannot approve a budget you wrote.

## Plan, approve and put a budget in force

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Accounting", "Budgets"]} /> and select <UiLabel>New Budget</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Set a **Name**, **Fiscal Year**, **Start Date** and **End Date**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>+ Add Item</UiLabel> for each account, with its **Annual Amount**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Create Budget</UiLabel>. It starts as a draft. A different officer opens it and selects <UiLabel>Approve</UiLabel>, then <UiLabel>Activate</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Accounting", "Budgets"]} /> and tap **+**.</Step>
<Step>Name it, set the year and its first and last day, then add a line for each income or spending account with the amount for the year and, if you like, a note.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Save draft**. A different officer opens it and taps **Approve**, then **Put in force**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

<Screenshot src="/images/accounting-guide/budgets-and-variance-1.webp" alt="Budgets list showing a 2026 Annual Budget with its fiscal year, period, total budget and Active status, and a New Budget button" />

Only one budget is in force for a year. Putting a new one in force closes the one before it.

Spending reaches a budget line when an [expense](/docs/accounting-guide/expenses) is approved. The expense form shows where the category stands, and warns without blocking when a voucher takes it over.

## Read how you are doing

Open a budget to see each account against its plan, with a bar for how much is used and a status of **Under budget**, **On track** or **Over budget** (more than 10% above or below the plan).

- **Actual is what was posted within the budget's dates,** not everything since the books began.
- **Income and spending are kept apart.** Adding money in to money out would mean nothing, so each has its own total.
- **A budget covers income and expense accounts only,** each account once, in whole kobo.
- **Only a draft can be changed.**
- **Find a budget** by status and by year in the budgets list.

Worked example, partway through the year: a ₦1,200,000 plan for Membership Dues with ₦50,000 posted so far shows **Under budget**. That is expected this early, and not necessarily a problem.

Select an account line, or the spending or income total, to see the entries posted inside the budget's dates. See [Opening a figure to see the entries behind it](/docs/accounting-guide/opening-a-figure).
