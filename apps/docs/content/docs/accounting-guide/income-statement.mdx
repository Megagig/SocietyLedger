The income statement breaks income and spending down by account, for the period you choose. It counts what was posted **within that period**, not everything since the books began.

## How it works

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Accounting", "Income Statement"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Pick a **Period**: this month, last month, this quarter, this year, or a custom range.</Step>
<Step>Switch on **Include Comparison** to see the stretch of the same length just before.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Generate Report</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Accounting", "Financial statements"]} /> and choose **Income**.</Step>
<Step>Pick a **Period** from the list, or **Choose dates**. Under **Compare with**, choose **The period before** to see how income, expenses and the result changed, in naira and as a percentage.</Step>
<Step>Tap the share icon at the top to send the statement as text, or the PDF icon (if you may export) to send it as a PDF with your association's name on it.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

<Screenshot src="/images/accounting-guide/income-statement-1.webp" alt="Income Statement report with revenue and expense accounts, Net Income total, and a period comparison column" />

Worked example, for October 2026 with ₦50,000 of dues posted in October and ₦20,000 posted in September:

| Account | October | September |
|---|---|---|
| Income — Membership Dues | ₦50,000 | ₦20,000 |
| **Total income** | **₦50,000** | **₦20,000** |
| Total expenses | ₦0 | ₦0 |
| **Net surplus** | **₦50,000** | **₦20,000** |

## What it counts

- **Posted entries only.** A draft, or an entry waiting for approval, is not counted yet.
- **Days are Lagos days.** A period includes the whole of its last day.
- **A loss is shown as a loss.** A negative result reads **Net loss**.
