The full income statement breaks revenue and expenses down by individual account, not just the totals shown on the dashboard.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Accounting", "Income Statement"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Pick a **Period** — This Month, Last Month, This Quarter, This Year, or a custom range.</Step>
<Step>Toggle **Include Comparison** to see the same accounts for the period before.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Generate Report</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/accounting-guide/income-statement-1.webp" alt="Income Statement report with revenue and expense accounts, Net Income total, and a period comparison column" />

Worked example, for October 2026 with one posted entry so far:

| Account | This period | Last period | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue — Membership Dues | ₦50,000 | ₦0 | +₦50,000 |
| **Total Revenue** | **₦50,000** | **₦0** | +₦50,000 |
| Total Expenses | ₦0 | ₦0 | — |
| **Net Income** | **₦50,000** | **₦0** | +₦50,000 |

Only **Posted** entries are included — a draft or pending entry for the same amount wouldn't move these numbers yet.

## Why it matters

The per-account breakdown is what shows *where* income or expense came from, not just whether the period was profitable — useful for spotting a category that's unexpectedly empty, which usually means unsynced activity rather than no activity.
