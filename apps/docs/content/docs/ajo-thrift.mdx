Ajo, esusu and adashe are savings circles: a group pays in on a schedule, and the money is kept for the people in it or paid out to them in turn. SocietyLedger keeps the records. **It never holds your group's money.** The money stays in your own bank account, and SocietyLedger records every payment, gives each person a receipt, and works out who is owed what.

You can use Ajo in two ways:

- **As part of an association.** The association adds the Ajo add-on and its officers run circles for members.
- **On its own.** A savings group with no association signs up as a **thrift group**. It pays only for Ajo, and nothing else in SocietyLedger.
