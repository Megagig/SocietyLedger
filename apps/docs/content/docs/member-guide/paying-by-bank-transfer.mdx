If you've transferred money to your association's bank account to cover your dues, levies or a penalty, record it the same way you'd record any payment.

## Before you start

Ask your association's officers for their **bank account details** if you don't already have them — see [Adding your association's bank accounts](/docs/payments/association-bank-accounts) for where officers set these up.

<Callout type="warning" title="No proof upload on the web yet">
Today, recording a bank transfer here is a self-declaration — there's no attachment upload or officer verification step on the web. Only record it once the transfer has gone through; uploading a photo of your transfer receipt for an officer to verify is planned for the mobile app.
</Callout>

## Record a bank transfer payment

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["My Finances", "Pay"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Tick the items the transfer covers.</Step>
<Step>Set **Payment Method** to **Bank Transfer**, and add your transfer reference number under **Reference** so it's easy to match against your bank statement later.</Step>
<Step>Select **Pay ₦&#123;total&#125;**.</Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

The item is marked paid immediately, and the method shows as "bank transfer" in your [payment history](/docs/member-guide/payment-history-and-receipts).
