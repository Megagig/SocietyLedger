Pay what you owe — dues, levies or penalties — in a few steps, and get a receipt you can see in your payment history straight away.

## Before you start

You need the **view your own payments** permission, which every member has by default.

<Callout type="warning" title="Pay only after you've actually paid">
Selecting an item and choosing **Pay** records it as paid straight away — there's no separate card charge or bank confirmation step on the web today. Only confirm a payment once the money has actually left your account; a true in-app card checkout and bank-transfer verification are planned for the mobile app.
</Callout>

## Pay what you owe

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["My Finances", "Pay"]} /> (or select **Make Payment** from your dashboard).</Step>
<Step>Tick the items you want to pay — dues, levies or penalties each appear as a separate line with their amount.</Step>
<Step>Choose a **Payment Method** (Bank Transfer, Cash, Card Payment or POS) and optionally add a **Reference**, such as your transfer reference number.</Step>
<Step>Select **Pay ₦&#123;total&#125;**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/paying-online-1.webp" alt="Make Payment page with one Monthly Dues item selected, Bank Transfer as the payment method, and a reference entered" />

## What happens next

You'll see a confirmation message, and the item disappears from your outstanding list straight away. It shows up immediately in your [payment history](/docs/member-guide/payment-history-and-receipts) with a receipt number.
