Welfare funds are how your association looks after its own — bereavement, medical emergencies and similar needs — built up through member contributions and paid out through requests.

## How it works

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/welfare-1.webp" alt="Welfare page showing Total Balance, Contributions, Total Payouts and Participation stats, with a Bereavement Support Fund overview and Request Support and Record Contribution quick actions" />

Each **welfare fund** — like a Bereavement Support Fund — shows its balance, how many members have contributed, and how many requests are pending. Under <MenuPath items={["Welfare", "Contributions"]} />, you can see every confirmed contribution to a fund.

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/welfare-2.webp" alt="Payout Requests page listing a disbursed welfare request, with filters for fund, status and request type" />

### Request support from a fund

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Welfare", "Requests"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Select **Request Support**.</Step>
<Step>Choose the **Welfare Fund**, a **Request Type** (such as Bereavement or Medical), and enter the **Amount Requested** and a **Reason**.</Step>
<Step>Attach any supporting documents, then submit.</Step>
</Steps>

## Why it matters

Welfare funds work because members contribute into them before anyone needs to draw on them — knowing the balance and who's contributed helps everyone see the fund is being used fairly.
