Choose which events notify you, and through which channel, so you only hear about what matters to you.

## Before you start

You need the **view notifications** permission, which every member has by default.

## Set your preferences

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Notifications", "Preferences"]} />.</Step>
<Step>For each notification type — Payment Due, Payment Received, System, Approval, Announcement — toggle **Email**, **SMS** and **In-App** on or off.</Step>
<Step>Optionally turn on **Quiet Hours** and set a start and end time to silence notifications overnight.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/notification-preferences-1.webp" alt="Notification Preferences page listing notification types with Email, SMS and In-App toggles, and a Quiet Hours section with start and end time fields" />

<Callout type="warning" title="What the toggles do — and don't — control">
Your choices are saved to your account and applied when SocietyLedger sends you a payment, support-ticket, guarantor, savings-deposit or loan-repayment notification: a type you switch off for a channel isn't sent on that channel. A few senders don't check them yet — meeting reminders and platform announcements arrive regardless. **Quiet Hours** are saved but not applied yet, so notifications still arrive overnight.
</Callout>

## Open what a notification is about

Tap a notification in the app, or select **View details →** on the web (in the notification bell or on the Notifications page), to go straight to the item it's about — a support ticket, a guarantor request, your savings or loans, or the announcement. Older notifications that don't point anywhere simply stay in the list.

## What happens next

Your choices are saved and used for the next notification of that type. Anything you switched off for a channel stops arriving there, apart from the senders listed above.
