My Meetings is meant to be your personal view of what's coming up and how often you've attended.

## How it works

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/meetings-and-attendance-1.webp" alt="My Meetings page with sections for Upcoming Meetings and My Attendance History, and Total Meetings, Attended and Attendance Rate stats" />

When an officer schedules a meeting for your association, it's meant to appear under **Upcoming Meetings** here, with the date, time and location. **My Attendance History** tracks whether you were marked present at past meetings, and the three stats below summarise your overall attendance rate.

<Callout type="note" title="Still rolling out">
This screen doesn't yet show your real meetings and attendance — officers can still see and schedule meetings from their own screens. Check announcements or ask an officer for upcoming meeting details in the meantime.
</Callout>

## Why it matters

Your attendance record can matter for quorum and for anything your association ties to participation, so being able to check it yourself is worth having even while this screen catches up.

## What you can do

- Check your dashboard's **Upcoming Meetings** card for a quick count.
- Ask an officer if you're unsure about an upcoming meeting's details.
