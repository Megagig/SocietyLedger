Three kinds of charge can appear on your account — dues, levies and penalties — each with its own page under My Finances.

## How it works

**Dues** are your regular, recurring charge — usually monthly — set by your association. Each month appears as its own line, marked <StatusChip status="paid" />, <StatusChip status="overdue" /> or <StatusChip status="pending" /> (not yet due).

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/dues-levies-and-penalties-1.webp" alt="My Dues page listing twelve months of Monthly Dues, most marked PAID, one OVERDUE and one PENDING, each with a Pay Now option" />

**Levies** are one-off campaigns — a building fund or a special project — that your association asks every member to contribute a set amount toward by a deadline. Your levy page shows your progress toward that amount.

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/dues-levies-and-penalties-2.webp" alt="My Levies page showing the Building Fund Levy 2026 campaign with a deadline, 0 percent paid, and a Contribute Now button" />

**Penalties** are late fees added automatically when a due isn't paid by its deadline. They appear as their own line, separate from the due that caused them.

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/dues-levies-and-penalties-3.webp" alt="Penalties page showing one unpaid Late Payment Fee with a Pay Penalty button" />

### Worked example

Ngozi Eze's October dues (₦5,000) were still unpaid after the due date, so a ₦500 late fee was added automatically as a separate penalty — her October due and the penalty now show as two outstanding items, ₦5,500 in total.

## Why it matters

Keeping these separate means you always know exactly why you owe what you owe — a regular due, a one-off levy you've chosen to support, or a fee for paying late.

## What you can do

- [Seeing what you owe](/docs/member-guide/what-you-owe) — all three combined into one outstanding total.
- [Paying online with card or bank (Paystack)](/docs/member-guide/paying-online) — pay any of the three the same way.
