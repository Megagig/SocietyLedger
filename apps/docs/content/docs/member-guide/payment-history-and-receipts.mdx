Every payment you've made — dues, levies, penalties and donations alike — is listed here with a receipt number you can quote if you ever need to query it.

## Before you start

You need the **view your own payments** permission, which every member has by default.

## Check your payment history

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["My Finances", "Payments"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Check **Total Payments Made** at the top for your running total this year.</Step>
<Step>Scroll the table for the date, description, type, method and amount of each payment, with its receipt number underneath the description.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/payment-history-and-receipts-1.webp" alt="Payment History table listing dates, descriptions, receipt numbers, payment methods and amounts, with Total Payments Made at the top" />

## What happens next

Any payment you make — whether through [Paying online](/docs/member-guide/paying-online) or recorded by an officer — appears here immediately, newest first.
