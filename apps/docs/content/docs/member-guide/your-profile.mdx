Your profile holds your contact details and emergency contact — keep them current so officers and other members can reach you.

## Before you start

You need the **edit your own member record** permission, which every member has by default.

## Update your profile

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["My Profile"]} /> from the sidebar, or select **My Profile** from your dashboard's Quick Actions.</Step>
<Step>Select **Edit Profile**.</Step>
<Step>Update your **Phone Number**, **Address** or **Emergency Contact** details.</Step>
<Step>Save your changes.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/your-profile-1.webp" alt="My Profile page showing Email Address, Phone Number, Address and Emergency Contact fields, with Edit Profile and Change Password buttons" />

To change your password instead, select **Change Password** on the same page — see [Changing your password](/docs/account-security/changing-your-password).

## Add or change your profile photo

Your photo appears next to your name for your association's officers and members, and in the sidebar and on your profile. Without one, you see your initials.

<Steps>
<Step>Open <MenuPath items={["My Profile"]} /> and select your picture, or **Add photo** underneath it. On the mobile app, open the **Profile** tab and tap your picture.</Step>
<Step>Choose a JPEG, PNG or WebP photo of up to 5MB. On the mobile app you can take a new photo or choose one from your phone, and crop it to a square.</Step>
<Step>Check the preview, then select **Save photo**.</Step>
</Steps>

To go back to initials, select **Remove** (web) or choose **Remove photo** from the menu (mobile).

<Callout type="note" title="Who can see it">
Only people signed in to your association can see your photo, wherever your name appears for them. It is never shown on public pages. Officers with permission to edit members can also set or remove a member's photo, which matters for members who haven't signed in yet.
</Callout>

## What happens next

Your updated details are saved immediately and used anywhere your contact information appears, including to officers managing your member record.
