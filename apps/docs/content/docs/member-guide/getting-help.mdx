Help & Support is your first stop for answers, before raising a ticket.

## Find an answer

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Help & Support"]} /> from the sidebar.</Step>
<Step>Browse **Featured Articles** or a **Category**, or check **Frequently Asked Questions** for a quick answer.</Step>
<Step>Try **AI Assistant** for a conversational answer to a specific question.</Step>
<Step>If none of these help, select **Create Ticket** to reach your association's support team directly.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/getting-help-1.webp" alt="Help & Support page with Quick Actions, Featured Articles, Frequently Asked Questions, an AI Assistant card, and a Create Ticket button" />

## What happens next

A support ticket goes to your association's officers, who can reply from their own support screen — you'll see their reply and can respond back from <MenuPath items={["Help & Support", "Create Ticket"]} />'s ticket list.
