Your financial ledger is a running statement of every debit and credit on your account, with a downloadable PDF version.

## Before you start

You need the **view your own ledger** permission, which every member has by default.

## View and download your statement

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["My Finances", "Ledger"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Check **Current Balance**, **Total Debits** and **Total Credits** at the top.</Step>
<Step>Switch between **All Entries**, **Statement** and **Category Breakdown** to see your activity different ways.</Step>
<Step>Select **Export PDF** to download a copy.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/your-statement-1.webp" alt="My Financial Ledger page showing Current Balance, Total Debits, Total Credits and Total Entries, with All Entries, Statement and Category Breakdown tabs" />

<Callout type="note" title="If your ledger looks empty">
The ledger only shows entries that were posted to it directly — it may show no entries even if you have dues and payments elsewhere on your account. For your actual payment record, use [Payment history and receipts](/docs/member-guide/payment-history-and-receipts) instead.
</Callout>

## What happens next

Every payment, charge and adjustment on your account that's posted to the ledger appears here, oldest or newest first depending on the tab.
