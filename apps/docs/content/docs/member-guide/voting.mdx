My Voting is meant to be where you take part in your association's elections and polls — nominating yourself, casting a ballot, and seeing results.

## How it works

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/voting-1.webp" alt="My Voting page with sections for Active Elections, Active Polls and My Voting History, and stats for Elections Participated, Polls Participated and Participation Rate" />

When an election opens for voting or a poll is published, it's meant to appear under **Active Elections** or **Active Polls** here, with a link to cast your ballot. **My Voting History** keeps a record of what you've voted in.

<Callout type="note" title="Still rolling out">
This screen doesn't yet show elections or polls, even while one is open for nominations or voting. Check announcements for election and poll updates in the meantime.
</Callout>

## Why it matters

Being able to check what's open for voting from your own account — rather than relying only on announcements — makes it easier to take part before a deadline passes.

## What you can do

- [How elections work](/docs/elections/how-elections-work) — the phases an election goes through, from nominations to results.
- Check announcements for election and poll updates.
