My Finances is where you see exactly what you owe, split into dues, levies and penalties, and how much of the year you've already paid.

## How it works

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/what-you-owe-1.webp" alt="My Finances page showing an overdue payment banner, Payment Progress at 91 percent complete, and a breakdown of Dues, Levies and Penalties" />

If anything is overdue, a banner at the top tells you how much and lets you **Pay Now** straight away. Below it, **Payment Progress** shows what percentage of everything you owe this year is paid off, with **Dues**, **Levies** and **Penalties** broken out separately — each shows the outstanding amount and how many items make it up. Further down, **Upcoming Payments** lists what's due next, and **My Contributions** shows your donation total.

### Worked example

Ngozi Eze owes ₦5,500 in total: a ₦5,000 overdue monthly due and a ₦500 late payment fee. Her dues are otherwise fully paid for the year, so Payment Progress shows 91% complete.

| Category | Outstanding | Items |
|---|---|---|
| Dues | ₦5,000 | 1 |
| Levies | ₦0 | 0 |
| Penalties | ₦500 | 1 |

## Why it matters

Knowing the breakdown — not just one total — means you know exactly what you're paying for before you pay it, and whether a penalty was added because something was late.
