Donations are separate from your dues — a way to give extra, whenever you choose to.

## Before you start

An officer needs to have set up at least one donation category first. If none appears in the form, ask your treasurer to add one.

## Make a donation

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["My Finances", "Donations"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Select **Make a Donation**.</Step>
<Step>Enter an **Amount** and choose a **Donation Category**.</Step>
<Step>Select **Donate**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/making-a-donation-2.webp" alt="Make a Donation form with Amount and Donation Category fields, and Cancel and Donate buttons" />

## What happens next

Your donation is recorded immediately and added to **Total Donations** on the Donations page, where you can see your full giving history.
