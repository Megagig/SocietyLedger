Donations are separate from your dues — a way to give extra, whenever you choose to.

## Before you start

An officer needs to have set up at least one donation category first. If none appears in the form, ask your treasurer to add one.

## Make a donation

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["My Finances", "Donations"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Select **Make a Donation**.</Step>
<Step>Enter an **Amount** and choose a **Donation Category**.</Step>
<Step>Select **Donate**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/making-a-donation-2.webp" alt="Make a Donation form with Amount and Donation Category fields, and Cancel and Donate buttons" />

## What happens next

Your donation is recorded immediately and added to **Total Donated** on the Donations page, where you can see your full giving history. In the app, open <MenuPath items={["Money", "My Donations"]} /> and tap **Make a donation**.

## If you made a pledge

Sometimes an officer records a **pledge** for you — a donation you have promised but not yet paid. A pledge is **not** counted in **Total donated**. It shows under **Pledged, still to pay**, with the date it is due.

<Steps>
<Step>Open <MenuPath items={["My Finances", "Donations"]} /> (in the app, <MenuPath items={["Money", "My Donations"]} />).</Step>
<Step>Find the pledge — it is marked **Pledged** and shows what is **still to pay**.</Step>
<Step>On the web, select **Pay Now**. In the app, tap the pledge.</Step>
<Step>Pay online (if your association accepts card payments) or by bank transfer and attach your receipt. A transfer is added to your giving once an officer verifies it.</Step>
</Steps>

Once it is fully paid, the pledge becomes a normal donation and counts toward **Total donated**. If you pay only part of it, the part you paid is counted and the rest stays under **Pledged, still to pay**. You can only pay your own pledges.
