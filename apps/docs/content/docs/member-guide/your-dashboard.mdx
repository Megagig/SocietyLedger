Your dashboard is the first thing you see after signing in — a quick summary of where you stand, with shortcuts to the things you'll do most often.

## How it works

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/your-dashboard-1.webp" alt="Member dashboard showing Outstanding Balance, Paid This Month, Next Due Date and Upcoming Meetings stat cards, plus Quick Actions and Recent Activity" />

Four stat cards summarise your account: **Outstanding Balance**, **Paid This Month**, **Next Due Date**, and **Upcoming Meetings**. Below them, **Quick Actions** links straight to My Finances, Make Payment, My Meetings and My Profile, and **Recent Activity** lists anything that's happened on your account recently.

<Callout type="note" title="For the full breakdown">
The dashboard's Outstanding Balance card is a quick summary. For the exact amount you owe, broken down by dues, levies and penalties, see [Seeing what you owe](/docs/member-guide/what-you-owe).
</Callout>

## Why it matters

Checking your dashboard first means you don't have to go looking for what matters — whether anything is due, whether a meeting is coming up, and what you've paid recently are all in one place.

- [Your meetings and attendance](/docs/member-guide/meetings-and-attendance) — more on the Upcoming Meetings card.
- [Keeping your profile up to date](/docs/member-guide/your-profile) — update your details from the Quick Actions shortcut.
