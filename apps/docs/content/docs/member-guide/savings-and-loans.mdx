If your association runs a cooperative, this is where you check your savings balance, apply for a loan, and respond when someone asks you to guarantee theirs.

## How it works

**Cooperatives → Overview** is your starting point: your total savings, what you owe, how much you can borrow, and anything waiting for an officer to confirm, with shortcuts to everything below.

<Screenshot pending="Cooperatives overview page for a member showing Total savings, Loans you owe, You can borrow up to, and shortcut cards" />

**My Savings** lists every savings account opened for you. It shows your total balance and — if part of your savings is held against a loan — how much is held and how much is available.

<Screenshot pending="My Savings page showing an account's Current Balance, Total Deposits, Total Withdrawals, a Make a Deposit button, and the My Deposits list" />

To save, pay into the cooperative's bank account and select **Make a Deposit** to upload your receipt. It's added once an officer confirms it — see [Depositing into your savings](/docs/cooperatives/depositing-into-your-savings). Only an officer can record a withdrawal.

**My Loans** shows anything you've borrowed, with a **Pay Now** button to declare a repayment with your receipt ([Paying towards your loan](/docs/cooperatives/paying-your-loan)), or lets you apply for a new one if you have none active.

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/savings-and-loans-2.webp" alt="My Loans page with no active loans and an Apply for Loan button" />

### Apply for a loan

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Apply"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Choose a **Loan Product** — its interest rate, tenure range and how many guarantors it needs are shown upfront, and, if it lends against savings, how much you can borrow.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Amount** and **Tenure**, your **Purpose**, and name the required number of **Guarantors** — search for them by name or member number.</Step>
<Step>Review and submit.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/savings-and-loans-3.webp" alt="Apply for Loan form showing the Personal Loan product's amount range, interest rate, tenure and guarantor requirement" />

Your application then waits for your named guarantors to accept, before an officer reviews and disburses it.

### Respond to a guarantor request

If another member names you as a guarantor on their loan, it appears under <MenuPath items={["Cooperatives", "Guarantors"]} /> for you to accept or decline.

<Screenshot src="/images/member-guide/savings-and-loans-4-pending.webp" alt="Guarantor Requests page showing no pending requests" />

## Why it matters

Being a guarantor is a real commitment — accepting one means you share responsibility if the borrower can't repay — so being able to see and respond to a request yourself, rather than finding out some other way, matters.
