A levy campaign is a one-off collection — a building fund or special project — with its own target and deadline, separate from regular dues.

## Before you start

You need a **Levy**-type category — see [Setting up financial categories](/docs/finance/financial-categories) — before you can start a campaign.

## Start a campaign

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Finance", "Levies"]} />, then the **Campaigns** tab.</Step>
<Step>Select **New Campaign**.</Step>
<Step>Choose the levy **Category**, set a **Target Amount** and **Amount per Member**, and a **Deadline**. The deadline can't be in the past.</Step>
<Step>Save, then **Assign Members** to charge everyone who should contribute.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Finance", "Levies"]} />, then the **Campaigns** tab.</Step>
<Step>Tap **+** to start a campaign.</Step>
<Step>Choose the **Levy type**, enter the **Total to raise**, the **Amount per member** and a **Deadline**. The deadline can't be in the past.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Start campaign**, then **Assign members**: all active members, or the ones you choose.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## What happens next

Each assigned member gets a charge for their share, on their ledger as money they owe, visible in their own account and in [Transactions](/docs/finance/editing-voiding-and-deleting) under the levy's category, tracked the same way as any other charge. You can put more members on the campaign at any time while it is active; members already on it are skipped.

## A levy is a debt

What members were charged stays owed until they pay it, or you waive or void their charge — the campaign itself never makes a debt disappear.

- **Close campaign** stops new members being added. Nothing else changes: members keep being reminded, and the amount they owe stays on their ledger. A closed campaign shows how many members still owe and how much.
- **Completed** is not something you set. A campaign shows as completed by itself once everyone on it has paid.
- A campaign can't be reopened once it is closed.
- To drop what one member owes, open their charge and waive or void it, with a reason. That is always done one member at a time.

### Removing a member by mistake

If you put the wrong member on a campaign, open them in the campaign and choose **Remove**. Their charge is voided and taken off their ledger, so their balance is correct. This isn't possible once they have paid anything — waive or void the charge instead.

<Callout type="note" title="Progress can lag behind real payments">
A campaign's own progress percentage can read lower than what's actually been collected — if it looks wrong, check [Transactions](/docs/finance/editing-voiding-and-deleting) filtered to the levy's category for the real figures.
</Callout>
