A levy campaign is a one-off collection — a building fund or special project — with its own target and deadline, separate from regular dues.

## Before you start

You need a **Levy**-type category — see [Setting up financial categories](/docs/finance/financial-categories) — before you can start a campaign.

## Start a campaign

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Finance", "Levies"]} />, then the **Campaigns** tab.</Step>
<Step>Select **New Campaign**.</Step>
<Step>Choose the levy **Category**, set a **Target Amount** and **Amount per Member**, and a **Deadline**.</Step>
<Step>Save, then **Assign to Members** to charge everyone who should contribute.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/finance/levy-campaigns-1.webp" alt="Levies Campaigns tab showing an active Building Fund Levy 2026 campaign with its target amount, member count and deadline" />

<Callout type="note" title="Progress can lag behind real payments">
A campaign's own progress percentage can read lower than what's actually been collected — if it looks wrong, check [Transactions](/docs/finance/editing-voiding-and-deleting) filtered to the levy's category for the real figures.
</Callout>

## What happens next

Each assigned member gets a charge for their share, visible in their own account and in [Transactions](/docs/finance/editing-voiding-and-deleting) under the levy's category, tracked the same way as any other charge.
