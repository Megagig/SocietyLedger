Made a mistake on a charge? What you can do about it depends on whether anything has been paid against it yet.

## Before you start

You need the **edit transactions** permission, which Administrators and Treasurers have by default.

## If nothing has been paid yet

<Steps>
<Step>Open the transaction and select **Edit Transaction**.</Step>
<Step>Change the member, category, amount, due date or notes.</Step>
<Step>Enter a reason for the edit (at least 5 characters) and save.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/finance/editing-voiding-and-deleting-1.webp" alt="Transaction detail page for an unpaid transaction, with Edit Transaction and Record Payment buttons" />

An **EDITED** badge appears on the transaction afterward — select **View History** to see exactly what changed, when, and why.

You can also select **Delete Transaction** instead, if the charge should never have existed. Deleting is a real removal, but you have a few seconds to undo it from the confirmation message. In the app, open the charge, tap the **⋮** menu, then **Delete charge**.

## If it's been paid or partially paid

Editing and deleting aren't available once money has moved — select **Void & Replace** instead. In the app it is in the **⋮** menu on the charge.

<Steps>
<Step>Select **Void & Replace** on the transaction.</Step>
<Step>Enter a reason (at least 10 characters) and the corrected details.</Step>
<Step>Enter **your password** to confirm it is you, then submit.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/finance/editing-voiding-and-deleting-2.webp" alt="Void and Replace modal with a warning about voiding the transaction and a reason field" />

## What happens next

The original transaction gets a **VOIDED** badge (with your reason visible on hover) and a new, corrected transaction is created and linked to it — nothing about the original record disappears, it's just superseded.

## Good to know

- **A charge paid in cash at a meeting** can't be deleted until you void that cash receipt (see [Cash collections](/docs/cash/collecting-cash-from-members)).
- **A voided charge** no longer counts towards outstanding or overdue totals, can't be paid, and stops getting reminders.
- **Undo brings the charge back, not its payments.** That is why the app only offers **Delete** on a charge with nothing paid on it.
