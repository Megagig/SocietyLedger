When a member pays by cash, bank transfer or any method outside the app, record it against their charge so their balance updates.

## Before you start

You need the **record payments** permission, which Administrators and Treasurers have by default.

<Callout type="danger" title="Only confirm a real payment">
Recording a payment updates the member's balance immediately and can't be easily undone — only do this once you've actually received the money.
</Callout>

## Record a payment

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Open the charge from [Transactions](/docs/finance/editing-voiding-and-deleting) or the member's profile.</Step>
<Step>Select **Record Payment**.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Amount Paid** — less than the full balance records a partial payment — the **Payment Date**, and **Payment Method**.</Step>
<Step>Select **Record Payment** to confirm.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Finance", "Record a payment"]} />, or open a charge and tap **Record payment**.</Step>
<Step>Choose the **Member**, then the charge the money is for. Only charges with something left to pay are listed.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Amount received** (it can't be more than what is left), how it was paid, and the date.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Record payment**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

<Callout type="note" title="Taking cash at a meeting?">
Use [Cash collections](/docs/cash/how-cash-collections-work) instead. It tracks the cash from your hand to the bank and lets a second officer check it.
</Callout>

A charge that was voided can't be paid — pay its replacement.

<Callout type="note" title="Online payments go through the platform account">
Card and bank transfer payments made in-app are settled to your association within 24 hours, with a 2% processing fee deducted — full split-payment integration is planned but not live yet.
</Callout>

## What happens next

The charge moves to <StatusChip status="partial" /> or <StatusChip status="paid" /> depending on how much was paid, a receipt is generated, and it appears in the member's own payment history right away.
