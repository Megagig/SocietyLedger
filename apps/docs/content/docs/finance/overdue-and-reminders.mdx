Two related screens help you chase overdue payments: a list of what's overdue, and a way to remind the members who owe it.

## See who's overdue

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Finance", "Overdue"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Check the stat bar for total overdue transactions, outstanding amount, members affected and how old the oldest one is.</Step>
<Step>Sort by due date, amount or when the charge was created, and filter by category.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/finance/overdue-and-reminders-1.webp" alt="Overdue Management page listing overdue transactions with member, category, balance and days overdue columns" />

## Send reminders

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Finance", "Reminders"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Filter to **Overdue Only** if you just want the most urgent members.</Step>
<Step>Select the members to remind, or **Select All**.</Step>
<Step>Select **Send Reminders**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Finance", "Overdue and reminders"]} />. **Overdue** lists members past their due date, biggest balance first; **All unpaid** also shows members who aren't late yet.</Step>
<Step>Tick the members, or tap **Select all**. Tap the phone button on a row to call them instead.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Remind**, add a short message of your own if you like, then **Send reminders**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

<Screenshot src="/images/finance/overdue-and-reminders-2.webp" alt="Payment Reminders page listing members with outstanding balances, each showing their transaction count and a Select All option" />

## How members are reminded

- A member who has a login gets a **notification in the app** (and a push), saying how much they owe.
- A member with an email also gets an **email** for each charge they owe.
- A member with neither can't be reminded here — the list marks them, and you can call them instead.

A charge that was reminded in the **last 24 hours is skipped**, so pressing send twice, or two officers sending at once, doesn't pester anyone. After a send you're told how many members were reminded, how many were skipped and how many couldn't be reached. Charges that were voided aren't included.

## What happens next

Nothing changes about the charges — it's a nudge, not an action on the transaction itself. Your association's automatic reminders (set under notification settings) keep running on their own schedule.
