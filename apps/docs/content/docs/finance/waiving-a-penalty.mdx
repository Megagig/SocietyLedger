Sometimes a late fee shouldn't stand — a payment was actually on time, or there's a good reason to forgive it. Waiving forgives some or all of what is still owed on a penalty.

## Before you start

You need the **approve payments** permission, which Administrators and Treasurers have by default. Only a **penalty** can be waived, and only while something is still owed on it. For a due or a levy, [void and replace the charge](/docs/finance/editing-voiding-and-deleting) instead.

## Waive a penalty

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Finance", "Penalties"]} />, then the **Applied Penalties** tab.</Step>
<Step>Find the penalty and select **Waive**.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Waived Amount** (it starts at everything that is owed) and a **Reason**.</Step>
<Step>Confirm.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Finance", "Penalties"]} />, then the **Charged** tab.</Step>
<Step>Tap the penalty, then **Waive this penalty**. You can also open the penalty charge and choose **Waive this penalty** from its menu.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Amount to waive** (it starts at everything that is owed) and a **Reason**.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Waive penalty**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## What happens next

- **Waiving part of a penalty** lowers it by that amount, and the rest stays owed. The member's balance, the overdue list and reminders all show the lower amount straight away.
- **Waiving everything that is owed** marks the penalty **Waived** and it is no longer owed. Anything the member had already paid stays recorded as paid.
- The member's statement shows a credit for what was forgiven, and the waiver is kept on the **Waivers** tab with the reason, the amount and the date.

## Limits

- You can't waive more than is still owed on the penalty.
- A reason is required, at least 5 characters.
- A penalty that is already waived, paid or voided can't be waived.
- If someone else changes the same penalty at the same moment, the second waiver is refused and asks you to reload — a penalty can't be waived twice by mistake.
- A waiver can't be undone from the app.
