Sometimes a late fee shouldn't stand — a payment was actually on time, or there's a good reason to forgive it.

<Callout type="warning" title="No waive button on the dashboard yet">
The **Waivers** tab on the Penalties page is ready to show a history of waived penalties, but there's currently no button anywhere to actually waive one — the feature isn't reachable yet, even though the capability exists behind the scenes. Contact SocietyLedger support if you need a penalty waived in the meantime.
</Callout>

## Before you start

Note the member, the penalty amount, and your reason — support will need all three.

## What happens next

Once a penalty is waived, it's removed from the member's outstanding balance and appears on the Penalties page's **Waivers** tab with the reason on record.
