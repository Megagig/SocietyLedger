Donations have two layers: a **cause** (a donation category such as "Building fund" that members give to), and optional **funds** (allocations) — named pots a gift can be directed into, with an optional target.

## Before you start

You need the **create categories** permission to set up causes and funds, and the **create charges** permission to record a gift. Administrators and Treasurers have both by default.

## Set up a cause

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Finance", "Donations"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Select **Create Category**, give it a **Name**, and save.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Finance", "Donations"]} />, on the **Causes** tab.</Step>
<Step>Tap **+**, give it a **Name**, and tap **Add category**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## Create a fund (allocation)

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Switch to the **Allocations** tab and select **Create Allocation**.</Step>
<Step>Give it a **Name**, **Description** and, if relevant, a **Target Amount**.</Step>
<Step>Save.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to the **Funds** tab and tap **+**.</Step>
<Step>Give it a **Name**, choose its **Kind**, and set a **Target** if you have one.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Add fund**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## Record a donation

A gift is either **paid now** (money received) or a **pledge** (a promise to give by a date).

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>On a cause, select **Record Donation**.</Step>
<Step>Choose the member, whether it is an instant payment or a pledge, and the amount. For a pledge, add the date it is expected.</Step>
<Step>Optionally choose a fund, add the donor's message, or mark it anonymous, then save.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>On the **Causes** tab, tap a cause, then **Record a donation**.</Step>
<Step>Choose the **Donor**, **Paid now** or **Pledge**, and the **Amount**. Paid now asks how it was paid; a pledge asks for the expected date.</Step>
<Step>Optionally choose a **Fund**, add a message, or turn on **Anonymous**, then tap **Record donation**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

The donor must be an active member, the amount must be above ₦0, and a pledge date can't be in the past.

## What happens next

- **A gift paid now** is added to the member's ledger as a donation, appears under **Recent Donations** with a receipt number, and shows in the donor's own [donation history](/docs/member-guide/making-a-donation).
- **A pledge** is a charge the member owes until they pay it. It has no receipt yet, and it counts as **pledged**, not **received**, in the totals. When the member pays it, it becomes a received gift.
- **An anonymous gift** leaves the donor's name off the receipt and the list.

## Receipts

Open a received donation to see its receipt. On the web you can print it; on the phone, tap **Share receipt** to send it as text through your phone's share sheet.
