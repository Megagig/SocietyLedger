The finance overview is your starting point — total collected, what's outstanding, and what's overdue, with quick actions to the rest of Finance.

## How it works

<Screenshot src="/images/finance/finance-overview-1.webp" alt="Financial Overview page showing Total Collected, Outstanding, Overdue and Active Members stat cards, this month's activity, and Quick Actions" />

**Total Collected** and **This Month** summarise real activity, and **Quick Actions** links straight to recording a transaction, managing categories, viewing overdue members, or generating dues. **Recent Payments** lists the latest payments as they come in.

On the app, open <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Finance"]} />. You see **Collected this month**, what you **billed** this month, what is **still outstanding**, the **collection rate** (how much of everything billed has been collected), a chart of what you've billed by kind (dues, levies, penalties, donations), and the latest payments. If any charges are overdue, a banner takes you straight to them.

<Callout type="note" title="Voided charges aren't counted">
A charge that was voided and replaced no longer counts towards **Outstanding** or **Overdue**, so the figures reflect what members actually owe.
</Callout>

## Why it matters

Knowing which numbers to trust matters as much as having them — the overview is the quickest way to see whether collections are on track.
