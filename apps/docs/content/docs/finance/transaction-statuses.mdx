Every charge has one status, plus badges that can appear alongside it once it's been edited or voided.

## Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | Not yet due, nothing paid. |
| Overdue | Past its due date, nothing paid. |
| Partial | Some paid, balance remaining. |
| Paid | Fully paid. |
| Waived | Closed by an officer without full payment, never counted as outstanding again. |

## Badges

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| EDITED | The transaction's details were changed after it was created — select **View History** for what changed, when and why. |
| VOIDED | The transaction was voided and replaced with a corrected one — hover for the void reason. |
