Due Generation turns a dues category into real charges for the members you choose — the quickest way to bill everyone for the month at once.

## Before you start

You need a **Due**-type category already set up — see [Setting up financial categories](/docs/finance/financial-categories).

## Generate dues

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Finance", "Dues"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Choose a **Due Category**. Its usual amount fills in automatically — override it under **Amount** if this round is different.</Step>
<Step>Set a **Due Date** and any **Notes**.</Step>
<Step>Filter members by status if needed, then **Select All** or tick individual members. Select All covers every matching member, however many there are.</Step>
<Step>Check the **Summary** — selected members, amount per member, total — then select **Generate Dues**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Finance", "Generate dues"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Choose the **Category**. Its usual amount fills in automatically — change it if this round is different.</Step>
<Step>Set a **Due date** and any **Note**.</Step>
<Step>Choose **All active members**, or **Choose members** and tick the ones you want.</Step>
<Step>Check the summary, then tap **Generate dues for all members**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

<Screenshot src="/images/finance/setting-up-dues-1.webp" alt="Due Generation page with a Due Category selected, Amount and Due Date fields, a Summary panel, and a member selection list" />

## What happens next

One charge is created per selected member, each showing as <StatusChip status="pending" /> until paid or overdue — the same as [charging members individually](/docs/finance/creating-charges), just for everyone at once. Each charge goes on the member's ledger as money they owe.

<Callout type="note" title="Generating twice charges twice">
Pressing **Generate** again creates a second set of charges. If you generated by mistake, void the extra charges from [Transactions](/docs/finance/editing-voiding-and-deleting) before members are reminded.
</Callout>
