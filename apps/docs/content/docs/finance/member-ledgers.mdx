The Member Ledger page lists every member's balance in one place — but unlike Transactions, its numbers aren't always current on their own.

## How it works

<Screenshot src="/images/finance/member-ledgers-1.webp" alt="Member Ledgers page listing members with their balance and status, most showing zero balance and no activity" />

Each member shows a **Balance** and a status — <StatusChip status="paid" label="BALANCED" /> or <StatusChip status="overdue" label="OUTSTANDING" />. Search, sort by balance, and select **Sync All Ledgers** or **Rebuild All Ledgers** when the figures look stale.

<Callout type="warning" title="Sync before you trust this page">
Member ledger balances don't update automatically when charges and payments happen — they can sit at zero for every member even with real activity elsewhere. Selecting **Sync All Ledgers** brings the balances up to date; confirmed by testing, it fixes the summary balance but not the itemised entry list underneath, which can still show no entries even after a sync.
</Callout>

<Screenshot src="/images/finance/member-ledgers-2-after-sync.webp" alt="Member Ledgers page after running Sync All Ledgers, now showing real outstanding balances for several members" />

## In the app

Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Finance", "Member ledgers"]} /> and search for a member. A red amount is what they **owe**, a green one is **credit** in their favour, and **Settled** means nothing is owed. Open a member to see their balance, what they've been charged and paid, and every entry.

From the **⋮** menu:

- **Share statement** — choose *This year*, *Last 3 months* or *Everything*, or pick your own **From** and **To** dates for any period up to today, and send it through WhatsApp, email or any app on your phone. It goes as text, not a PDF file.
- **Rebuild this ledger** — if you have the approve-payments permission, this rebuilds the member's ledger from their charges and payments. Use it when a balance looks wrong.

<Callout type="note" title="Voided charges aren't counted as owed">
When a charge is voided and replaced, only the replacement counts towards what the member owes. Any payments already made on the voided charge still count as money received. Rebuilding a ledger after a void now gives the right balance.
</Callout>

## Why it matters

A member's balance here is also what they see on their own statement — if it's stale for them, it's stale for you too, so a periodic sync is worth building into your routine, not just something to reach for when a number looks wrong.
