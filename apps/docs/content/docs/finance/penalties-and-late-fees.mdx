A late fee rule applies a penalty automatically once a charge is overdue — you don't have to catch and charge each one by hand.

## Before you start

You need the **create categories** permission to set up rules, which Administrators and Treasurers have by default. Applying late fees by hand and waiving a penalty need the **approve payments** permission.

## Set up a late fee rule

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Finance", "Penalties"]} />, then select **New Rule**.</Step>
<Step>Name it, and set a **Grace Period** (days after the due date before a fee applies).</Step>
<Step>Choose a **Fee Type** — a fixed amount or a percentage of the balance — and the fee itself.</Step>
<Step>Save.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Finance", "Penalties"]} />, open the **Rules** tab and tap **+**.</Step>
<Step>Name it, and set the **Days overdue before the fee** (the grace period).</Step>
<Step>Choose **Fixed amount** or **Percentage**, and the fee. A percentage can be at most 100, and can have a cap on a single fee.</Step>
<Step>Optionally turn on **Repeat the fee** to charge again every few days while it stays unpaid, then tap **Add rule**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

<Screenshot src="/images/finance/penalties-and-late-fees-1.webp" alt="Penalties page with Penalty Types, Late Fee Rules and Waivers tabs, and an active Late Payment Fee rule" />

You can switch a rule off and on at any time. Deleting a rule that has already charged members switches it off instead, so the penalties it charged and their history stay.

## Who is charged a late fee

A late fee goes to a real overdue **due or levy** that is past its grace period. These are never charged a late fee:

- a **voided** charge, because it is no longer owed;
- a **donation pledge**, because a pledge is a promise rather than a billed obligation;
- a **penalty** — a penalty never earns another penalty.

A rule that names one specific category (other than a penalty) applies to that category, so you can choose on purpose to penalise something else. A rule that doesn't repeat charges a charge once, however many times fees are applied.

## Apply fees to existing overdue charges

New rules also apply automatically each day. For anything already overdue, **Apply Late Fees** runs the rules right now. You always see what it will do first: how many fees, to how many members, the total, and who — and nobody is charged until you confirm.

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
Select **Apply Late Fees**, review the list, and confirm.
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
Tap **Apply late fees now**, review who would be charged, then tap **Charge … late fees**.
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## What happens next

Each affected member gets a new penalty charge, separate from the due that caused it, shown on their account as a **Late Payment Fee** — see [Your dues, levies and penalties](/docs/member-guide/dues-levies-and-penalties) for how it looks to them. If a penalty shouldn't stand, [waive it](/docs/finance/waiving-a-penalty).
