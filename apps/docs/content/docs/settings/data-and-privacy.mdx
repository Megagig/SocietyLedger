Data & Privacy settings record your retention preferences, and this is also where you can export every settings category as a single file.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission, which only Administrators have by default to change settings; **view settings** is enough to export.

## Update data and privacy settings

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Data & Privacy"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Set **Data Retention (Months)**, and toggle **Soft Delete**, **Anonymize Inactive Members**, **Allow Data Export** and **GDPR Compliant**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Save Changes</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>With **Allow Data Export** on, select <UiLabel>Export All Settings</UiLabel> to download your current configuration as a JSON file.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/settings/data-and-privacy-1.webp" alt="Data & Privacy Settings page with retention, soft delete, anonymization and export toggles" />

<Callout type="warning" title="The retention and privacy toggles aren't enforced">
Like the other preference-style settings pages, nothing in the system currently reads **Data Retention**, **Anonymize Inactive Members** or **GDPR Compliant** — no scheduled process acts on a retention period, and inactive members are never automatically anonymized. **Export All Settings** does work, and genuinely downloads your current configuration across every category.
</Callout>

## What happens next

Nothing in your actual data changes based on the retention or privacy toggles. Use **Export All Settings** to back up your configuration before making large changes.
