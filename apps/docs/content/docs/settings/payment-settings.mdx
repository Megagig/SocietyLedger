Payment settings record which channels you'd like to offer and your default method — cosmetic preferences only, not a switch that turns real payment methods on or off.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission, which only Administrators have by default.

## Update payment settings

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Payment Settings"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Toggle which **Enabled Channels** you want to list (Bank Transfer, Cash, Online), choose a **Default Payment Method**, and toggle **Enable Manual Payments**, **Manual Payment Approval Required**, **Enable Partial Payments** and **Auto-Generate Receipts**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Save Changes</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/settings/payment-settings-1.webp" alt="Payment Settings page with enabled channel toggles, default payment method, and receipt and approval toggles" />

<Callout type="note" title="What these settings do">
**Enabled Channels** is checked when an officer records a payment: a method you switch off can't be used, and **Cash** must be on to use [Cash collections](/docs/cash/how-cash-collections-work). **Allow Partial Payments** is checked when a payment is less than the balance. Paystack availability is still decided by whether a gateway key is configured (see [Integrations](/docs/settings/integrations)), not by this list.
</Callout>

## Days to lodge collected cash

When **Cash** is enabled, a **Cash Collections** card appears. Set **Days to lodge collected cash** (3 by default). An officer who still holds cash this many days after closing a collection is reminded, and so are the officers who watch cash.

## What happens next

The settings take effect straight away for new payments.
