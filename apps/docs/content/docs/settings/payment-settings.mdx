Payment settings decide which ways to pay your association accepts when a payment is recorded, whether receipts are made automatically, and how long an officer has to lodge cash.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission or the **billing** permission. Only Administrators have the edit permission by default.

## Update payment settings

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Payment Settings"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Switch on the **Enabled Channels** you accept, and set the other options.</Step>
<Step>When **Cash** is on, set **Days to lodge collected cash**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Save Changes</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Settings", "Payment settings"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Under **Ways members can pay**, switch on the ones you accept. At least one must stay on.</Step>
<Step>Set **Make a receipt automatically**, **Allow part payments** and **Days to lodge cash**.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Save changes**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## What each setting does

- **Ways members can pay.** Checked when a payment is recorded: a method you switch off cannot be used. **Cash** must be on to use [Cash collections](/docs/cash/how-cash-collections-work).
- **Allow part payments.** When off, a payment must cover the whole balance. It is also off if it is off under [Financial settings](/docs/settings/financial-settings).
- **Days to lodge cash.** An officer who still holds cash this many days after closing a collection is reminded, and so are the officers who watch cash. The default is 3.

## Online payments

The mobile screen shows whether card payments (Paystack) and bank payments (Nomba) are on, but cannot change them. They are switched on from the SocietyLedger website. See [Integrations](/docs/settings/integrations).

<Callout type="note" title="Settings with no effect yet">
The web page also shows **Default Payment Method**, **Enable Manual Payments** and **Manual Payment Approval Required**. Nothing in the app uses them yet, so they are not on the mobile screen.
</Callout>

## What happens next

The settings take effect straight away for new payments.
