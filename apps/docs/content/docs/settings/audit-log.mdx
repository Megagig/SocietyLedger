The audit log is a feed of changes made in your association, such as role changes and settings edits, newest first. Each entry shows what happened, who did it, which part of the app it belongs to and when.

## Before you start

You need the **view audit log** permission, which Administrators have by default.

## Look through the log

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Audit Log"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Search, or filter by activity and area, to narrow the list.</Step>
<Step>Select an entry to see the values before and after.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Settings", "Audit log"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Search, or choose an **Action** (Create, Update, Delete, Login, Settings or Role changes), an **Area** and a time under **When**, to narrow the list.</Step>
<Step>Tap an entry to see what changed and the record number it was about.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## What you will see

- Times are in Lagos time.
- Anything that looks like a password, token or key is hidden and shown as `[hidden]`.
- The log is for your association only. Nobody can change or delete an entry.

Not every action in the app is recorded. Role changes, settings changes and the actions of several other areas are.
