Roles are given on the Users page, next to the role badges you see in [Managing user accounts](/docs/settings/managing-users). The Roles & Permissions page is for building roles, not giving them.

## Before you start

You need the **assign roles** permission, which only Administrators have by default.

## Give a role

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Users"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Find the person and select <UiLabel>Assign Role</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Choose a role and confirm.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Settings", "Users"]} /> and tap the person.</Step>
<Step>Under **Give a role**, tap the role.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Give role** to confirm.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## Take a role away

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>On the person's row, select the **×** on the role badge you want to remove.</Step>
<Step>Confirm **Remove** in the dialog.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Tap the person, then tap **Remove** beside the role.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Remove role** to confirm.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## The rules

- **Not your own roles.** You cannot give yourself a role or take one away from yourself. Ask another Administrator.
- **Never the last Administrator.** An association must always have an active Administrator. Give someone else the Administrator role first.
- **Only roles you could hold.** You can only give a role whose permissions you hold yourself. The Election Committee role is the exception, which Administrators can give.
- **One role each, unless you allow more.** Under [Members & access](/docs/settings/members-and-access), **Allow Multiple Roles** decides whether giving a new role replaces the old one.
- **Never left without a role.** If you take away someone's only role, they become an ordinary Member again.
- **Approved people only.** Someone still waiting for approval cannot be given a role.

## What happens next

The change is recorded in the [audit log](/docs/settings/audit-log). The person's permissions update within a few minutes, or when they next sign in.
