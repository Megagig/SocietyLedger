Role assignment happens on the Users page, alongside the role badges you see in [Managing user accounts](/docs/settings/managing-users) — not on the Roles & Permissions page itself.

## Before you start

You need the **manage roles** permission, which only Administrators have by default.

## Assign a role

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Users"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Find the user and select <UiLabel>Assign Role</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Choose any built-in or custom role from the list and confirm.</Step>
</Steps>

## Remove a role

<Steps>
<Step>On the same user's row, select the **×** on the role badge you want to remove.</Step>
<Step>Confirm **Remove** in the dialog that appears.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/settings/assigning-roles-1.webp" alt="A user's role badges on the Users page, each with a remove control, and an Assign Role action" />

<Callout type="note" title="A role change takes effect immediately">
The affected user may need to log out and back in to see every change, since their permission list is loaded at login.
</Callout>

## What happens next

The user's permissions change right away — what they can see and do in the app reflects their new role set on their next action, or after they re-authenticate.
