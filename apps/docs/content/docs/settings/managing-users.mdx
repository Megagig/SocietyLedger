The Users page lists everyone who can sign in, with the roles they hold. It is also where you give and take roles, covered in [Assigning and removing roles](/docs/settings/assigning-roles).

## Before you start

You need the **view roles** permission to see the list. Giving or taking a role needs **assign roles**. Only Administrators have these by default.

## See who can sign in

## What is not here

Only people who can sign in and are active are listed. A person who registered themselves appears once an officer approves them under [Pending approvals](/docs/members/self-registration-and-approvals).

You cannot switch a login off from this page. To stop someone signing in, disable them under [Editing or disabling a member](/docs/members/editing-and-disabling).

To add someone new, see [Inviting members to log in](/docs/members/inviting-members-to-log-in).
