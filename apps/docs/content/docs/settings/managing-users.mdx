The Users page lists everyone with a login account and the role or roles currently assigned to them — it's also where you assign and remove roles, covered in [Assigning and removing roles](/docs/settings/assigning-roles).

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission, which only Administrators have by default to assign or remove a role; viewing the list needs only **view settings**.

## View users

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Users"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Each row shows the user's name, email, platform role (if any) and a badge for each association role they hold.</Step>
<Step>Search or filter the list to find a specific user.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/settings/managing-users-1.webp" alt="User Role Management page listing users with their role badges" />

## What happens next

To add someone new, see [Inviting members to log in](/docs/members/inviting-members-to-log-in) — this page only manages roles for accounts that already exist. There's no way to deactivate a login from here; see [Editing or disabling a member](/docs/members/editing-and-disabling) for disabling the underlying member record instead.
