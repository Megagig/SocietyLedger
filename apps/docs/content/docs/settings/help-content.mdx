This is separate from this documentation site — it's where you write short articles and FAQs specific to your own association, shown to your members inside the app's help center.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission, which only Administrators have by default.

## Add an article or FAQ

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Help Content"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Switch between the **Articles** and **FAQs** tabs.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Add Article</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Add FAQ</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>For an article, enter a **Title**, **Content** (supports markdown), a short **Description** and comma-separated **Tags**. For a FAQ, enter a **Question** and **Answer**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Create Article</UiLabel> or <UiLabel>Create FAQ</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/settings/help-content-1.webp" alt="Help Content Management page with Articles and FAQs tabs and an Add Article button" />

## What happens next

The new article or FAQ appears in your members' in-app help center right away.
