These settings decide whether people can sign themselves up, what role they start with, how long an invite link lasts and how member IDs are made.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission, which only Administrators have by default.

## Update members & access settings

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Members & Access"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Switch **Allow Self-Signup** on or off and choose the **Default Role for New Members**.</Step>
<Step>Set **Invite Expiry (Hours)**, and **Allow Multiple Roles** if one person may hold several.</Step>
<Step>Switch **Auto-Generate Member IDs** on or off, and set a **Member ID Prefix**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Save Changes</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Settings", "Members and access"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Switch **Let people sign themselves up** on or off and choose the role they start with.</Step>
<Step>Set how many hours an invite lasts, and the member ID options.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Save changes**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## What each setting does

- **Self sign-up.** When off, nobody can register on your association's address. They need an invite instead. When on, every new sign-up still waits for an officer to approve it. See [Self-registration and approvals](/docs/members/self-registration-and-approvals).
- **Role they start with.** The role a self-registered person gets once approved.
- **Invite lifetime.** How long a new invite link works. Invites already sent keep the time they were sent with. The limit is 30 days.
- **Member IDs.** With automatic IDs on, new members get the prefix and the next number, such as `OGU0042`. With a blank prefix the prefix is `MEM`. With automatic IDs off, the officer types the ID when adding a member.
- **Multiple roles.** When off, giving someone a new role takes away the one they had.

<Callout type="note" title="Settings that cannot be changed">
The web page also shows **Require Admin Approval** and **Require Email Verification**. Both are always on, so switching them does nothing. They are not on the mobile screen.
</Callout>
