Beyond the built-in roles, you can build a custom role with any combination of permissions. This is useful for a position like an Events Coordinator who needs more than a Member but less than an Administrator.

## Before you start

- To add a role you need the **create roles** permission. To change or delete one you need **edit roles**. Only Administrators have these by default.
- You can only give a role permissions you hold yourself. If you try to give one you do not hold, the app tells you which.

## Create a role

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Roles & Permissions"]} /> and select <UiLabel>Create Role</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Name** and an optional **Description**.</Step>
<Step>Select every permission the role needs. They are grouped by part of the app.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Create Role</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Settings", "Roles and permissions"]} /> and tap **+**.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Name** and an optional description.</Step>
<Step>Under **What it can do**, open each group and switch on what the role needs. Use **Turn all on** to switch on a whole group, or search to find one permission. Choose at least one.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Add role**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## Change or delete a role

Open the role, change its name, description or permissions, and save. On the phone you can search the permissions, and turn a whole group on or off. A role can be deleted only when nobody holds it. Remove it from everyone first, as described in [Assigning and removing roles](/docs/settings/assigning-roles).

## What is protected

- **The Administrator role** always keeps all of its permissions. They cannot be changed, so an association can never lock itself out.
- **Built-in roles** (Administrator, Treasurer, Financial Secretary, Member and Election Committee) cannot be renamed or deleted. Except for the Administrator, you can change which permissions they carry.
- **A custom role's name** cannot be the name of a built-in role.
- **Election Committee permissions.** Administrators do not hold the permissions to run elections, but they can give them to a role, so they can appoint the committee. See [The Election Committee](/docs/elections/election-committee).

## What happens next

A new role can be given to anyone straight away. A change to a role's permissions reaches the people who hold it within a few minutes, or the next time they sign in.
