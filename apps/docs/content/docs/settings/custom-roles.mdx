Beyond the five built-in roles (Admin, Treasurer, Financial Secretary, Secretary, Member), you can build a custom role with any combination of permissions — useful for a position like an Events Coordinator who needs more than a Member but less than full Admin.

## Before you start

You need the **manage roles** permission, which only Administrators have by default.

## Create a role

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Roles & Permissions"]} /> and select <UiLabel>Create Role</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Name** and optional **Description**.</Step>
<Step>Select every permission the role needs — permissions are grouped by category (Members, Finance/Ledger, Settings, and so on).</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Create Role</UiLabel> to save it.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/settings/custom-roles-1.webp" alt="Create New Role panel with name, description, and grouped permission checkboxes" />

## Edit or delete a role

<Steps>
<Step>Select a role from the list, then select <UiLabel>Edit Role</UiLabel> to change its name, description or permissions.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Delete Role</UiLabel> to remove a custom role entirely.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="System roles are protected">
Admin, Treasurer, Financial Secretary, Secretary and Member can't be edited or deleted — only roles you create yourself can. A role also can't be deleted while anyone still holds it; remove it from everyone first (see [Assigning and removing roles](/docs/settings/assigning-roles)).
</Callout>

## What happens next

A new role is immediately available to assign to any user — see [Assigning and removing roles](/docs/settings/assigning-roles).
