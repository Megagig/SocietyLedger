Financial settings hold your association's default late fee, the grace period before it applies, and whether members can pay a charge in parts.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission to change anything; only Administrators have it by default. With only the **view settings** permission you can open this screen and read it, but not change it.

## Update financial settings

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Financial Settings"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Set **Grace Period (Days)**, switch on **Enable Late Fees**, then choose a **Late Fee Type** (Fixed or Percentage) and the amount.</Step>
<Step>Switch **Enable Partial Payments** on or off.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Save Changes</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Settings", "Financial settings"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Switch on **Charge late fees**, then choose whether the fee is a fixed amount or a percentage, and enter it. Set the **Grace period in days**.</Step>
<Step>Switch **Allow part payments** on or off.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Save changes**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## How these are used

- **Late fee and grace period.** They are used only while you have no late-fee rule under [Penalties and late fees](/docs/finance/penalties-and-late-fees). With no rule, applying late fees uses these settings. Once you add a rule, the rule is used instead and these are ignored.
- **Part payments.** When off, a payment must cover the whole balance of a charge. Part payments are also switched off if they are off under [Payment settings](/docs/settings/payment-settings).

A percentage late fee cannot be more than 100.

<Callout type="note" title="Settings with no effect yet">
The web page also shows **Require Payment Approval**, **Approval Threshold**, **Maximum Late Fee**, **Currency** and **Decimal Places**. Nothing in the app uses them yet, so they are not on the mobile screen.
</Callout>
