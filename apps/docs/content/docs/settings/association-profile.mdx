Your association's profile controls its name, logo and contact details. The name and logo appear throughout the app, including the sidebar every member and officer sees.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission, which only Administrators have by default.

## Update your profile

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Association Profile"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Update **Name**, **Association Type**, contact details, address, **Fiscal Year Start**, **Timezone** and **Currency** as needed.</Step>
<Step>Under **Branding**, select <UiLabel>Upload</UiLabel> to add a logo, or <UiLabel>Remove</UiLabel> to delete the current one. Set a **Primary Color** and **Secondary Color**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Save Changes</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Settings", "Association profile"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Under **Logo**, tap **Add a logo** or **Change logo** and choose a picture. Tap **Remove** to delete the current one. The logo is saved straight away.</Step>
<Step>Update **Name**, **Type**, **Brand colour**, contact details and address.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Save changes**. The button stays off until you change something.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## What the logo needs

Use a JPEG, PNG or WebP picture up to 2MB. A square picture works best. The app checks what the file really is, so a different kind of file renamed to end in `.png` is refused.

Without a logo, members see your association's initials on a coloured square. The colour is your **Brand colour**.

## What happens next

Your name, logo and colour update straight away across the app. A field you empty is cleared.

<Callout type="note" title="Fiscal year, timezone and currency">
**Fiscal Year Start**, **Timezone** and **Currency** are on the web page for your records. Reports and the books do not use them yet, and the mobile app always shows naira. They are not on the mobile screen.
</Callout>
