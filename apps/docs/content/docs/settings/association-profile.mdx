Your association's profile controls its name, logo and basic details — the name and logo appear throughout the app, including the sidebar every member and officer sees.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission, which only Administrators have by default.

## Update your profile

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Association Profile"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Update **Name**, **Association Type**, contact details, address, **Fiscal Year Start**, **Timezone** and **Currency** as needed.</Step>
<Step>Under **Branding**, select <UiLabel>Upload</UiLabel> to add a logo (JPEG, PNG or WebP, max 2MB), or <UiLabel>Remove</UiLabel> to delete the current one. Set a **Primary Color** and **Secondary Color**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Save Changes</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/settings/association-profile-1.webp" alt="Association Profile page showing name, type, branding logo upload, and locale fields with a Save Changes button" />

## What happens next

Your name and logo update immediately across the app. Changing **Currency**, **Timezone** or **Fiscal Year Start** here is cosmetic only — see [Financial settings](/docs/settings/financial-settings) for why.
