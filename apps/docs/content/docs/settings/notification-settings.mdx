Notification settings record your preferred reminder schedule, channels and sender name at the association level.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission, which only Administrators have by default.

## Update notification settings

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Notification Settings"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Toggle **Email Enabled** and **SMS Enabled**, set **Payment Reminder Days** (how many days before a due date to remind), an **Overdue Reminder Frequency**, and a **Sender Name**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Save Changes</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/settings/notification-settings-1.webp" alt="Notification Settings page with email and SMS toggles, payment reminder days, overdue frequency, and sender name fields" />

<Callout type="warning" title="Nothing currently reads these values">
These settings save correctly but nothing reads them — payment reminders are a manual action with no real send behind them regardless of what's configured here (see [Following up overdue members](/docs/finance/overdue-and-reminders)), and there's no scheduled job anywhere that would act on a reminder schedule even if one were set.
</Callout>

## What happens next

Nothing changes elsewhere in the app yet — see the callout above.
