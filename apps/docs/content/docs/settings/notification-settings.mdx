These settings control the automatic reminder emails members get about charges, and the sender name on those emails and on payment emails.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission, which only Administrators have by default.

## Update reminder settings

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Notification Settings"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Switch **Email Enabled** on or off, set **Payment Reminder Days** and the **Overdue Reminder Frequency**, and enter a **Sender Name**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Save Changes</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Settings", "Reminders and sender"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Switch **Email members about their charges** on or off. Enter the **Days before the due date**, separated by commas, such as `7, 3, 1`, and choose how often to remind once a charge is overdue.</Step>
<Step>Enter the name the emails come from, then tap **Save changes**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

<Screenshot src="/images/settings/notification-settings-1.webp" alt="Notification Settings page with email and SMS toggles, payment reminder days, overdue frequency, and sender name fields" />

## What happens

Once a day, in the morning, the app emails each member whose charge is due in one of your reminder days. A charge that is overdue is reminded again every day, week or month, as you chose. Choose **Never** to stop repeat reminders. You can use up to 6 reminder days, from 1 to 60.

Switching email off stops every automatic reminder email for your association. Reminders you send by hand from [Following up overdue members](/docs/finance/overdue-and-reminders) still use your sender name.

<Callout type="note" title="Settings with no effect yet">
The web page also shows **SMS Enabled** and quiet hours. Nothing in the app uses them yet, so they are not on the mobile screen. Texts are sent from [Communication](/docs/communication/sending-sms).
</Callout>
