The Exports Center is the one place to download any of your reports, your member list, or an individual member's statement.

## Before you start

You need the **view finances** or **export members** permission, which Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries have by default.

## Export something

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Exports"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Under **Quick Exports**, select **Financial Summary** (PDF or Excel), **All Members** (Excel), or **Member Statement** (PDF, after choosing a member).</Step>
<Step>Your file downloads automatically.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/reports/exporting-reports-1.webp" alt="Exports Center with Quick Export cards for Financial Summary, All Members and Member Statement, Recent Exports, and links to Export History and Custom Export" />

## What happens next

The export also appears under **Recent Exports** here and in your full [Export history](/docs/reports/export-history), so you can re-download it without generating it again — for a while, at least.
