Collection rate is simply what you collected divided by what you expected to collect — a single number that tells you how well billing is translating into actual money.

## How it works

<Screenshot src="/images/reports/collection-rate-1.webp" alt="Collection Rate Report showing Total Expected, Total Collected, Outstanding and Collection Rate stats, a trend chart over time, and a Collection Rate by Category table" />

**Total Expected**, **Total Collected** and **Outstanding** give the raw numbers; **Collection Rate** is the percentage. **Collection Trend Over Time** charts it by period, and **Collection Rate by Category** breaks it down by what the charge was for.

### Worked example

This period, ₦3,636,500 was expected and ₦3,145,000 was collected — a collection rate of 86.5%. Monthly Dues alone came in a little higher, at 87.4%.

## Why it matters

A falling collection rate is an early warning sign, even before overdue amounts pile up — it's worth checking alongside [aging analysis](/docs/reports/aging-analysis) if the trend turns down.
