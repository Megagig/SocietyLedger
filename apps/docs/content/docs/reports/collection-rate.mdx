Collection rate is what has been paid divided by what you charged: one number that tells you how well billing turns into money.

## How it works

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
Go to <MenuPath items={["Reports", "Collection Rate"]} />. Pick a period (it starts on this year) and choose to group **by month, quarter or year**.
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Reports", "Collection rate"]} />. Pick a period (it starts on this year) and choose **By month**, **By quarter** or **By year**.
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

**Charged**, **Paid so far** and **Still owed** give the raw numbers, and **Collection rate** is the percentage. The chart shows the rate for each month, quarter or year, and a table breaks it down by category.

A charge belongs to the period it was **charged** in, and counts as paid whenever it was paid. Cancelled and forgiven charges are not counted. A custom date range is used exactly as you pick it.

## Open any figure

Select **Charged** to see every charge, **Still owed** to see the unpaid ones, a bar or row to see the charges raised in that month, or a category to see its charges. See [Opening a figure](/docs/reports/drilling-into-a-figure).

## Why it matters

A falling collection rate is an early warning, even before overdue amounts pile up. If it turns down, check [aging analysis](/docs/reports/aging-analysis) next.

## What you can do

- [Financial summary report](/docs/reports/financial-summary) — the same rate, alongside the money received.
- [Income by category](/docs/reports/income-by-category) — see which category is dragging the rate down.
