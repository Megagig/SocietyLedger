The money that came in during a period, how much of what you charged in it has been paid, and how this period compares to the one before.

## Before you start

You need the **view finances** permission, which Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries have by default. Your plan must include reports.

<Step>Pick a **Period**. It starts on this month. Choose **Custom** to pick two dates.</Step>

## What the figures mean

- **Money received** is every payment dated in the period, whichever charge it was for. A payment made this month for last month's dues counts as money received this month.
- **Charged in this period** is what was charged during it. A charge that was cancelled (voided) or forgiven (waived) is not counted.
- **Paid on those charges** is how much of that has been paid so far, whenever it was paid.
- **Still owed on them** is the difference, and the **collection rate** is paid divided by charged.

Penalties members pay count as money received. The report does not show spending: that is kept in [Accounting](/docs/accounting/income-statement-at-a-glance).

Periods run on Lagos time, from midnight on the first day to the last moment of the last day.

## Open any figure

Every figure can be opened. Select **Money received** to see each payment, **Charged** to see each charge, **Still owed** to see what is unpaid, and a category to see its payments. The list always adds up to the figure you selected. See [Opening a figure](/docs/reports/drilling-into-a-figure).
