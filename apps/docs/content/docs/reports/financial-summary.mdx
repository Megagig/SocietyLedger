Your income, how much of it you actually collected, and how this period compares to the last one.

## Before you start

You need the **view finances** permission, which Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries have by default.

## Read the report

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Reports", "Financial Summary"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Pick a **Period** — the report defaults to this month.</Step>
<Step>Toggle **Include period comparison** to see it against the previous period.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/reports/financial-summary-1.webp" alt="Financial Summary Report showing Total Income, Total Expected, Total Collected and Collection Rate, a period comparison chart, and a category breakdown table" />

**Total Income**, **Total Expected**, **Total Collected** and **Collection Rate** summarise the period; **Income by Category** and **Category Breakdown** show where it came from.

## What happens next

Select **Export PDF** or **Export Excel** to download it — see [Exporting to PDF and Excel](/docs/reports/exporting-reports).
