See one category's numbers on their own: how much was charged, paid and is still owed, and how many of its charges are in each state.

## Before you start

You need the **view finances** permission, which Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries have by default.

## Read the report

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Reports", "By Category"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Filter by category **Type** (Due, Levy, Donation, Penalty) if you want.</Step>
<Step>Select a category to see its full breakdown.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Reports", "By category"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Pick a period and filter by type with the chips.</Step>
<Step>Each category is a card with its charged, paid and owed amounts.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

Each category shows how many of its charges are **Paid**, **Part paid**, **Not paid yet**, **Overdue** or **Waived**. A charge that is not paid and is past its due date shows as **Overdue** even if nobody has run the overdue job. Cancelled charges are left out, and waived charges are shown on their own line and not counted as owed.

## Open any figure

Select a category to see every charge in it, a count (such as **Overdue 4**) to see exactly those charges, or a recent charge to open it. See [Opening a figure](/docs/reports/drilling-into-a-figure).

## What happens next

Use this alongside [financial categories](/docs/finance/financial-categories) if a category's numbers look off: it reads from the same charges you set up there.
