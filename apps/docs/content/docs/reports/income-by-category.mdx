See one category's numbers on their own — how much was expected, collected, and what status every charge in it is in.

## Before you start

You need the **view finances** permission, which Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries have by default.

## Read the report

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Reports", "By Category"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Pick a **Period**, and optionally filter by category **Type** (Due, Levy, Donation, Penalty).</Step>
<Step>Select a category from the chart or list to see its full breakdown.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/reports/income-by-category-1.webp" alt="Category-wise Analysis showing a pie chart of expected amount by category, and a detail panel for Monthly Dues with Expected, Collected, Rate and a Transaction Status breakdown" />

Selecting a category shows its **Collection Progress** and a **Transaction Status** breakdown — how many charges in it are paid, pending, overdue, partial or waived.

## What happens next

Use this alongside [financial categories](/docs/finance/financial-categories) if a category's numbers look off — it's reading from the same charges you set up there.
