Not all overdue money is equally urgent — aging analysis groups it by how long it's been outstanding, so you know where to focus first.

## How it works

<Screenshot src="/images/reports/aging-analysis-1.webp" alt="Aging Analysis Report showing Total Outstanding, Members with Balance and Outstanding Transactions stats, a bar chart of four age buckets, and Member Details grouped by age" />

Every outstanding charge falls into one bucket — **0-30 days**, **31-60 days**, **61-90 days**, or **90+ days** — each shown with its transaction count, amount, and share of the total. **Member Details** breaks each bucket down by who owes what.

### Worked example

₦491,500 is outstanding across the association. Of that, ₦289,000 (58.8%) is less than 30 days overdue — recent and likely to be paid soon — while ₦82,500 (16.8%) has been outstanding for over 90 days and may need a direct follow-up.

## Why it matters

A member who's 5 days late needs a different response than one who's 95 days late — aging analysis tells you which is which without checking each account individually.
