Not all unpaid money is equally urgent. Aging analysis groups it by how late it is, so you know where to focus first.

## How it works

Everything still owed falls into one band:

- **Not yet due**: the due date has not come, or the charge has no due date.
- **1-30 days late**, **31-60 days late**, **61-90 days late** and **Over 90 days late**.

Each band shows how many charges it holds, how much is owed in it, and its share of the total. The bands always add up to the **Total still owed**. Under each band you can see which members owe what in it.

A charge is late once its due date has passed, the same as on the overdue list. A cancelled or forgiven charge is not counted, and neither is a member who was disabled.

## Open any figure

Select a band to see every charge in it, or a member under a band to see just their charges there. The list adds up to the figure you selected. See [Opening a figure](/docs/reports/drilling-into-a-figure).

## Why it matters

A member who is 5 days late needs a different response than one who is 95 days late. Aging analysis tells you which is which without checking each account.
