See every member's charges, payments and remaining balance in one searchable, sortable list.

## Before you start

You need the **view finances** permission, which Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries have by default.

## What the figures mean

The figures at the top add up **every member in the list**, not just the page you are looking at. A member's **balance** is what they were charged minus what they paid. Cancelled and forgiven charges are not counted, so a member is never shown as owing money that was cancelled or forgiven.

Members who are active, inactive or suspended are listed, because they still owe what they owe. A member who was disabled is left out, and so is their debt.

A charge is **overdue** when it still has something to pay and its due date has passed.

## Open any figure

Select a member's balance to see the charges they owe, and from there any single charge or their whole ledger. Select **Still owed** to see every unpaid charge, or **Overdue charges** to see the overdue ones. See [Opening a figure](/docs/reports/drilling-into-a-figure).
