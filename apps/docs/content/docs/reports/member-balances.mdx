See every member's dues, payments and remaining balance in one sortable list.

## Before you start

You need the **view finances** permission, which Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries have by default.

## Read the report

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Reports", "Member Balances"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Search by name, or sort by balance, dues or last payment.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/reports/member-balances-1.webp" alt="Member Balances Report with Total Members, Total Outstanding and Members with Overdue stats, and a table of members with their dues, paid amount and balance" />

**Total Outstanding** and **Members with Overdue** summarise the whole association; each row shows one member's dues, what they've paid, their balance, and when they last paid.

## What happens next

Select **Export Excel** to download the full list — see [Exporting to PDF and Excel](/docs/reports/exporting-reports).
