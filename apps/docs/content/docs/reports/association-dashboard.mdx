Officers land here after signing in — a snapshot of members, revenue and payment activity, with charts for a quick read, and a list of everything waiting on you.

## How it works

<Screenshot src="/images/reports/association-dashboard-1.webp" alt="Admin dashboard showing Total Members, Total Revenue, Pending Payments and Overdue Payments stat cards, a Revenue Analytics chart, Payment Methods breakdown, Quick Stats, and Recent Transactions" />

**Total Members**, **Total Revenue**, **Pending Payments** and **Overdue Payments** summarise where things stand. **Revenue Analytics** charts recent months, **Quick Stats** adds month-over-month changes, and **Recent Transactions** lists the latest activity.

## Needs your attention

At the top of the dashboard, **Needs your attention** lists the work waiting on you — one line per queue, with a count. Select a line to open that queue. It can include:

- new members to approve
- payment proofs to verify, and bank transfers to confirm
- cash lodgments to check (ones you lodged yourself are not counted, since someone else has to check them)
- charges that are overdue
- support tickets that need a reply
- loan applications, loan repayments and savings deposits (if you use Cooperatives), and welfare requests (if you use Welfare)

You only see the lines you have the permission to act on, and a line disappears when nothing is waiting. On the web, the whole card is hidden when there is nothing to do. In the app, it says you are all caught up.

## In the app

Open **Home** and switch to **Association**. The same list is at the top, followed by the stat tiles, **Revenue**, **Payment methods**, **Quick Stats** (which includes **Outstanding Dues**) and **Recent Transactions**. Pull down to refresh. The numbers on the **Manage** tab — the "waiting" and "overdue" badges beside Payments, Cash, Finance, Members and Support — come from the same list, so they always agree with Home.

Some queues, such as loan applications and welfare requests, can only be opened on the web for now. They are counted on the web dashboard but not listed in the app.

<Callout type="note" title="Overdue means past its due date">
**Overdue Payments** counts charges that are still owed and whose due date has passed. Cancelled (voided) charges are not counted, here or in **Outstanding Dues**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note" title="Collection Rate here may not match the reports">
The dashboard's own Collection Rate figure can read differently from the same metric on the [Collection rate report](/docs/reports/collection-rate) — if you need the precise figure, use the dedicated report instead.
</Callout>

## Why it matters

This is the page you'll check most often — enough detail to know if something needs attention, without opening a full report.
