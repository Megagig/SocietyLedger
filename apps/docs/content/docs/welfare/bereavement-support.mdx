Bereavement is one of your association's most sensitive welfare categories — and today it's handled through the same request form as any other type, not a dedicated one.

## How it works

Set up a fund with **Fund Type: Bereavement** (see [Setting up welfare funds](/docs/welfare/welfare-funds)). When a member or officer requests support, they choose **Bereavement** from the same **Request Type** dropdown used for Medical, Education, Emergency, Maternity and Wedding — there's no separate form for the deceased's name, relationship, or date of death. Include those details in the **Reason** field instead, so reviewers have the full picture.

From there, a bereavement request is reviewed, approved and disbursed exactly like [any other support request](/docs/welfare/payout-requests).

## Why it matters

Because there's no dedicated bereavement field set, be explicit in the reason you write — who passed away, the relationship to the member, and the date — since that's the only place this information is recorded.

## What you can do

- [Handling support requests](/docs/welfare/payout-requests) — the same review and approval flow applies.
- [Disbursing payouts](/docs/welfare/disbursing-payouts) — paying out an approved request.
