Create a welfare fund — Bereavement, Medical, Education, Emergency, Maternity, Wedding, or General — and set the rules for who can draw on it and when.

## Before you start

You need the **Administrator**, **Treasurer** or **Financial Secretary** role.

## Create a fund

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Welfare", "Social Funds"]} /> and select **Create Fund**.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Fund Name**, optional **Description**, and choose a **Fund Type**.</Step>
<Step>Optionally set a **Target Balance**, **Monthly Contribution**, and **Minimum Contribution**.</Step>
<Step>Set a **Maximum Payout** to cap how much a single request can ask for.</Step>
<Step>Set a **Waiting Period (Days)** and **Min. Contribution Months** — how long a member must belong, and how many months they must have contributed, before they're eligible to request support.</Step>
<Step>Select **Create Fund**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Welfare", "Funds"]} /> and tap **+**.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Name**, choose the **Kind of fund**, and add an optional description.</Step>
<Step>Optionally set a **Target balance**, **Monthly contribution** and **Smallest contribution**.</Step>
<Step>Set **Most paid out at a time** to cap how much one request can be approved for.</Step>
<Step>Set the **Days a member must wait after joining** and the **Months of contributions needed** before they can ask for support.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Add fund**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

To change a fund, tap it on your phone or edit it on the web. Setting its status to **Suspended** or **Closed** stops new contributions and new requests; what it holds and what was paid out stay on record. A fund with no contributions or requests can be deleted; any other can only be closed.

On your phone, the **Welfare** screen also shows the money held across all funds, what has come in and gone out, and the contributions and requests waiting for you.

## What happens next

The fund appears on the Welfare dashboard and the Social Funds list with a ₦0 balance, **Active** status, and 0 contributors. It starts collecting real contributions once you [record one](/docs/welfare/recording-contributions).
