Create a welfare fund — Bereavement, Medical, Education, Emergency, Maternity, Wedding, or General — and set the rules for who can draw on it and when.

## Before you start

You need the **Administrator**, **Treasurer** or **Financial Secretary** role.

## Create a fund

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Welfare", "Social Funds"]} /> and select **Create Fund**.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Fund Name**, optional **Description**, and choose a **Fund Type**.</Step>
<Step>Optionally set a **Target Balance**, **Monthly Contribution**, and **Minimum Contribution**.</Step>
<Step>Set a **Maximum Payout** to cap how much a single request can ask for.</Step>
<Step>Set a **Waiting Period (Days)** and **Min. Contribution Months** — how long a member must belong, and how many months they must have contributed, before they're eligible to request support.</Step>
<Step>Select **Create Fund**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/welfare/welfare-funds-2.webp" alt="Create Welfare Fund modal with fund name, description, type, target balance and waiting period fields" />

## What happens next

The fund appears on the Welfare dashboard and the Social Funds list with a ₦0 balance, **Active** status, and 0 contributors. It starts collecting real contributions once you [record one](/docs/welfare/recording-contributions).
