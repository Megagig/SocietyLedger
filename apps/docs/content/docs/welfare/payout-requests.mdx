Review a member's welfare request, approve it at the requested amount or a different one, or reject it with a reason.

## Before you start

You need the **Administrator** or **Treasurer** role.

## Review a request

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Welfare", "Requests"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Select the eye icon on a **Pending** request to open its full details — member, fund, type, amount and reason.</Step>
<Step>To approve, select **Approve** — adjust the **Approved Amount** first if you're granting less than requested.</Step>
<Step>To reject, enter a **Rejection Reason**, then select **Reject**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/welfare/payout-requests-1.webp" alt="Request Details panel for request WPR000002, showing member, fund, type, amount, reason, and an editable Approved Amount field" />

<Callout type="note" title="A faster path for the common case">
The checkmark and cross icons directly on the requests list approve at the full requested amount, or open a quick text prompt for a rejection reason — useful when you don't need to adjust the amount.
</Callout>

## What happens next

An approved request moves to **Approved** and waits for [disbursement](/docs/welfare/disbursing-payouts). A rejected request shows **Rejected** with your reason, visible to the member who requested it.
