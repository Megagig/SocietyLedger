Review a member's welfare request, approve it at the requested amount or a different one, or reject it with a reason.

## Before you start

You need the **Administrator** or **Treasurer** role.

## Review a request

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Welfare", "Requests"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Select the eye icon on a **Pending** request to open its full details — member, fund, type, amount and reason.</Step>
<Step>To approve, select **Approve** — adjust the **Approved Amount** first if you're granting less than requested.</Step>
<Step>To reject, enter a **Rejection Reason**, then select **Reject**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Welfare", "Payout requests"]} />. The **To decide** tab lists what is waiting.</Step>
<Step>Open a request to see the member, fund, kind, amount, reason, and how much they have contributed.</Step>
<Step>To approve, tap **Approve**, adjust the **Amount to approve** if you're granting less than requested, and tap **Approve request**.</Step>
<Step>To reject, tap **Reject**, say why, and confirm.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## Limits on an approval

An approval can be less than was asked, but never more than **any** of these:

- the amount the member **asked for**;
- the fund's **Maximum Payout**, if it has one;
- what the fund **holds right now**.

The screen tells you which limit applies. Approving doesn't take any money out of the fund yet — that happens when you [pay it out](/docs/welfare/disbursing-payouts).

**You can't decide your own request.** If you are also the member who asked, another officer has to approve or reject it. A request can only be decided once: if two officers open the same one, the second to decide is told it was just decided.

<Callout type="note" title="A faster path for the common case">
The checkmark and cross icons directly on the requests list approve at the full requested amount, or open a quick text prompt for a rejection reason — useful when you don't need to adjust the amount.
</Callout>

## What happens next

An approved request moves to **Approved** and waits for [disbursement](/docs/welfare/disbursing-payouts). A rejected request shows **Rejected** with your reason, visible to the member who requested it.
