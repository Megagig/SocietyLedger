Record a member's welfare contribution, then confirm it — a contribution only adds to the fund's balance once confirmed.

## Before you start

You need the **Administrator**, **Treasurer** or **Financial Secretary** role to record a contribution. Confirming one needs the **Administrator** or **Treasurer** role.

## Record a contribution

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Welfare", "Contributions"]} /> and select **Record Contribution**.</Step>
<Step>Choose the **Welfare Fund** and the **Member**.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Amount**, **Contribution Date** and **Payment Method**.</Step>
<Step>Optionally add a **Reference** and **Notes**, then select **Record Contribution**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/welfare/recording-contributions-1.webp" alt="Record Contribution modal with fund, member, amount, contribution date and payment method fields" />

## Confirm it

A recorded contribution starts as **Pending** and doesn't count toward the fund's balance yet.

<Steps>
<Step>On the Contributions list, find the **Pending** row.</Step>
<Step>Select the green checkmark to **Confirm**, or the red cross to **Reject**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/welfare/recording-contributions-2.webp" alt="Welfare Contributions list with a confirmed contribution showing Chiamaka Nwosu, Medical Emergency Fund, ₦5,000, Confirmed status" />

## What happens next

Once confirmed, the amount is added to the fund's balance immediately, and the member's contribution history for that fund updates — this feeds directly into [eligibility](/docs/welfare/eligibility) for future requests.
