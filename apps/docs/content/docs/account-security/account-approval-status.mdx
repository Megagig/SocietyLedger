Beyond email verification, a new account goes through an approval step before it can sign in — and an existing account can later be suspended. Here's what each status means if you see it.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step><StatusChip status="pending" label="Pending admin approval" /> — your email is verified, but a SocietyLedger platform admin still needs to review and approve your account. This usually takes 1–2 business days. You'll get an email once it's approved.</Step>
<Step><StatusChip status="overdue" label="Rejected" /> — your registration was reviewed and rejected. Contact your association's admin to find out why, or to be invited instead.</Step>
<Step><StatusChip status="overdue" label="Account suspended" /> — your individual account was deactivated after previously working. Contact your association's admin.</Step>
<Step><StatusChip status="overdue" label="Association suspended" /> — the whole association's access is suspended, affecting everyone in it, not just you. Contact SocietyLedger support.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note">If you were invited by an officer rather than registering yourself, none of this applies — an invited account is active as soon as you set your password.</Callout>

## Why it matters

Knowing which of these applies to you decides who to contact: a pending or rejected account is between you and the review process, an individually suspended account is your association's admin's decision, but a suspended association is between your admin and SocietyLedger support — not something your own admin can fix from their dashboard.
