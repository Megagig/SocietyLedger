If you've forgotten your password, you can reset it yourself from the sign-in page — no need to ask an officer.

## Before you start

You need access to the email address on your account.

## Request a reset link

<Steps>
<Step>On your association's sign-in page, select **Forgot your password?**.</Step>
<Step>Enter your **Email Address** and select **Send Reset Link**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/account-security/resetting-your-password-1.webp" alt="Check Your Email screen confirming a password reset link was sent, expiring in 60 minutes" />

You'll see a confirmation either way, whether or not that email has an account — this is normal and doesn't mean anything went wrong.

## Set a new password

<Steps>
<Step>Open the reset link in the email — it expires after 60 minutes.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **New Password** and **Confirm New Password**.</Step>
<Step>Select **Reset Password**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/account-security/resetting-your-password-2.webp" alt="Reset Password form with New Password and Confirm New Password fields filled in" />

## What happens next

You'll see "Password Reset Successful!" and can sign in right away with your new password.
