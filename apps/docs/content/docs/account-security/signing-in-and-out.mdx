Signing in and out works the same way for everyone — members, officers and founders alike.

## Before you start

You need your association's own web address and a verified, approved account — see [Signing in for the first time](/docs/getting-started/signing-in) if this is your first time.

## Signing in

<Steps>
<Step>Go to your association's web address.</Step>
<Step>Enter your **Email Address** and **Password**, then select **Sign In**.</Step>
</Steps>

Once signed in, you stay signed in on that device for up to 7 days, even if you close the browser — you don't need to sign in again every time.

## Signing out

<Steps>
<Step>Select **Logout** in the sidebar.</Step>
<Step>Confirm in the **Confirm Logout** dialog.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/account-security/signing-in-and-out-1.webp" alt="Confirm Logout dialog asking Are you sure you want to log out, with Cancel and Logout buttons" />

## What happens next

After confirming, you're taken back to the sign-in page, and your dashboard is no longer reachable from that browser until you sign in again.

<Callout type="note">Signing out only affects the device you're on — if you're signed in elsewhere (another browser or device), that session isn't affected.</Callout>
