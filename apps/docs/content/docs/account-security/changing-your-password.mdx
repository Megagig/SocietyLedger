If you know your current password and just want to change it, you can do that from your profile — you don't need to go through the forgot-password flow.

## Before you start

You need to know your current password. If you don't, see [Resetting a forgotten password](/docs/account-security/resetting-your-password) instead.

## Change your password

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["My Profile"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Under **Account Settings**, select **Change Password**.</Step>
<Step>Enter your **Current Password**, then your **New Password** and **Confirm New Password**.</Step>
<Step>Select **Change Password**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/account-security/changing-your-password-1.webp" alt="Change Password dialog with Current Password, New Password and Confirm New Password fields" />

## What happens next

You'll see "Password changed successfully," and your new password works immediately — no need to sign in again.
