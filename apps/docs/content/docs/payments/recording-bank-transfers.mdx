Record a transfer as soon as you see it land in your bank account. Another officer can then check it against your statement and verify it.

## Before you start

- To see and record transfers, you need the **record payments** permission.
- To verify or reject one, you need the **approve payments** permission. Administrators and Treasurers have both by default.
- Add your [bank accounts](/docs/payments/association-bank-accounts) first, so a transfer can be tied to the account it landed in.

## Record a transfer

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Finance", "Payment Management"]} />, select **Bank Transfers**, then select **Record Transfer**.</Step>
<Step>Select the **Member** if you know who sent it, and the **Receiving Bank Account** it landed in.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Sender Bank Name**, **Amount** and **Reference Number**. The sender's account details and a narration are optional.</Step>
<Step>Attach the member's **Payment Proof** if they sent you one, then select **Record Transfer**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Payments", "Bank transfers"]} />, then select the **+** button.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Amount**, the bank's **Reference** and the **Transfer date**, and choose the account it was **Received into**.</Step>
<Step>Choose the **Sender's bank**, and the **Member** if you know who sent it.</Step>
<Step>Select **Record transfer**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

<Screenshot src="/images/payments/recording-bank-transfers-1.webp" alt="Record Bank Transfer form with Member, Receiving Bank Account, Sender Bank Name, Amount, Reference Number fields and a Payment Proof upload" />

A reference can only be used once. "TRF123" and "trf123 " count as the same reference. You can edit a transfer until it has been verified, rejected or matched.

## Verify or reject a transfer

Open a transfer that is **Waiting** and compare it with your bank statement. **You can't verify or reject a transfer you recorded yourself** — another officer has to, so one person never both enters and approves the money. Select **Verify transfer** if it is there, or **Reject** if it isn't, and give a reason. Both are final.

<Callout type="note" title="Verifying doesn't credit the member">
Verifying a transfer confirms that the money reached your account. It doesn't record a payment against a member's charges. To credit a member, [verify their payment proof](/docs/payments/verifying-payment-proofs) or match the transfer to a charge in [reconciliation](/docs/payments/matching-and-reconciliation).
</Callout>

## What happens next

Officers who can verify transfers get a notification when you record one, so you aren't left to remember to ask. You aren't notified about a transfer you recorded yourself. The status moves from **Waiting** to **Verified** or **Rejected**, and to **Matched** once reconciliation ties it to a charge.

## If something looks wrong

- **"A bank transfer with this reference already exists."** It may already be recorded. Search the list for the reference.
- **"This transfer is already verified."** Another officer decided it first. Reopen it to see the result.
- **"That bank account is no longer active."** Choose another receiving account, or reactivate the account first.
