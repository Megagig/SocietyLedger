Record a transfer as soon as you see it land in your bank account, with the member's proof attached if they sent you one.

## Before you start

You need the **record payments** permission, which Administrators and Treasurers have by default.

## Record a transfer

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Payments", "Bank Transfers", "New"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Select the **Member** if you know who sent it, and the **Receiving Bank Account** it landed in.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Sender Bank Name**, **Amount** and **Reference Number** — the sender's account details and a narration are optional.</Step>
<Step>Attach the member's **Payment Proof** if they sent you one.</Step>
<Step>Select **Record Transfer**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/payments/recording-bank-transfers-1.webp" alt="Record Bank Transfer form with Member, Receiving Bank Account, Sender Bank Name, Amount, Reference Number fields and a Payment Proof upload" />

## What happens next

The transfer is logged for reconciliation, and if a proof was attached, it joins the queue in [Verifying payment proofs](/docs/payments/verifying-payment-proofs).
