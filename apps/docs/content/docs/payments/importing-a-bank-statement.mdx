Importing your bank statement is what reconciliation matches against — do this before running auto-match.

## Before you start

You need the **create finance records** permission, which Administrators and Treasurers have by default. Export your statement from your bank as a CSV first.

## Import a statement

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Payments", "Nigerian", "Statements"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Select **Download Template** if you need the expected column layout.</Step>
<Step>Select **Upload Statement** and choose your CSV file.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/payments/importing-a-bank-statement-1.webp" alt="Import Bank Statements page with Download Template and Upload Statement options, and an empty Import History" />

## What happens next

The import appears in **Import History**, and its entries become available for [matching and reconciliation](/docs/payments/matching-and-reconciliation) — select **View Statement Entries** to see each line.
