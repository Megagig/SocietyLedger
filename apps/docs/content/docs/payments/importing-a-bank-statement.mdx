Importing your bank statement is what reconciliation matches against. You don't need to reformat anything: upload the file as your bank gave it.

## Before you start

- You need the **create finance records** permission, which Administrators and Treasurers have by default.
- Add the bank account under [Association bank accounts](/docs/payments/association-bank-accounts) first, since every statement belongs to one account.
- Download the statement from your bank as **CSV** or **Excel (.xlsx)**. PDF statements can't be read reliably, so they are not accepted.

## How your file is read

Banks lay out statements differently, so SocietyLedger works out the layout instead of asking you to convert the file.

- It finds the table even when the file starts with lines of account details.
- It recognises common column names (Date, Narration, Reference, Debit, Credit, Withdrawal, Deposit, Balance), whether money in and out are two columns or one signed amount.
- It reads Nigerian dates (31/10/2026, 31-Oct-2026) and amounts such as 1,250,000.00.
- It shows you what it understood **before** anything is saved. If a column is wrong, you pick the right one.
- It remembers how the columns were read for that bank account, so the next statement from the same bank needs no correction.

A line it can't read is left out, and the preview tells you which row and why.

## Importing the same lines twice

Every line has an identity, so importing the same file twice, or two statements that overlap, never adds a line twice. The preview says how many lines are new and how many were imported before. If every line was imported before, nothing is added.

## What happens next

Only money coming in (credits) can pay a charge. The new lines appear in [Matching payments and reconciling](/docs/payments/matching-and-reconciliation), and each import is listed under **Statements imported** with how many of its lines are matched.
