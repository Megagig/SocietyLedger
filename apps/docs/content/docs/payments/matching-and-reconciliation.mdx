Reconciliation ties each line in your bank statement to a recorded payment — automatically where it can, by hand where it can't.

## Before you start

[Import a bank statement](/docs/payments/importing-a-bank-statement) first — there's nothing to match against without one.

## Reconcile

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Payments", "Nigerian", "Reconciliation"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Select **Run Auto-Match** to match statement entries to payments automatically wherever the amount and reference line up.</Step>
<Step>Select **View Unmatched Entries** for anything auto-match couldn't resolve, and match those by hand.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/payments/matching-and-reconciliation-1.webp" alt="Payment Reconciliation page with Unmatched Payments and Unmatched Amount stats, and Auto-Match, Manual Reconciliation and Import Statement quick actions" />

## What happens next

Each match can be decided only once: confirming it records the payment, and confirming it again (a double tap, or a second officer) is refused instead of recording the payment twice. The full set shows up in [Reconciliation history](/docs/payments/reconciliation-history).
