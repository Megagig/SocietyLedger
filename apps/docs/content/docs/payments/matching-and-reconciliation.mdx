Reconciliation ties each line of money received in your bank statement to the charge it paid. SocietyLedger suggests the matches and shows how sure it is; an officer proposes them, and a second officer confirms.

## Before you start

- [Import a bank statement](/docs/payments/importing-a-bank-statement) first.
- Proposing matches needs the **approve payments** permission. The same permission is needed to confirm, but not by the same person.

## How the suggestions are made

Each suggestion says why: the charge's reference is in the narration, the member's name is, or the amount is exactly what is owed.

- **Very likely (90% and up):** the reference or the member's name fits, and the amount fits. Ticked for you.
- **Likely:** the member fits but the amount is only part of what is owed.
- **Possible:** only the amount fits. It is shown, never ticked, and only when exactly one charge has that amount.

A line is never matched to a charge it can't pay: a line larger than what the charge still owes is refused, and a charge can be filled only up to what it owes.

## The rules that keep the books right

- **The amount is the bank line's own.** It is never typed in and never zero.
- **One line pays one charge.** A line already matched can't be matched again, even by two officers at once.
- **Nothing is recorded until a match is confirmed.** Confirming records one payment for the amount that reached the bank, dated the day it arrived. A payment smaller than the charge makes it **part paid**; only a full payment makes it **paid**.
- **You can't confirm a match you proposed.** A second officer has to check it. You can turn down your own proposal.
- **Turning a match down frees the line** so it can be matched to the right charge. Nothing is recorded.
- **Each match is decided once.** Confirming it twice, or turning down one that is confirmed, is refused.
- A cancelled or waived charge, or one for a disabled member, can't be matched.

## What happens next

A confirmed match shows in the member's ledger and in [Reports](/docs/reports/finding-the-report-you-need) like any other payment. The full set is in [Reconciliation history](/docs/payments/reconciliation-history).
