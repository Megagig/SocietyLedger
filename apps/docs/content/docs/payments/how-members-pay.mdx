Members have three ways to pay — but today, two of the three go through an officer rather than being self-service.

## How it works

<Screenshot src="/images/payments/how-members-pay-1.webp" alt="Nigerian Payments dashboard showing Unmatched Payments, Unmatched Amount and Pending Verifications stats, with quick actions for Bank Accounts, Bank Transfers, Verification and Reconciliation" />

- **Card, via Paystack or Nomba** — an officer opens the member's charge and selects **Pay**, which redirects to a real checkout page to complete the card payment. See [Online payments with Paystack](/docs/payments/online-payments-with-paystack).
- **Bank transfer** — the member transfers to your association's account, and an officer records it (and can attach the transfer proof) against the matching charge. See [Recording and verifying bank transfers](/docs/payments/recording-bank-transfers).
- **Cash or POS** — recorded directly by an officer the same way as any manual payment — see [Recording a payment a member made](/docs/finance/recording-a-payment).

<Callout type="note" title="Members pay themselves too, on their own account">
Members can also mark a charge as paid directly from their own account — see [Paying online with card or bank](/docs/member-guide/paying-online). That's currently a self-declaration rather than a verified card or bank transfer.
</Callout>

## Why it matters

Knowing which path a payment actually took matters for reconciliation — a card payment settles through the platform account, a bank transfer needs matching against your real bank statement, and cash needs nothing beyond the record itself.

## What you can do

- [Adding your association's bank accounts](/docs/payments/association-bank-accounts) — set up where transfers should go.
- [Matching payments and reconciling](/docs/payments/matching-and-reconciliation) — tie your bank statement back to what's recorded.
