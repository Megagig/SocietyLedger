Members need somewhere to send a bank transfer — add your association's account here before you start recording transfers.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission, which Administrators have by default.

## Add a bank account

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Payments", "Nigerian", "Bank Accounts"]} />, then select **Add Bank Account**.</Step>
<Step>Enter the **Bank Name**, **Account Name** and **Account Number**.</Step>
<Step>Save.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/payments/association-bank-accounts-1.webp" alt="Add Bank Account modal with Bank Name, Bank Code, Account Name and Account Number fields" />

You can add more than one account and mark one as default — members and officers recording a transfer choose which account it went to.

## What happens next

The account appears in your list as **Active** and becomes available wherever a bank transfer needs a receiving account — see [Recording and verifying bank transfers](/docs/payments/recording-bank-transfers).
