A record of every match, so you can check what was paired, by whom, and what was decided.

## Check your history

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Payments", "Nigerian", "Reconciliation History"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Choose **To confirm**, **Confirmed** or **Turned down**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Payments", "Reconciliation"]} /> and open the **Matches** tab.</Step>
<Step>Choose **To confirm**, **Confirmed** or **Turned down**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

Each row shows the member and charge, the bank line it was matched to, the amount, and its status. A turned-down match keeps the reason that was given.

## What happens next

A match under **To confirm** is still waiting: it has recorded nothing. Open [Matching payments and reconciling](/docs/payments/matching-and-reconciliation) to decide it.
