Card payments go through Paystack or Nomba, initiated by an officer from the member's own charge.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission to change which channels are enabled, which Administrators have by default.

<Callout type="note" title="Settled through the platform account">
Card payments settle to your association within 24 hours, with a 2% processing fee deducted — there's no separate Paystack account to configure per association.
</Callout>

## Enable card payments

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Payments"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Make sure **Card (Paystack)** is ticked under **Payment Channels**.</Step>
<Step>Save your changes.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/payments/online-payments-with-paystack-1.webp" alt="Payments & Billing Settings page with Payment Channels checkboxes including Card (Paystack), and Default Settings below" />

## Take a card payment

<Steps>
<Step>Open the member's charge from [Transactions](/docs/finance/editing-voiding-and-deleting).</Step>
<Step>Choose **Paystack** or **Nomba**, then select **Pay ₦&#123;amount&#125;**.</Step>
<Step>This redirects to the gateway's real checkout page to complete the card payment.</Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

After paying, you land back on a verification page showing whether it succeeded, with a receipt number if so — the charge updates to <StatusChip status="paid" /> automatically.
