When a transfer has a proof attached — a screenshot or receipt of the sender's transfer — check it here before confirming the payment.

## Before you start

You need the **view payments** permission, which Administrators and Treasurers have by default.

## Review a proof

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Payments", "Nigerian", "Verification"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Filter by status if you have a backlog, and open a proof to review it against your bank statement.</Step>
<Step>Select **Verify** if it matches, or **Reject** with a reason if it doesn't.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/payments/verifying-payment-proofs-1.webp" alt="Payment Verification Queue showing Pending, Verified, Rejected and Total counts, currently empty" />

<Callout type="note" title="Proofs are attached when a transfer is recorded">
A proof reaches this queue when an officer attaches one while [recording a bank transfer](/docs/payments/recording-bank-transfers) — there's no separate member-facing upload yet.
</Callout>

## What happens next

A verified proof confirms the transfer; a rejected one notifies whoever submitted it, with your reason.
