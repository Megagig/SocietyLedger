Credits pay for AI requests once your free requests for the day are used. One credit is one request; a report takes five.

## Before you start

You need the permission to approve payments or to manage billing. Other officers can use the assistant but are told to ask someone who can buy.

## Buy credits

<Steps>
<Step>On the web, go to <MenuPath items={["AI Assistant", "Buy credits"]} />. In the app, open **Manage**, **AI assistant**, then **Buy credits**.</Step>
<Step>Choose a pack. The price per credit and the smallest purchase are shown on the screen.</Step>
<Step>Pay on the Paystack page. The credits are added as soon as the payment is confirmed.</Step>
</Steps>

Credits are added once per payment, even if confirmation arrives twice. A failed or abandoned payment adds nothing, and credits never expire.

## What happens next

Free requests are always used first. Your purchases are listed on the same screen.
