Every AI feature shares one daily allowance of free requests per association. Your plan sets it, and it starts again at midnight, Lagos time.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["AI Assistant", "Usage"]} /> on the web, or open the top of any AI screen in the app, to see how many requests are left today and how many credits you have.</Step>
<Step>A question or a reminders list costs 1 request. A report costs 5.</Step>
<Step>Free requests are used first, then credits. A request that fails costs nothing.</Step>
</Steps>

The daily allowance is set by the platform and by your plan, so it can change. The numbers shown on screen are always the current ones. When you need more, see [Buying AI credits](/docs/ai/buying-ai-credits).

## What the AI never sees

Member names, phone numbers and emails are never sent to the AI provider. Members appear to it as "Member 1", "Member 2" and so on, and SocietyLedger puts the real names back in the answer you read. The provider is also asked not to keep what it is sent. The figures come from the same calculations as Reports and Accounting.

## What you can do

Avoid typing anything in a question that you would not want processed by an outside service. Names and contact details you type are replaced before they are sent.
