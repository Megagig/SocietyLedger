Every AI feature shares one daily budget, per association.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["AI Assistant", "Usage"]} /> to see where you stand.</Step>
<Step>**Daily Rate Limit** shows requests and tokens used against your limit, and when it resets.</Step>
<Step>Below that, **Total Requests**, **Total Tokens** and a usage-over-time chart break down activity by day and by feature.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/ai/limits-and-privacy-1.webp" alt="AI Usage page with Daily Rate Limit, total requests and tokens, and a usage chart" />

Worked example: the default daily budget is 100 requests and 500,000 tokens, resetting at midnight UTC. Every question to the Assistant, every Natural Language Query, every reminder refresh and every report generated counts against this same shared pool.

## Why it matters

A busy officer running several reports and queries in one session can use up the daily budget faster than expected — if a request fails with a limit message, it resets the next day, not on request.

## What you can do

Your question text and relevant association data (the context the AI needs to answer accurately — member names, transaction amounts, dates) are sent to the configured AI provider to generate a response. Avoid including anything in a free-text question you wouldn't want processed by a third-party AI service.
