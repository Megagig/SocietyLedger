## How it works

<Callout type="note" title="Coming soon, and clearly labelled as such">
Opening <MenuPath items={["AI Assistant", "Document Q&A"]} /> shows a "Coming Soon" notice, not a working feature — the app is upfront about this rather than presenting a broken form.
