When the starting period or a paid plan ends and isn't renewed, SocietyLedger pauses your association until a payment is made.

## How it works

A paid plan that reaches the end of its period gets a short **grace period** (3 days unless SocietyLedger has set a different number). During it everything keeps working, a banner tells admins the date access will pause, and the admin is emailed. After the grace period, your association is paused, and the server enforces that: it applies to pages that were already open and to anything that tries to use your data directly. A free trial has no grace period. A cancelled plan keeps working until the period you already paid for ends.

While an association is paused:

- Everyone in it, including officers and members, sees a notice instead of the app, on the web and in the mobile app. The notice says when it ended.
- Admins and officers who can manage billing can still open the subscription pages on the web, choose a plan and pay (**Resubscribe Now**), and reach help and support.
- The mobile app doesn't take payments. It tells admins to resubscribe on the web.
- Members are told to contact their administrator.
- Nothing is deleted: members, transactions, documents and settings are all kept.

Access comes back only when a payment is confirmed, or when SocietyLedger activates your subscription and records why. Choosing a plan or returning from a payment page on its own doesn't restore it.

## Why it matters

Renew before the date shown on the subscription page. You get reminders by email seven, three and one day before it ends. As soon as a payment is confirmed, access comes back for everyone.

## What you can do

Keep payments on schedule. See [Your starting period](/docs/plan-billing/free-trial) for how a new association's period ends, or [Cancelling and reactivating](/docs/plan-billing/cancelling-and-reactivating) if you want to stop a renewal cleanly.
