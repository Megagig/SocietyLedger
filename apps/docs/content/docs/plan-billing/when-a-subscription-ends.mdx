When the free trial or a paid plan ends and isn't renewed, SocietyLedger pauses your association until a payment is made.

## How it works

A paid plan that reaches the end of its period moves to **Past due** for a short grace period of 3 days, during which everything keeps working and the admin is emailed. If no payment arrives, it becomes **Expired**. A free trial goes straight to blocked when its last day passes. A cancelled plan keeps working until the period you already paid for ends.

While an association is paused:

- Everyone in it, including officers and members, sees a notice instead of the app, on the web and in the mobile app.
- Admins and officers who can manage billing can still open the subscription pages on the web to pay. The mobile app doesn't take payments; it tells admins to renew on the web.
- Members are told to contact their administrator.
- Nothing is deleted.

## Why it matters

Renew before the date shown on the subscription page. As soon as a payment is confirmed, access comes back for everyone.

## What you can do

Keep payments on schedule. See [Your free trial](/docs/plan-billing/free-trial) for how the trial ends, or [Cancelling and reactivating](/docs/plan-billing/cancelling-and-reactivating) if you want to stop a renewal cleanly.
