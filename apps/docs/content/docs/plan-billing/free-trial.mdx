A new association starts on a plan chosen by SocietyLedger, for a fixed period. You don't choose a plan or pay to start, unless the pricing page says you must pay first.

## How it works

The period starts the moment SocietyLedger approves your association's first admin, not when you sign up, so waiting for approval never uses up your days. SocietyLedger sets the length and the plan, and the pricing page always shows the current offer. It is one of three things:

- **A free trial.** You get everything the plan includes, and it is shown as a trial.
- **Complimentary access.** The same, recorded as free access rather than a trial.
- **Pay first.** There is no free time. You choose a plan and pay before you start.

The plan you are given is the one SocietyLedger is offering on the day you register. If the plan changes later, your association keeps what it started with.

You get an email when the period starts, telling you which plan you have and the date it ends. Seven, three and one day before it ends, the admin gets a reminder email, and officers see a banner in the app.

## When the period ends

Once it ends, your association is paused until a subscription payment is made: admins, officers and members are all blocked. Your data is safe, and everything comes back as soon as you subscribe. A free trial has no extra grace days. See [What happens when a subscription ends](/docs/plan-billing/when-a-subscription-ends).

## Why it matters

Choose a plan before the period ends so your members never lose access.
