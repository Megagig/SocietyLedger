Every new association starts with a free trial that includes every feature. You don't pay or choose a plan to start it.

## How it works

The trial starts the moment SocietyLedger approves your association's first admin, not when you sign up, so waiting for approval never uses up your days. It lasts 14 days by default (SocietyLedger can change this length) and gives you the full set of features.

Three days and one day before the trial ends, the admin gets an email, and officers see a reminder banner in the app and on the web.

## When the trial ends

Once the trial ends, your association is paused until a subscription payment is made: admins, officers and members are all blocked. Your data is safe, and everything comes back as soon as you subscribe. See [What happens when a subscription ends](/docs/plan-billing/when-a-subscription-ends).

## Why it matters

Choose a plan before the trial ends so your members never lose access. A trial is started once per association, so choosing a plan afterwards doesn't give a second trial.
