Choosing a plan takes you straight to checkout — there's no separate "add a subscription" step.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission, which only Administrators have by default.

## Choose a plan and pay

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Subscription", "Plans"]} />, pick **Monthly** or **Annual**, and select a plan.</Step>
<Step>On checkout, review the order summary, then choose **Paystack** or **Nomba**.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Pay ₦[amount]</UiLabel> — you're redirected to that provider's real hosted checkout page.</Step>
<Step>Complete payment there. You're returned to SocietyLedger, where the payment is verified automatically.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/plan-billing/subscribing-and-paying-1.webp" alt="Checkout page with order summary, Paystack and Nomba provider options, and a Pay button" />

## What happens next

Once verified, your subscription status becomes **Active**, your billing period starts, and the payment appears in your [billing history](/docs/plan-billing/billing-history).
