Cancelling doesn't end your subscription right away — it stops it from renewing at the end of the period you've already paid for.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission, which only Administrators have by default. Cancelling and reactivating only work on an **Active** (or **Past Due**) subscription — there's nothing to cancel during a trial, since a trial isn't a paid period yet.

## Cancel your subscription

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Subscription"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Cancel Subscription</UiLabel> and confirm.</Step>
</Steps>

## Reactivate before the period ends

<Steps>
<Step>Go back to <MenuPath items={["Subscription"]} /> any time before your current period ends.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Reactivate Subscription</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/plan-billing/billing-history-1.webp" alt="Subscription page with plan details and a Cancel Subscription button" />

<Callout type="note" title="Reactivating only works before the period actually ends">
Cancelling just flags the subscription not to renew — your plan keeps working until the period you already paid for finishes. Reactivating simply clears that flag. Once the period actually ends, there's no "undo" — see [What happens when a subscription ends](/docs/plan-billing/when-a-subscription-ends).
</Callout>

## What happens next

A cancelled subscription keeps working normally until its current period ends, then follows the same path as any other lapsed subscription.
