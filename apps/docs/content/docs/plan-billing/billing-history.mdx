Your payment history lives on the main Subscription page, not a separate screen.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Subscription"]} />.</Step>
<Step>**Payment History** lists your 5 most recent payments, each with its amount, date and status (Paid, Pending or Failed). A confirmed payment has a **Receipt** link.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/plan-billing/billing-history-1.webp" alt="Subscription page's Payment History section listing amount, date and status per payment" />

<Callout type="note" title="Receipts">
Select **Receipt** beside a confirmed payment to see what you paid for, the amount and the payment reference. Use **Print or save as PDF** to keep a copy for your own accounting. Payments that are still pending, failed, or being checked have no receipt.
</Callout>

## Why it matters

This is a quick reference for "did that payment actually go through," not a full billing ledger — it only shows your 5 most recent payments, with nothing older available.
