Three plans — Starter, Professional and Enterprise — set your member limit and which optional modules and features are available.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Subscription", "Plans"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Toggle between **Monthly** and **Annual** billing — annual saves 20%.</Step>
<Step>Compare member limits and included features across all three plans. See current pricing at [societyledger.com.ng/pricing](https://www.societyledger.com.ng/pricing).</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/plan-billing/plans-and-whats-included-1.webp" alt="Choose Your Plan page with Monthly/Annual toggle and three plan cards" />

Roughly, by plan: **Starter** (100 members, no custom branding, single admin), **Professional** (500 members, custom branding, multiple admins, priority support, advanced reporting), **Enterprise** (unlimited members, everything in Professional plus API access, custom integrations and a dedicated account manager — and the plan that unlocks Accounting and Cooperatives).

## Why it matters

Your plan determines your member limit and which modules are meant to be available — see [Plans & modules](/docs/core-concepts/plans-and-modules) for how modules map to plans.

## What you can do

- [Subscribing and paying](/docs/plan-billing/subscribing-and-paying) — choose a plan and pay.
- [Upgrading or downgrading](/docs/plan-billing/changing-your-plan) — change plans later.
