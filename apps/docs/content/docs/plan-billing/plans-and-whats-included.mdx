Each plan sets your member limit and which modules and features you can use. The plans on offer, their prices and what each includes are set by SocietyLedger and shown live on the plan page, so this page doesn't list them.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Subscription", "Plans"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Switch between **Monthly** and **Annually**. Every price on the page updates, and annual billing shows what it saves.</Step>
<Step>Compare plans in the cards and in the **Compare plans** table below them, which lists every module and the member limit for each plan.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/plan-billing/plans-and-whats-included-1.webp" alt="Choose Your Plan page with the Monthly and Annually toggle and the plan cards" />

The same prices appear on the website's pricing page and at checkout. If SocietyLedger changes a price, all three change together.

## Your plan keeps its terms

When you subscribe, you get the plan's terms as they are that day: the price, the modules, the features and the member limit. If SocietyLedger later changes the plan, **your subscription keeps what you bought**. Your price changes only if SocietyLedger moves your association to the new terms, and you are told by email before that happens. The subscription page shows which version of the plan you are on.

## Why it matters

Your plan decides what you can use. A module or feature that isn't in your plan is unavailable until you move to a plan that includes it, or add an [add-on](/docs/plan-billing/add-ons) that does.
