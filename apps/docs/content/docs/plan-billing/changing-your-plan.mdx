A higher plan starts straight away and you are credited for the unused part of what you paid. A cheaper plan starts when your current period ends, and nothing is charged now.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission, which only Administrators have by default.

## Change your plan

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Subscription", "Plans"]} /> and select a different plan, or switch between monthly and annual billing.</Step>
<Step>**Moving up** — checkout shows the new plan's price, minus a credit for the unused days of what you paid for the current period. You pay the difference and the new plan starts right away, with a fresh period from today.</Step>
<Step>**Moving down** — checkout shows that nothing is charged now and the date the change takes effect. Confirm to schedule it. Until then you keep your current plan.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/plan-billing/plans-and-whats-included-1.webp" alt="Choose Your Plan page used for both subscribing and changing plans" />

## What happens next

An upgrade applies once the payment is confirmed. A scheduled downgrade is shown on the subscription page; to cancel it, choose your current plan again. If you change your mind before it takes effect, nothing is lost.

If you have add-ons, the ones the new plan allows come with you and are re-priced for the new period with credit for their unused days. An add-on the new plan doesn't allow ends when the change takes effect.

A credit is based on what you actually paid. If your current period was free (a trial or complimentary access), there is nothing to credit.
