An upgrade charges you a prorated top-up right away; a downgrade changes your plan immediately too, even though SocietyLedger tells you otherwise.

## Before you start

You need the **edit settings** permission, which only Administrators have by default.

## Change your plan

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Subscription", "Plans"]} /> and select a different plan.</Step>
<Step>**Upgrading** — SocietyLedger calculates a prorated amount for the rest of your current period and takes you to a real payment page to pay it immediately.</Step>
<Step>**Downgrading** — SocietyLedger shows a message saying the change is "scheduled" for your current period's end date, with no payment required.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/plan-billing/plans-and-whats-included-1.webp" alt="Choose Your Plan page used for both subscribing and changing plans" />

<Callout type="danger" title="A downgrade is not actually scheduled — it applies immediately">
Confirmed live: right after downgrading, the subscription's plan had already changed — not at the stated future date. The "scheduled for [date]" message describes what was intended, not what the code does; there's no field anywhere that defers a plan change, so a downgrade takes effect the moment you confirm it, same as an upgrade.
</Callout>

## What happens next

An upgrade's new plan applies once payment is verified. A downgrade's new plan applies immediately, regardless of what the confirmation message says — plan for that if your association was relying on keeping a higher plan's features until the message's stated date.
