The in-app help centre is a searchable library of articles and FAQs your association's admins write — separate from (and shown instead of) this documentation site while you're inside the app. The step-by-step **User manual** is this documentation site, one tap away.

## Before you start

The help centre is open to every signed-in user — members, officers and admins alike. You'll only see articles meant for your role.

## Find an answer

### On the web

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Help & Support", "Help Center"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Search, or browse **Articles** and **FAQs** by category.</Step>
<Step>Open an article or FAQ to read it, and mark it helpful or not helpful.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/help-support/in-app-help-center-1.webp" alt="Help centre homepage with a search bar and top FAQs" />

### In the mobile app

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Profile", "Help & support"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Type in the search box, or use the **Featured articles**, **Browse by category** and **Popular questions** sections.</Step>
<Step>Open an article to read it, then tap **Yes** or **No** under **Was this helpful?**.</Step>
<Step>Tap **FAQs** to open every question grouped by category — tap a question to show its answer, and use the search box to narrow them down.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot pending="Help & support screen in the mobile app: search box, User manual / FAQs / Contact support tiles, and featured articles" />

## The User manual

Tap **User manual** (on the Help & support screen, or on your Profile) to open this documentation site in the in-app browser. The manual is the same on the web, where it's in the sidebar.

## Help for the screen you're on

Many screens have a **?** button at the top. Tap it to open the manual page for exactly that screen — for example, My Savings opens the page on depositing into your savings. Screens without a matching page don't show the button.

## What happens next

If the help centre doesn't answer your question, you can **Contact support** from the Help & support screen in the app, which opens a WhatsApp chat — see [Contacting support](/docs/help-support/contacting-support). To open a support ticket, see [Opening and following a support ticket](/docs/help-support/support-tickets); tickets are opened on the web for now.
