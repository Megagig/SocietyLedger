The in-app help centre is a searchable library of articles and FAQs your association's admins write — separate from (and shown instead of) this documentation site while you're inside the app.

## Before you start

The help centre is open to every signed-in user — members, officers and admins alike.

## Find an answer

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Help"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Search, or browse **Articles** and **FAQs** by category.</Step>
<Step>Open an article or FAQ to read it, and mark it helpful or not helpful.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/help-support/in-app-help-center-1.webp" alt="Help centre homepage with a search bar and top FAQs" />

## What happens next

If the help centre doesn't answer your question, select **Open a Ticket** — see [Opening and following a support ticket](/docs/help-support/support-tickets).
