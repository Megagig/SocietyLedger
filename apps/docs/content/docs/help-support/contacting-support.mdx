A [support ticket](/docs/help-support/support-tickets) is the most direct way to reach SocietyLedger from inside the app — it's tracked, and you can follow its status.

## How it works

You can also email **support@societyledger.com.ng** directly. SocietyLedger states a 24-hour response time for email support, with priority support available on Professional and Enterprise plans.

## Why it matters

A ticket keeps the conversation and its history in one place you can return to; email works the same way any other email does, without that built-in tracking.

## What you can do

- [Opening and following a support ticket](/docs/help-support/support-tickets) — the tracked option, recommended first.
