There are three places to turn for help, and the right one depends on what's wrong.

## Your association's admins — for anything about your own account

Questions about your dues, a payment that isn't showing, your membership or what your association's rules are belong with your association's admins and officers. They hold your records and can act on them. Open a [support ticket](/docs/help-support/support-tickets) — it's tracked, you can follow the replies, and you're notified when they answer.

## SocietyLedger's support team — for the platform itself (association admins)

If you're an association admin and the problem is with SocietyLedger rather than a member — billing and plans, a bug, a module that isn't working, a data correction, or a feature request — use **Contact SocietyLedger**. It sends a ticket to SocietyLedger's own support team, who answer from their own inbox. See [Handling support tickets](/docs/help-support/handling-support-tickets).

Your members never contact SocietyLedger this way, and SocietyLedger's team can't read a member's ticket to your association.

## A chat or an email — for problems with the app itself

If the app crashes, or you can't sign in before you've been approved, use **Contact support** on the Help & support screen in the mobile app, which opens a WhatsApp chat with SocietyLedger. You can also email **support@societyledger.com.ng**. SocietyLedger states a 24-hour response time for email support, with priority support on Professional and Enterprise plans.

## Why it matters

Sending a question to the team that can actually answer it gets it solved faster, and keeps your members' account details with the people who are meant to see them.
