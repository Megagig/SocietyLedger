A support ticket is a question you send to **your own association's admins and officers** — they hold your membership and payment records, so they're the ones who can look into it and act. It's for anything the help centre's articles and FAQs don't answer.

<Callout type="note" title="Who answers your ticket">
Tickets go to the admins of the association you belong to, not to SocietyLedger's own team. If something is wrong with the app itself — it crashes, or you can't sign in before you've been approved — use **Contact support** in the help centre instead, which opens a chat with SocietyLedger. If you're an association admin, see [Handling support tickets](/docs/help-support/handling-support-tickets) for answering your members and contacting SocietyLedger.
</Callout>

## Before you start

Every signed-in user can open a ticket and see their own. You can only ever see your own tickets — not other members'.

## Open a ticket

### On the web

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Help & Support", "My Tickets"]} /> and select <UiLabel>New Ticket</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Subject** and **Description**. As you type (20+ characters), relevant help articles may appear — worth checking before you submit, in case one already answers your question.</Step>
<Step>Set a **Category** (Access & Login, Payments, Reports, Members, Technical, Bug Report, Feature Request, Other) and **Priority** — both may be pre-filled automatically based on your description.</Step>
<Step>Optionally attach up to three files.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Submit</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/help-support/support-tickets-1.webp" alt="New Ticket form with subject, description, category and priority fields" />

### In the mobile app

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Profile", "Help & support", "My tickets"]} /> and tap **New ticket** (or **Open a new ticket** on the Help & support screen).</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Subject** (at least 5 characters) and describe the problem (at least 10). Suggested help articles appear under the description while you type.</Step>
<Step>Choose a **Category** and **Priority**. If the app guesses a category from your description, it fills it in — change it if it's wrong.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Attach files** to add up to three photos or PDFs, using the camera, your photos or your files.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Send ticket**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot pending="New ticket screen in the mobile app: subject, description, suggested articles, category, priority and attachments" />

## Attachments

Attach screenshots, receipts or documents so your association can see exactly what you see.

- Up to **3 files** per ticket or reply.
- **Photos or PDFs**, up to **5MB** each.
- If a file can't be uploaded, your ticket or reply is still sent — you'll be told which files didn't make it, and you can attach them again in a reply.

Tap a file on the ticket to open it. Only you and your association's admins can open files on your ticket.

## Follow a ticket

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Help & Support", "My Tickets"]} /> on the web, or <MenuPath items={["Profile", "Help & support", "My tickets"]} /> in the app, and open yours from the list.</Step>
<Step>Read the replies and add a message any time to continue the conversation — you can attach files to a reply too.</Step>
</Steps>

In the app, **Open** tickets are listed first and **Closed** ones are on their own tab. A ticket marked **Waiting on you** is waiting for your reply.

<Screenshot pending="Ticket thread in the mobile app: the conversation, a reply box with an attach button, and Close this ticket" />

## Close or reopen a ticket

If your question is sorted, close the ticket. If you still need help later, reopen a resolved or closed ticket and it goes back to your association's admins. You can only close and reopen your own tickets.

## Get notified

You're told when your association replies to your ticket — an in-app notification, a push notification in the app (which opens the ticket), and an email. Your association's admins are told the same way when a ticket arrives or you reply. Which of these you receive follows your notification preferences.

<Callout type="note" title="Article suggestions and auto-categorization are AI-assisted">
Both only appear when the AI service is configured and available — without it, the form still works normally, you just fill in category and priority yourself.
</Callout>
