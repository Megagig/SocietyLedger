A support ticket reaches SocietyLedger directly — for anything the help centre's articles and FAQs don't answer.

## Before you start

Every signed-in user can open a ticket.

## Open a ticket

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Help", "Tickets"]} /> and select <UiLabel>New Ticket</UiLabel>.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Subject** and **Description**. As you type (20+ characters), relevant help articles may appear — worth checking before you submit, in case one already answers your question.</Step>
<Step>Set a **Category** (Access & Login, Payments, Reports, Members, Technical, Bug Report, Feature Request, Other) and **Priority** — both may be pre-filled automatically based on your description.</Step>
<Step>Select <UiLabel>Submit</UiLabel>.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/help-support/support-tickets-1.webp" alt="New Ticket form with subject, description, category and priority fields" />

## Follow a ticket

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Help", "Tickets"]} /> and open yours from the list.</Step>
<Step>Add a message any time to continue the conversation.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="Article suggestions and auto-categorization are AI-assisted">
Both only appear when the AI service is configured and available — without it, the form still works normally, you just fill in category and priority yourself.
</Callout>

## What happens next

Your ticket gets a reference number and a status (Open, In Progress, Waiting on You, Waiting on Staff, Resolved, Closed) you can track from the ticket list.
