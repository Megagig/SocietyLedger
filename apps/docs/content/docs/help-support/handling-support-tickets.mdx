Members' support tickets come to your association's admins and officers. This page covers answering them, and what to do when the problem needs SocietyLedger's help.

## Before you start

You need the support permissions that come with the admin role: viewing all tickets, responding and managing them. Contacting SocietyLedger needs a separate permission, held by admins.

## Answer a member's ticket

### On the web

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Help & Support", "My Tickets"]} />. You see every member ticket in your association.</Step>
<Step>Open a ticket and read the conversation.</Step>
<Step>Write your reply and select <UiLabel>Send Response</UiLabel>. Tick **Internal note** first if it's for your team — the member won't see it.</Step>
<Step>Use **Move to** to change the status, or **Assign to me** to take the ticket. Select **Suggest a reply** for a draft you can edit, when the AI service is available.</Step>
</Steps>

### In the mobile app

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Support tickets"]} />. **Need a reply** counts the tickets waiting on you.</Step>
<Step>Open a ticket, then choose **Reply to member** or **Internal note** at the bottom.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Send reply**. Use **Assign to me** or the status buttons (such as **Resolve**) above the conversation, and **Suggest a reply** for a draft.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot pending="Support tickets in the mobile app: a member ticket with the reply box, the Reply to member / Internal note switch and the status buttons" />

The member is notified of every reply — an in-app notification, a push notification and an email. Internal notes notify no one.

## Contact SocietyLedger support

When the problem is with the platform — billing, a bug, a module, a data correction or a feature request — send SocietyLedger a ticket of your own.

<Steps>
<Step>On the web, go to <MenuPath items={["Help & Support", "My Tickets"]} />, switch to **Contact SocietyLedger**, then select **Contact SocietyLedger**. In the app, go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Support tickets", "To SocietyLedger"]} /> and tap **Contact SocietyLedger**.</Step>
<Step>Choose a category (Access & Login, Billing, Data, Technical, Bug Report, Feature Request or Other), describe the problem, and optionally attach up to three files.</Step>
<Step>Send it. SocietyLedger's team answers on the same ticket and you're notified of each reply.</Step>
</Steps>

Only you can see a ticket you send to SocietyLedger — other admins in your association don't.

## Escalate a member's ticket

If you can't solve a member's problem yourself, you can pass it on without sharing the member's ticket.

<Steps>
<Step>Open the member's ticket and select **Escalate to SocietyLedger** (on the web, next to **Close Ticket**; in the app, below the status buttons).</Step>
<Step>Write the subject and a summary **in your own words**, choose a category and priority, and attach files if they help.</Step>
<Step>Send it. A new ticket opens with SocietyLedger, and a staff-only note on the member's ticket records that it was escalated.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="What SocietyLedger sees">
Only what you write in the escalation. They never see the member's ticket, name or messages, and the member isn't told you escalated it.
</Callout>

## What happens next

You follow an escalated or direct ticket under **Contact SocietyLedger** — the same screen as a member's ticket thread. You can reply, attach files, close and reopen it, and you're notified when SocietyLedger's team replies or marks it resolved.
