Attendance History rolls up every meeting's attendance into one rate per member, so you can see at a glance who attends regularly and who doesn't.

## How it works

<Screenshot src="/images/meetings/attendance-history-1.webp" alt="Attendance History page with Total Members, Avg Attendance, High Attendance and Low Attendance stat cards, and a per-member table" />

Each member's row shows their total meetings, how many they attended, how many they missed, apologies, times late, and an overall **attendance rate** — coloured green at 80% and above, yellow from 60–79%, and red below 60%. Search narrows the table by name.

## Why it matters

A low attendance rate is worth a conversation before it becomes a pattern — especially ahead of an AGM or a vote, where quorum depends on who actually shows up.
