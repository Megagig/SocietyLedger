Schedule a regular meeting, an AGM, or an emergency meeting, and set where and when it happens.

## Before you start

You need the **Administrator** role — scheduling, editing and cancelling meetings is Administrator-only today, regardless of any other officer role a member holds. An association that wants to delegate this to a Secretary can create a [custom role](/docs/settings/custom-roles) with the same permission.

## Schedule the meeting

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Governance", "Meetings"]} /> and select **Schedule Meeting**.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Meeting Title** and choose a **Meeting Type** — Regular, Emergency, AGM, EGM, Committee or Special.</Step>
<Step>Optionally set **Quorum Required** — the minimum number of members who must attend for the meeting to count as valid.</Step>
<Step>Set the **Date** and **Start Time** — **End Time** is optional.</Step>
<Step>Add a **Physical Location**, a **Virtual Meeting Link**, or both.</Step>
<Step>Select **Schedule Meeting**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="Add the agenda while you're here">
The form on this page also has an **Agenda Items** section — see [Building the agenda](/docs/meetings/building-an-agenda) to fill it in before you save, or add items later from the meeting's own page.
</Callout>

## What happens next

The meeting appears as **Scheduled** for every member of the association, with an in-app notification and an email sent to everyone. It stays **Scheduled** until you cancel it or a reminder fires 24 hours beforehand — there's currently no way to mark a meeting as held or completed from this screen; see [Cancelling or postponing a meeting](/docs/meetings/cancelling-or-postponing) if plans change.

## In the app

Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Meetings"]} /> and tap **+**. Choose the kind of meeting, the start (date and time are separate taps), an optional end, the venue or online link, and the quorum if you have one. The end must be after the start. Members are told when you schedule it.

## Meetings become "Held" by themselves

Once a meeting's end time has passed — or three hours after it started, if you didn't set an end — it moves from **Upcoming** to **Held** automatically, so your recent-meetings list stays right. You can also mark a meeting as held yourself from its **⋮** menu.
