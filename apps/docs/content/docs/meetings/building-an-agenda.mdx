Add agenda items — each with its own title, presenter and time estimate — while you're scheduling a meeting.

## Before you start

<Callout type="warning" title="Agenda items can only be added while scheduling">
There's currently no way to add, edit or remove agenda items after a meeting has been scheduled — build the full agenda on the [Schedule Meeting](/docs/meetings/scheduling-a-meeting) form before you save it. If you missed something, cancel the meeting and reschedule it with the complete agenda.
</Callout>

## Add agenda items

<Steps>
<Step>On the Schedule Meeting form, scroll to **Agenda Items** and select **Add Item**.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **title** for the item (required) — for example, "Financial Report" or "Election of Officers".</Step>
<Step>Optionally add a **presenter name**, a **description**, and a **duration** in minutes.</Step>
<Step>Select **Add Item** again for each additional item — they're numbered in the order you add them.</Step>
<Step>Select the trash icon on an item to remove it before saving.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/meetings/building-an-agenda-1.webp" alt="Agenda Items section of the Schedule Meeting form with two items added, each showing title, presenter and duration fields" />

## What happens next

The agenda is saved with the meeting and shown to every member on the meeting's detail page, in the order you set. There's no separate approval step — whatever you save is what members see.
