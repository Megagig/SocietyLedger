Once a meeting is scheduled, no further action is needed to remind members — it happens on its own.

## How it works

| Event | Who's notified | How |
|---|---|---|
| A meeting is scheduled | Every active member | In-app notification, immediately |
| 24 hours before start | Every active member with an email | In-app notification and email |
| A meeting is cancelled | Every active member with an email | In-app notification and email |

A background check runs every hour looking for scheduled meetings starting within the next 24 hours that haven't had a reminder sent yet. Each qualifying meeting gets exactly one reminder — it's marked as sent so it doesn't go out twice, even if the hourly check runs again before the meeting starts.

## Why it matters

Reminders only go out for meetings that are still **Scheduled** — a meeting that's cancelled before the 24-hour mark never triggers one, so members aren't reminded about a meeting that isn't happening.

<Callout type="note" title="Reminders depend on a current email address">
A member without an email on file still gets the in-app notification, but not the email reminder — keep member contact details current if reminders matter to your association.
</Callout>

## What you can do

- There's nothing to configure — reminders are automatic for every scheduled meeting.
- [Scheduling a meeting](/docs/meetings/scheduling-a-meeting) — set the date and time reminders are timed from.
