Mark attendance for a meeting's members, one at a time or all at once. The page works on a phone's browser, so you can take attendance from the floor of the meeting itself.

## Before you start

You need the **manage attendance** permission, which Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries have by default. Attendance can be taken before, during or after a meeting — but not for a **cancelled** meeting, which never took place. Only members of your association can be marked.

## Take attendance

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Open the meeting from <MenuPath items={["Governance", "Meetings"]} /> and select the **Attendance** tab.</Step>
<Step>The first time, select **Initialize Attendance** to create a **Pending** record for every active member.</Step>
<Step>For each member, use the status dropdown to mark them **Present**, **Late**, **Absent** or **Apology**.</Step>
<Step>Select **Mark All Present** to set everyone still **Pending** to Present in one action, then adjust the exceptions individually.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Meetings"]} />, open the meeting, then tap **Take attendance**.</Step>
<Step>The first time, tap **Start the attendance list** to add every active member as *not marked*.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Present**, **Absent**, **Excused** or **Late** beside each name. The tap shows at once, and if it can't be saved it is put back and you are told.</Step>
<Step>Search by name or member ID, and tap **Mark the not marked as present** to mark everyone you haven't touched in one go. Anyone you already marked is left as you set them.</Step>
</Steps>

The counts at the top (Present, Late, Not marked) and the quorum line follow every tap.
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

<Callout type="note" title="Check-in time and notes aren't set from this screen">
The attendance table has Check-in Time and Notes columns, but there's no field on this page to enter them — they stay blank unless set some other way.
</Callout>

## What happens next

Each member gets a notification that their attendance was recorded. If the meeting has a **Quorum Required**, the Details tab shows whether quorum has been met based on everyone marked Present or Late. The numbers also feed [Attendance history](/docs/meetings/attendance-history).
