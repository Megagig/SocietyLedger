Record what was discussed, the decisions made and the action items from a meeting, then approve the minutes to lock them in as the official record.

## Before you start

Writing and approving minutes needs the **manage minutes** permission (Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries have it by default). **A different officer has to approve the minutes than the one who wrote them.**

<Callout type="danger" title="Approving locks the minutes">
Once minutes are approved they are locked — they can't be edited by anyone. Review carefully before you approve. You can't approve minutes you wrote yourself.
</Callout>

## Record minutes

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Open the meeting and select the **Minutes** tab.</Step>
<Step>Fill in **Minutes Content** — a summary of what was discussed.</Step>
<Step>Fill in **Key Decisions** — the decisions made and any votes taken.</Step>
<Step>Fill in **Action Items** — what needs to happen next, and who's responsible.</Step>
<Step>Select **Save Minutes**. You can keep editing and re-saving until they are approved.</Step>
<Step>When the minutes are ready to stand as the record, ask another officer to select **Approve Minutes**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Meetings"]} />, open the meeting and tap **Minutes**.</Step>
<Step>Write what was discussed, the decisions and the action items, then tap **Save minutes**.</Step>
<Step>Another officer opens the same screen, reads them through and taps **Approve**. The button doesn't show for the officer who wrote them.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## What happens next

Once approved, the minutes show an **Approved** badge along with who recorded and who approved them. There's no separate "next meeting date" field to schedule a follow-up — record that as an action item, or schedule the next meeting from [Scheduling a meeting](/docs/meetings/scheduling-a-meeting).
