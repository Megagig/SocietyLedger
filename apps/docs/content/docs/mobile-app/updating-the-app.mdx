New features and fixes reach the app regularly. Running the latest version also keeps notifications and sign-in working properly.

## Check your version

Go to <MenuPath items={["Profile", "About"]} />. The **Version** is shown there, along with the privacy policy and terms.

## Update

<Steps>
<Step>Open the app store you installed from — TestFlight on iPhone, or Google Play on Android.</Step>
<Step>Find **SocietyLedger** and tap **Update** if one is available. Turning on automatic updates in the store means you don't have to remember.</Step>
<Step>Open the app. Some smaller fixes can also be delivered automatically the next time you open it, so a new version number isn't always needed.</Step>
</Steps>

## What's new

Notable changes are listed on [What's new](/docs/reference/release-notes), newest first.

## If something looks wrong after an update

Close the app fully and open it again. If it still misbehaves, [contact support](/docs/help-support/contacting-support) and mention your version number.
