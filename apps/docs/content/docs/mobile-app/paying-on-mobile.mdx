Everything you can pay on the web you can pay in the app.

## Before you start

You need to be signed in and have something outstanding. Your balance and what's due are on **Home** and on the **Money** tab.

## Pay a due, levy or penalty

<Steps>
<Step>Open <MenuPath items={["Money", "My Dues"]} /> (or **My Levies**, **My Penalties**), choose the item and tap **Pay**. You can also tap **Pay** on Home.</Step>
<Step>**Online:** choose **Pay with Paystack**. You're taken to Paystack's secure checkout, where cards, bank transfer and USSD are accepted. When you return, the payment shows in **Payment History**.</Step>
<Step>**Bank transfer:** choose **Bank transfer**, send the money to one of the listed accounts under **Transfer to**, then attach your receipt (a screenshot or your bank's debit alert) and tap **Submit payment proof**. An officer checks it and you're told the result.</Step>
</Steps>

If your association hasn't turned on online payments you only see bank transfer. If there's no bank account listed, ask your admin. See [Paying online](/docs/member-guide/paying-online) and [Paying by bank transfer](/docs/member-guide/paying-by-bank-transfer) for more.

## See what you've paid

<MenuPath items={["Money", "Payment History"]} /> lists your payments; open one for its receipt. <MenuPath items={["Money", "Statement"]} /> is your full statement and <MenuPath items={["Money", "My Ledger"]} /> your ledger.

## Savings and loans

- **Savings** — <MenuPath items={["Money", "My Savings"]} />: see your accounts and make a deposit with a receipt. See [Depositing into your savings](/docs/cooperatives/depositing-into-your-savings).
- **Loans** — <MenuPath items={["Money", "My Loans"]} />: apply, track and repay. See [Applying for a loan](/docs/cooperatives/applying-for-a-loan) and [Paying your loan](/docs/cooperatives/paying-your-loan).
- **Guarantor requests** — if someone names you as a guarantor, you're notified and can accept or decline under <MenuPath items={["Money", "Guarantor Requests"]} />. See [Guarantors](/docs/cooperatives/guarantors).

## What happens next

Transfers and deposits are confirmed by an officer — you get a notification when they decide.
