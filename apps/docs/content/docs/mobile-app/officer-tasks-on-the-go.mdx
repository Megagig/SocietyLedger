The app is built mainly for members. Officers get a **Manage** tab for the parts that work well on a phone, and the rest stays on the web.

## Before you start

The **Manage** tab only appears if your role includes a management permission. If you've just been given a role, close and reopen the app.

## Answer support tickets

<Steps>
<Step>Open <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Support tickets"]} />. **Need a reply** counts the tickets waiting on you; switch between **Member tickets** and **To SocietyLedger**.</Step>
<Step>Open a ticket and choose **Reply to member** or **Internal note**, then send. Use **Assign to me**, the status buttons, **Suggest a reply** and **Escalate to SocietyLedger** as you need.</Step>
</Steps>

Full details are in [Handling support tickets](/docs/help-support/handling-support-tickets).

## Get alerted to what needs confirming

When a member submits a savings deposit, a loan repayment or a loan application, treasurers, financial secretaries and admins get a notification. Tapping it opens the **Manage** tab; **confirming or rejecting it is done on the web** for now — [Approving savings deposits](/docs/cooperatives/approving-savings-deposits), [Approving loan repayments](/docs/cooperatives/approving-loan-repayments) and [Reviewing and disbursing loans](/docs/cooperatives/reviewing-and-disbursing-loans).

## Everything else

Verifying payment proofs, taking attendance, members, finance and reports are on the web today. See [What you still do on the web](/docs/mobile-app/whats-web-only).
