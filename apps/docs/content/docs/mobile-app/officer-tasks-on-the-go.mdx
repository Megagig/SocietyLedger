The app gives officers a **Manage** tab for the everyday jobs, so you can deal with them wherever you are. A few jobs are still easier at a desk — those are listed at the end.

## Before you start

The **Manage** tab only appears if your role includes a management permission, and each section only shows what your role allows. If you've just been given a role, close and reopen the app.

## See what needs you

On **Home**, switch to **Association**. **Needs your attention** lists the work waiting on you — members to approve, payment proofs to verify, bank transfers to confirm, cash lodgments to check, overdue charges and tickets that need a reply. Tap a line to open it. The same counts appear as badges in **Manage**. See [The association dashboard](/docs/reports/association-dashboard).

## Members

Open <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Members"]} /> to search and filter your members, open a profile, call, message or email them, add or edit a member, and email login invitations. **Pending approvals** is where people who registered themselves wait for you. See [Your member list](/docs/members/your-member-list), [Adding a member](/docs/members/adding-a-member) and [Self-registration and approvals](/docs/members/self-registration-and-approvals).

## Payments

Open <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Payments"]} />.

- **Payment proofs** — open a member's receipt, zoom in to read it, then verify it or reject it with a reason. See [Verifying payment proofs](/docs/payments/verifying-payment-proofs).
- **Bank transfers** — record, edit, verify or reject transfers. You can't verify a transfer you recorded yourself. See [Recording bank transfers](/docs/payments/recording-bank-transfers).
- **Bank accounts** — set the accounts members pay into. See [Your association's bank accounts](/docs/payments/association-bank-accounts).

## Finance

Open <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Finance"]} /> for collections and what's outstanding. From there you can create charges for one member or many, record a payment, edit, void and replace (you'll be asked for your password) or delete a charge, send reminders to members who are overdue, and open any member's ledger and share their statement. See [Finance overview](/docs/finance/finance-overview), [Recording a payment](/docs/finance/recording-a-payment), [Overdue and reminders](/docs/finance/overdue-and-reminders) and [Member ledgers](/docs/finance/member-ledgers).

## Meetings

Open <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Meetings"]} /> to schedule a meeting, build its agenda, take attendance live, write and approve minutes, cancel a meeting, and see every member's attendance record. See [Taking attendance](/docs/meetings/taking-attendance) and [Minutes](/docs/meetings/minutes).

## Cash

Open <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Cash"]} /> to take cash from members at a meeting, close the collection, lodge it into the bank with a photo of the slip, and check other officers' lodgments. See [Cash collections](/docs/cash/how-cash-collections-work).

## Support tickets

<Steps>
<Step>Open <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Support tickets"]} />. **Need a reply** counts the tickets waiting on you; switch between **Member tickets** and **To SocietyLedger**.</Step>
<Step>Open a ticket and choose **Reply to member** or **Internal note**, then send. Use **Assign to me**, the status buttons, **Suggest a reply** and **Escalate to SocietyLedger** as you need.</Step>
</Steps>

Full details are in [Handling support tickets](/docs/help-support/handling-support-tickets).

## What's still on the web

Loan, savings and welfare approvals, accounting, reports, communications and settings are done on the web for now — see [What you still do on the web](/docs/mobile-app/whats-web-only). You still get a notification when something needs confirming; tapping it opens the **Manage** tab.
