Push notifications tell you about things as they happen, even when the app is closed. The same notifications always appear in your inbox under the **bell**.

## Before you start

The app asks for permission to send notifications after you've started using it — not on your very first launch. If you said no, turn them on in your phone's settings for SocietyLedger.

## What you're notified about

- Payments received and confirmed, dues reminders and overdue notices, new levies and penalties.
- Meetings scheduled, reminded or cancelled; elections, polls and announcements.
- Savings: whether your deposit was approved or not.
- Loans: your application's progress, repayments confirmed, and guarantor requests (also sent by email).
- Support: replies to your tickets.
- For admins and officers: a new support ticket or member reply, and when a member submits a savings deposit, a loan repayment or a loan application for you to confirm.

## Open what a notification is about

Tap a notification — on your lock screen or in the inbox — to go straight to it: your savings, your loans, the guarantor request, the support ticket, or the announcement. Notifications that don't point anywhere just open the inbox. The number on the app's icon is your unread count.

## Choose what you get

Which events notify you, and by which channel, is set on the web under <MenuPath items={["Notifications", "Preferences"]} /> — see [Choosing which notifications you get](/docs/member-guide/notification-preferences). On Android you can also silence a whole group — **Payments**, **Governance** or **Announcements** — in the phone's notification settings.

## If you don't receive them

- Check notifications are allowed for SocietyLedger in your phone's settings.
- Check you haven't switched that type off in your preferences.
- Open the bell: the notification is there even if the push didn't arrive.
