The app keeps a copy of what you've recently looked at, so a weak or missing connection doesn't leave you with a blank screen.

## What you see offline

When the app can't reach the internet it shows a banner — **You're offline. Showing saved data from …** — and displays the last information it loaded. Saved copies cover your profile, balance and finances, ledger, meetings, voting and polls, and your notifications.

## What needs a connection

Anything that sends something to your association: paying, submitting a deposit or repayment, applying for a loan, accepting a guarantor request, voting, and sending or replying to a support ticket. If one fails because you're offline, nothing is lost — reconnect and try again.

## Tips

- Pull down on a screen to refresh once you're back online.
- Saved data can be out of date. Check the date in the banner before relying on a balance.

## If a screen says it can't load

Online but still seeing a "couldn't load" message? Pull down to retry. If a section says it's **locked**, your association's plan doesn't include it — ask your admin.
