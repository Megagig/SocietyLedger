The bar along the bottom of the app has up to five tabs. The **bell** at the top of most screens opens your notifications.

## Home

A summary of where you stand: your balance and what's next due, quick actions (**Pay**, **Upload proof**, **Statement**, **Savings**, **Apply for a loan** and **Support**), recent payments, the next meeting, an open vote, and the latest announcements.

## Money

Your **Ledger balance**, and everything financial:

- **Finances** — **My Ledger**, **Payment History** and **Statement**.
- **Cooperative** — **My Savings**, **My Loans** and, when you've been named a guarantor, **Guarantor Requests**.
- **Breakdown** — **My Dues**, **My Levies**, **My Penalties** and **My Donations**.

## Community

**Meetings**, **Voting**, **Polls**, **Welfare** and **Announcements**.

## Manage

Only appears if your role lets you manage something. Today it holds **Support tickets** for answering members — see [Officer tasks on the go](/docs/mobile-app/officer-tasks-on-the-go). If you've just been given a role and don't see the tab, close and reopen the app.

## Profile

**My profile**, **Notifications**, **Appearance** (light, dark or match your phone), **Help & support**, **Security**, **Switch association**, **About** (the app version) and **Sign out**.

## Things you'll notice

- A **?** on a screen opens the matching page of this manual.
- A section your association's plan doesn't include shows a **locked** message instead of loading. Ask your admin about upgrading.
- Offline? See [Using the app with poor or no internet](/docs/mobile-app/offline-and-saved-data).
