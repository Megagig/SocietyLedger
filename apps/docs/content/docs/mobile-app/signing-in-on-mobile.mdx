You sign in to the app the same way you do on the web, after telling it which association you belong to.

## Before you start

You need your association code — the first part of your association's web address — and either an existing account (email and password) or the details to create one.

## Sign in

<Steps>
<Step>On the welcome screen, tap **Get started**.</Step>
<Step>Enter your **Association code**. Associations you've used before are listed under **Recent associations** so you can tap one instead of typing.</Step>
<Step>Enter your **Email** and **Password** and tap **Sign in**. Forgotten it? Tap **Forgot password?** and we'll email you a reset link.</Step>
<Step>The first time, the app offers **Unlock faster next time?** — tap **Enable** to open the app with Face ID or your fingerprint, or **Not now**.</Step>
</Steps>

## Create an account

<Steps>
<Step>After choosing your association, tap **Create an account**.</Step>
<Step>Enter your first and last name, email and password, confirm the password and tick that you agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, then tap **Create account**.</Step>
<Step>Enter the 6-digit code we email you on the **Verify your email** screen. Use **Resend code** if it doesn't arrive.</Step>
<Step>An admin may need to approve your account. Until they do you see **Your account is pending approval** — you'll get an email when it's done, and **Check again** refreshes the screen.</Step>
</Steps>

## If you can't get in

- **We couldn't find an association with that code** — check the spelling against your association's web address.
- **Account suspended or not approved**, or **association suspended** — the screen says which; contact your association's administrator.
- **Too many attempts** — wait a minute and try again.
- **Platform staff sign in separately** — SocietyLedger staff use the admin website, not this app.

## Lock and security

Go to <MenuPath items={["Profile", "Security"]} /> to turn **Unlock with Face ID / fingerprint** on or off, choose how long the app waits before locking (**Lock after** 1, 5, 15 or 30 minutes), change your password, and see or sign out the devices that are signed in to your account. If you have no passcode or biometrics set up on your phone, set one up first. On a locked app, tap **Unlock**, or **Sign out** to leave.

To use another association, go to <MenuPath items={["Profile", "Switch association"]} />.

## What happens next

Once you're in, you land on **Home**. See [Finding your way around](/docs/mobile-app/finding-your-way-around).
