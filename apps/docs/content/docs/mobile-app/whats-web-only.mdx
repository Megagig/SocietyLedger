For members, the app covers almost everything. Officers can now do most day-to-day jobs in the app too. What's left is done at your association's web address.

## For members

- **Notification preferences** — choosing which events notify you and by which channel is on the web under <MenuPath items={["Notifications", "Preferences"]} />.
- **Creating an association** — sign up on the web at societyledger.com.ng.

## For admins and officers

Done on the web today:

- Confirming or rejecting savings deposits, loan repayments and loan applications, and disbursing loans.
- Welfare funds and payout requests.
- Loan products and cooperative member profiles.
- Accounting, reports and exports, importing members and bank statements, and matching bank transfers to charges (reconciliation).
- Roles and permissions.
- Running elections and polls.
- Sending communications and SMS, buying SMS credits.
- Settings, subscription and plan, webhooks and the AI assistant.
- Rebuilding every member's ledger at once, and permanently deleting a charge that already has payments.

You can already do these in the app — see [Officer tasks on the go](/docs/mobile-app/officer-tasks-on-the-go): members, payment proofs, bank transfers and bank accounts, finance and member ledgers, overdue reminders, meetings and attendance, cash collections and support tickets.

## For SocietyLedger staff

Platform staff sign in at the admin website, not in the app.

## Why it matters

The remaining tasks work best with a large screen and keyboard. If you're an officer and need one of them while away from a computer, your phone's web browser can open your association's website.
