For members, the app covers almost everything. Most of what's missing is the officer side of the association, which is done at your association's web address.

## For members

- **Notification preferences** — choosing which events notify you and by which channel is on the web under <MenuPath items={["Notifications", "Preferences"]} />.
- **Creating an association** — sign up on the web at societyledger.com.ng.

## For admins and officers

Done on the web today:

- Confirming or rejecting savings deposits, loan repayments and loan applications, and disbursing loans.
- Loan products and cooperative member profiles.
- Verifying payment proofs and bank transfers, finance, accounting, reports and exports.
- Members: adding, importing, roles and permissions.
- Creating meetings, taking attendance, running elections and polls, welfare funds.
- Sending communications and SMS, buying SMS credits.
- Settings, subscription and plan, webhooks and the AI assistant.

In the app you can answer support tickets — see [Officer tasks on the go](/docs/mobile-app/officer-tasks-on-the-go).

## For SocietyLedger staff

Platform staff sign in at the admin website, not in the app.

## Why it matters

These tasks work best with a large screen and keyboard. If you're an officer and need one of them while away from a computer, your phone's web browser can open your association's website.
