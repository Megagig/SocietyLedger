SocietyLedger has an app for iPhone and Android that covers what members do day to day — paying, savings and loans, meetings and voting, announcements and support — plus the support desk for association admins and officers.

## Before you start

You need a phone with an internet connection and your association's **association code** — the first part of its web address. For `opuogunstate.societyledger.com.ng`, the code is `opuogunstate`. Ask your association's admin if you don't know it.

## Install the app

<Steps>
<Step>Open the install link your association or SocietyLedger sent you. While the app is being rolled out, installation is by invitation — on iPhone through TestFlight, on Android through Google Play's testing programme.</Step>
<Step>Install **SocietyLedger** and open it.</Step>
<Step>Continue with [Signing in with your association code](/docs/mobile-app/signing-in-on-mobile).</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="Not invited yet?">
Ask your association's admin for the install link. Everything in the app is also available on the web at your association's own address, so you're never blocked while you wait.
</Callout>

## What happens next

The first time you open the app you see a short welcome, then you choose your association and sign in.
