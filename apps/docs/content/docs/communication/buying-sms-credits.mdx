Each SMS segment costs 1 credit. Your balance is shown on the SMS dashboard and on the Reports page.

## How it works

Selecting **Top Up Credits** on the SMS dashboard takes you to <MenuPath items={["Communication", "SMS Reports"]} />, which shows your balance and usage, and has a top-up form: an amount and a payment method (Paystack or Bank Transfer).

<Screenshot src="/images/communication/buying-sms-credits-1.webp" alt="SMS Reports page with a credit balance card, usage stats, and a top-up amount and payment method form" />

<Callout type="danger" title="Top-up doesn't actually charge anything yet">
Submitting the top-up form adds the credits to your balance immediately, regardless of which payment method you pick — no payment is actually taken. This needs to be wired to a real payment gateway before it can be trusted; until then, contact SocietyLedger support for SMS credit purchases rather than relying on this form.
</Callout>

## Why it matters

Don't rely on the in-app top-up for billing purposes while this is unresolved — track any credits added through it separately until a real payment gate is in place.
