Each part of a text costs one credit per person. Your balance shows on the SMS dashboard and at the top of the Communication screen on your phone.

## Before you start

You need the permission to manage billing. Credits can only be bought on the web, not in the mobile app.

## Buy credits

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Communication", "SMS Dashboard"]} /> and select **Buy credits**.</Step>
<Step>Choose a package, or enter an amount.</Step>
<Step>Pay on the Paystack page. You are brought back and the credits are added once the payment is confirmed.</Step>
</Steps>

Credits are added once per payment, even if you return to the page twice or the confirmation arrives twice. A failed or abandoned payment adds nothing.

## What happens next

Every purchase, use and refund shows in the credit history on <MenuPath items={["Communication", "SMS Reports"]} /> and in the app under **Texts and credits**. If you don't have enough credits for a send, the app tells you how many more you need before anything goes out.
