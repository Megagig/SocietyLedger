Officers can pick members with outstanding or overdue balances and send them a reminder.

## How it works

<MenuPath items={["Finance", "Reminders"]} /> lists every member with a pending or overdue transaction, with totals and an overdue count. Select one or more members, then **Send Reminders**, optionally with a custom message added on top of the standard reminder.

<Screenshot src="/images/communication/reminder-schedules-1.webp" alt="Payment Reminders page listing members with outstanding balances, a Send Reminders button, and summary stats" />

<Callout type="danger" title="Sending a reminder doesn't email anyone">
Selecting members and sending shows a success screen with "sent" counts — but no email is actually sent. The server only logs the reminder to its own console and records it in the audit log; a member with an outstanding balance receives nothing. This is logged for a fix in a later phase — right now, the only way to actually remind a member is a direct conversation, or an [announcement](/docs/communication/announcements) if the group is broad enough.
</Callout>

<Callout type="note" title="This is manual, not scheduled">
Despite the name, there's no automatic or recurring reminder — an officer has to open this page and send reminders themselves each time.
</Callout>

## Why it matters

Until sending is fixed, don't rely on this page's "Sent" count as confirmation that a member was actually notified.
