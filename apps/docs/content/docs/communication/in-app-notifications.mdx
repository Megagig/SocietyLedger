Every account — officer or member — has a notifications centre. It's where payment-due reminders, payment-received confirmations, approval alerts, system messages and announcements all land.

## Before you start

Nothing special — every signed-in user has their own notifications.

## Use the notifications centre

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Notifications", "Notifications Center"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Use **Search**, the **Type** filter (Payment Due, Payment Received, System, Approval, Announcement) or the date range to narrow the list.</Step>
<Step>Select **Mark read** on a notification, or **Mark all read** to clear everything at once.</Step>
<Step>Select **Delete** to remove a notification, or tick several with the checkboxes and use the bulk actions bar that appears.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/communication/in-app-notifications-1.webp" alt="Notifications centre with a mix of system, payment and approval notifications, one unread" />

## What happens next

Unread notifications show a teal highlight; the count badge in the sidebar drops as you mark things read.

## Notification preferences

<MenuPath items={["Notifications", "Preferences"]} /> lets you toggle Email / SMS / In-App per notification type, and set quiet hours.

**Don't send me text messages** stops announcement and campaign texts from your officers. It is on the same Preferences screen, on the web and in the app. An officer texting you one to one can still reach you.

<Callout type="warning" title="What the toggles control">
Your choices are saved to your account and applied to payment, support-ticket, guarantor, savings-deposit and loan-repayment notifications: a type switched off for a channel isn't sent on it. Meeting reminders and platform announcements don't check them yet, and **quiet hours** are saved but not applied yet.
</Callout>
