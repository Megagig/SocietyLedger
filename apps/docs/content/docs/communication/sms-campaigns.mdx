A campaign sends one message to a group: all members, a category, chosen members, typed phone numbers, members who owe, or members who are paid up.

## Before you start

You need the **manage SMS campaigns** permission, the SMS module on your plan, and [SMS credits](/docs/communication/buying-sms-credits).

## Make and start a campaign

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Communication", "SMS Campaigns"]} /> and select **New Campaign**.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Name**, choose who it goes to, and write the message (or start from a template).</Step>
<Step>Optionally set a time to send. Select **Create**.</Step>
<Step>Open the campaign and select **Start**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Communication", "Campaigns"]} /> and tap **+**.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Name**, choose who gets it, write the message or start from a template, and optionally set a time.</Step>
<Step>Tap **Save campaign**, then open it and tap **Start campaign**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## Cost and reach come first

Before you save, and again before you start, the app shows how many people will get a text, how many have no usable number, how many opted out, and the cost against your balance. If you are short, the campaign can't be started until you [buy more credits](/docs/communication/buying-sms-credits).

## While it sends

- Credits are set aside when you start it, and each text is recorded before it goes out.
- A campaign can only be started once, even if you tap twice or two officers start it together. Duplicate numbers get one text.
- Big campaigns send in the background. The campaign shows progress and you can leave the screen.
- **Stop sending** (web: **Cancel**) keeps texts already sent and refunds the ones that haven't gone out.
- If the server restarts mid-send the campaign shows **Interrupted**. Credits for the texts that didn't go are refunded, so make a new campaign for the people who were missed.
- Only a **Draft** can be deleted.
