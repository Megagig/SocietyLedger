Quick Send texts a few members or phone numbers straight away. To text a whole group, use an [SMS campaign](/docs/communication/sms-campaigns) or an [announcement](/docs/communication/announcements).

## Before you start

- You need the **send SMS** permission, and the SMS module must be on your plan.
- You need [SMS credits](/docs/communication/buying-sms-credits). Each part of a text costs one credit.
- Your association's SMS provider must be switched on in Settings.

## Send a text

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Communication", "Quick Send SMS"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Choose the recipients: members, or phone numbers you type or paste.</Step>
<Step>Write the message, or pick a template.</Step>
<Step>Select **Send Message**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Communication", "Quick text"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Choose **Members** and tick them, or choose **Phone numbers** and type or paste them, separated by commas, spaces or new lines.</Step>
<Step>Write the message.</Step>
<Step>Check **Before you send**, tap **Send** and confirm.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## What you see before you send

Once you have recipients and a message, the app shows how many people will get the text, how many have no usable phone number, any typed numbers that can't be texted, and the cost: how many parts the message has, the credits it uses and your balance. If you don't have enough credits it says by how many you are short, and the Send button stays off. Credits are bought on the web.

<Callout type="note" title="Long messages cost more">
A plain text holds 160 characters. A longer one is sent in parts of 153 characters, and a message with emoji or some accented letters holds fewer (70 characters, then 67 per part). Each part is one credit per person. The preview works this out for you.
</Callout>

## What happens next

- Credits are set aside before anything is sent, and every text is recorded before it goes out.
- A text that fails is marked **Failed** and its credit is refunded. The message you get afterwards says how many went out and how many didn't. Nothing is reported as sent when it wasn't.
- A text shows **Delivered** only when the phone network says so. Until then it shows **Sent**.
- Members who turned on **Don't send me text messages** are skipped by announcements and campaigns. A text you send to a member on purpose, one to one, still goes.
- If the same number appears twice, it is texted once.

See every text, and every credit bought or used, in [SMS history](/docs/communication/sms-history).
