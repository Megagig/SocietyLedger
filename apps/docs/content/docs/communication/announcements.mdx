An announcement is one message to the members you choose. Members who have signed up get it in their notifications and as a push notification. You can also send it by email and as a text message, which is how members without a login are reached. Every announcement is kept, with who sent it, who it went to and what it cost.

## Before you start

You need the **send notifications** permission. The Administrator has it by default, and you can give it to other officer roles in Settings. Texts also need the SMS module on your plan and enough [SMS credits](/docs/communication/buying-sms-credits).

## Send an announcement

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Communication", "Announcements"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Title** (up to 200 characters) and your **Message** (up to 2,000 characters), and pick a **Priority**: Info, Warning or Urgent.</Step>
<Step>Under **Who should get it?**, choose **All active members**, **By category** (tick the categories) or **Chosen members** (search and tick them).</Step>
<Step>Tick **Also send by email** and, if you want texts, **Also send as a text message**. You can write shorter wording for the text; otherwise it uses the title and message.</Step>
<Step>Check **Who this reaches** on the right, then select **Send**.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Communication", "Announcement"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Title** and **Message**, and pick how important it is.</Step>
<Step>Under **Who gets it**, choose **Everyone**, **Category** (tick the categories) or **Chosen** (search and tick members).</Step>
<Step>Switch on **Also send by email** and, if you want texts, **Also send as a text message**.</Step>
<Step>Check **Who this reaches**, then tap **Send** and confirm.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## See who it reaches before you send

As you fill in the form, the app shows how many members the announcement reaches and who it misses: members with a login, members without one, how many have an email, how many will get a text, how many have no usable phone number, and how many chose not to receive texts. If you ticked texts it also shows what they cost against your balance.

<Callout type="note" title="An empty choice never means everyone">
If you pick **By category** or **Chosen members** and choose nobody, the announcement can't be sent. It is never treated as going to everyone.
</Callout>

## What happens next

- **In the app**: each member with a login gets the announcement in their notifications and as a push notification.
- **Members without a login** are reached only by email or text, if you ticked them. Without either, they are not reached, and the form tells you how many that is.
- **Texts** use your credits. The credits are set aside before anything is sent. A text that fails is refunded. If you don't have enough credits, nothing is sent and nothing is recorded, and you are told how many more you need. Members who turned on **Don't send me text messages** are skipped.
- A large announcement keeps sending texts in the background. Its row under **Sent announcements** shows the progress.

The record is kept under <MenuPath items={["Communication", "Announcements"]} /> on the web (the list below the form) and under **Manage**, **Communication**, **Sent announcements** on your phone.
