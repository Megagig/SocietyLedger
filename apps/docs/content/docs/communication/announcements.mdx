An announcement reaches every active member at once — it lands in each member's notification centre, and can also go out by email.

## Before you start

You need the **Administrator** role — announcements aren't available to other officer roles.

## Send an announcement

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Communication", "Announcements"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Enter a **Title** (up to 200 characters) and your **Message** (up to 2,000 characters).</Step>
<Step>Pick a **Priority** — Info, Warning or Urgent. This only changes the colour of the preview; it doesn't change delivery.</Step>
<Step>Tick **Also send via email** if you want members to receive it in their inbox too, not just in-app.</Step>
<Step>Select **Send Announcement**.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/communication/announcements-1.webp" alt="Send Announcement form with a title, message, priority selector set to Info, and the send-via-email checkbox" />

## What happens next

Every active member gets a new notification (title prefixed with 📢) in their [notifications centre](/docs/communication/in-app-notifications). If you ticked **Also send via email**, each member with an email on file also gets it by email. The announcement is recorded in the audit log with the recipient count.
