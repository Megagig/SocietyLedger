There's an **Email Campaigns** page in the Communication menu, but it isn't built yet.

## How it works

<Callout type="warning" title="This page is a placeholder">
Opening <MenuPath items={["Communication", "Email Campaigns"]} /> shows a dashboard with stats that are always zero. Selecting **Compose Email** or **New Campaign** opens a form, but submitting it doesn't send anything — it shows a "coming soon" message and points you to Announcements instead.
</Callout>

Email does genuinely go out today, just through two other features, not through this page:

- [Sending an announcement](/docs/communication/announcements) — tick **Also send via email** to reach every member's inbox, not just their in-app notifications.
- Any officer-sent notification (payment reminders, approvals) can optionally include an email, decided by whichever feature sends it.

## Why it matters

If you're planning a one-off email to every member — a newsletter, an update — use Announcements with the email checkbox ticked rather than waiting on Email Campaigns.
