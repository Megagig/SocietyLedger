Every text, one-off or part of a campaign or announcement, is recorded with its status. The credit history lists each change to your balance.

## Before you start

You need the **view SMS** permission.

## View history

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Communication", "SMS History"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Filter by **Status**: Pending, Sent, Delivered, Failed or Rejected.</Step>
<Step>For credits, go to <MenuPath items={["Communication", "SMS Reports"]} />.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Communication", "Texts and credits"]} />.</Step>
<Step>On **Texts**, filter by status. On **Credits**, see what you have left, bought and used, and every change.</Step>
<Step>From a campaign, tap **See every text in this campaign** to see only its texts.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## What the statuses mean

- **Sending**: recorded and about to go out.
- **Sent**: handed to the phone network.
- **Delivered**: the network confirmed it reached the phone. This needs delivery reports switched on for your SMS provider; without them texts stay **Sent**.
- **Failed** or **Rejected**: it did not get through, and its credit was refunded. The reason shows under the message.
