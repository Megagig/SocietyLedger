Every SMS sent — one-off or part of a campaign — is recorded here with its delivery status.

## Before you start

You need SMS permissions on your officer role (Administrator has them by default).

## View message history

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Communication", "SMS History"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Filter by **Status** — Pending, Sent, Delivered, Failed or Rejected.</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/communication/sms-history-1.webp" alt="SMS message history, currently empty, with a status filter" />

## What happens next

Each row shows the recipient, message, type (single or bulk) and status, with any delivery error shown underneath a failed message. Nothing has been sent yet in this environment — see [Sending an SMS](/docs/communication/sending-sms) for why — so this list is currently empty, but the page itself works correctly.
