A budget sets an annual target for one or more accounts; once it's active, Accounting compares what you actually posted against it.

## How it works

Say you set a 2026 budget of ₦1,200,000 for Membership Dues, and by mid-October you've posted ₦50,000 against it:

| Account | Budget | Actual | Variance | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Membership Dues | ₦1,200,000 | ₦50,000 | ₦1,150,000 | Under |

A budget moves through **Draft → Approved → Active**; only one budget per fiscal year can be Active at a time — activating a new one closes the previous one automatically.

## Why it matters

Variance only means something once your actual activity is current — if you haven't synced recently (see [What happens automatically when money moves](/docs/accounting/what-happens-automatically)), "Under" might just mean "unsynced," not "under budget."
