A budget sets an annual target for one or more accounts; once it's active, Accounting compares what you actually posted against it.

## How it works

Say you set a 2026 budget of ₦1,200,000 for Membership Dues, and by mid-October you've posted ₦50,000 against it:

| Account | Budget | Actual | Variance | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Membership Dues | ₦1,200,000 | ₦50,000 | ₦1,150,000 | Under |

A budget moves through **Draft → Approved → Active**. A different officer has to approve it, and only one budget per year is in force at a time: putting a new one in force closes the previous one.

## Why it matters

Actual is what was posted within the budget's own dates, and income and spending are kept apart. If "Under" looks too low, check that nothing is waiting to be entered (see [What happens automatically when money moves](/docs/accounting/what-happens-automatically)).
