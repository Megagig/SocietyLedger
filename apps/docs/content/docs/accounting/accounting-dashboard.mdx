A one-screen summary of where your books stand right now.

## Before you start

You need the **view finances** permission, which Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries have by default. Your plan must include the Accounting module.

## Read the dashboard

<PlatformTabs>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Accounting"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Check **Quick Stats**: Total Assets, Total Liabilities, Total Equity, Net Income for the year so far, and Cash Balance.</Step>
<Step>**Recent Entries** lists your last 5 journal entries. **Pending Approvals** counts the ones waiting for a second person.</Step>
<Step>If a budget is in force, **Budget Status** shows how much of the spending plan is used. The **Monthly Trend** chart plots income against expenses for the last 6 months.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
<PlatformTab>
<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Manage", "Accounting"]} />.</Step>
<Step>The green panel shows your **Cash and bank** balance, with what you own, what you owe and the surplus for the year beneath it.</Step>
<Step>**Needs attention** shows journal entries waiting for you and any records not in the books yet. Tap **Catch up** to enter the missing ones.</Step>
<Step>Further down are the last six months, the budget so far, shortcuts to the rest of Accounting, and your latest entries.</Step>
</Steps>
</PlatformTab>
</PlatformTabs>

## What the figures count

- Every figure counts **posted** entries only. A draft, or an entry waiting for approval, is not in them yet.
- The year, and each month, start and end in Lagos time.
- Spending against the budget counts only spending posted within the budget's own dates.
