A one-screen summary of where your books stand right now.

## Before you start

You need the **view finances** permission, which Administrators, Treasurers and Financial Secretaries have by default.

## Read the dashboard

<Steps>
<Step>Go to <MenuPath items={["Accounting"]} />.</Step>
<Step>Check **Quick Stats** — Total Assets, Total Liabilities, Total Equity, Net Income (YTD) and Cash Balance.</Step>
<Step>**Recent Entries** lists your last 5 journal entries; **Pending Approvals** counts the ones waiting on a second person.</Step>
<Step>If a budget is active, **Budget Status** shows Total Budget, Total Spent, Remaining and Percent Used. The **Monthly Trend** chart plots income vs. expenses for the last 6 months.</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="Totals only reflect synced activity">
These numbers come from posted journal entries, not directly from Finance, Welfare or Cooperatives — see [What happens automatically when money moves](/docs/accounting/what-happens-automatically) for what that means in practice.
</Callout>

<Screenshot src="/images/accounting/accounting-dashboard-1.webp" alt="Accounting dashboard showing Quick Stats cards, Recent Entries, Pending Approvals count and the Monthly Trend chart" />

## What happens next

Nothing on this screen updates on its own — see [What happens automatically when money moves](/docs/accounting/what-happens-automatically) for what you need to do to keep these totals current.
