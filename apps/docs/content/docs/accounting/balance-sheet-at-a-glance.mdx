A balance sheet is a snapshot, not a period — it shows what your association owns and owes as of one specific date, and must always balance.

## How it works

Say you've posted one entry: ₦50,000 of dues recorded as receivable (not yet collected in cash). As of today:

| | Amount |
|---|---|
| Total Assets (Member Dues Receivable) | ₦50,000 |
| Total Liabilities | ₦0 |
| Total Equity (Retained Earnings) | ₦50,000 |
| **Assets = Liabilities + Equity** | **Balanced** |

A green **Balanced** indicator confirms Total Assets equals Total Liabilities plus Total Equity. If it ever doesn't, something was posted outside the normal journal entry flow — treat that as a bug to report, not something to work around.

## Why it matters

The balance sheet is what shows whether dues owed to you (receivables) have actually turned into cash yet — net income on the income statement can be positive while your cash balance is still zero, exactly like the example above.
