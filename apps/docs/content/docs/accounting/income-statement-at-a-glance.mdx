Your income statement adds up revenue and expense accounts for a period and shows the difference as net income.

## How it works

Say October's posted entries show ₦50,000 in Membership Dues and nothing in expenses yet. The income statement for October shows:

| | Amount |
|---|---|
| Revenue — Membership Dues | ₦50,000 |
| Total Expenses | ₦0 |
| **Net Income** | **₦50,000** |

Only **posted** journal entries count — a draft or pending entry for the same ₦50,000 wouldn't appear here yet. See [Journal entries: draft, approve, post](/docs/accounting-guide/journal-entries) for that workflow.

<Callout type="note" title="Not the same number as Reports → Financial Summary">
[Financial Summary](/docs/reports/financial-summary) reads your Finance transactions directly — it reflects every due and payment, synced or not. This income statement only reflects what's been posted to accounting. If you haven't synced recently, Financial Summary will show more activity than this page does.
</Callout>

## Why it matters

This is the number that answers "did we make or lose money this period" — but only for the activity that's actually reached accounting. See [What happens automatically when money moves](/docs/accounting/what-happens-automatically) before trusting it as a complete picture.
