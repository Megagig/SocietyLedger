Your income statement adds up revenue and expense accounts for a period and shows the difference as net income.

## How it works

Say the entries posted in October show ₦50,000 in Membership Dues and nothing in expenses yet. The income statement for October shows:

| | Amount |
|---|---|
| Revenue — Membership Dues | ₦50,000 |
| Total Expenses | ₦0 |
| **Net Income** | **₦50,000** |

Only **posted** journal entries count — a draft or pending entry for the same ₦50,000 wouldn't appear here yet. See [Journal entries: draft, approve, post](/docs/accounting-guide/journal-entries) for that workflow.

<Callout type="note" title="Not the same number as Reports → Financial Summary">
[Financial Summary](/docs/reports/financial-summary) reads your Finance records directly: money received, and what was charged. This income statement reads the books, from the entries posted to them. The two can differ, for example when an entry is still waiting for approval or could not be entered yet.
</Callout>

## Why it matters

This is the number that answers "did we make or lose money this period". It counts what was posted within the period only. If something looks missing, see [What happens automatically when money moves](/docs/accounting/what-happens-automatically).
