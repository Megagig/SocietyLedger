Less syncs automatically than you'd expect — right now, only Finance module activity can be synced at all, and even that needs a manual step.

## How it works

Say a member pays their ₦5,000 monthly dues in Finance. That payment does **not** appear in Accounting on its own — nothing in the codebase creates a journal entry automatically when a transaction or payment is recorded. The only way it reaches Accounting is a manual **Sync Now** action at <MenuPath items={["Settings", "Accounting Integration"]} />, which finds every not-yet-synced transaction and payment and posts a journal entry for each one.

<Callout type="warning" title="The sync page's own banner overstates this">
Settings → Accounting Integration currently says *"All new financial transactions are automatically synced to the accounting module."* That isn't what happens today — every sync is a manual **Sync Now** click (or a backlog of unsynced records waiting for one). Treat **Sync Now** as something you need to visit, not something you can forget about.
</Callout>

Welfare contributions and payouts, and cooperative savings, loans and repayments, go further: there's no sync path for them at all yet, manual or automatic. The sync status page shows their numbers stuck at 0% because nothing exists to move them — not because nobody has clicked a button.

| Activity | Syncs to accounting? |
|---|---|
| Finance transactions and payments | Yes — manually, via **Sync Now** |
| Welfare contributions and payouts | Not yet |
| Cooperative savings, loans and repayments | Not yet |

## Why it matters

If you set up your chart of accounts and never visit Settings → Accounting Integration, your income statement and balance sheet will keep showing ₦0 no matter how much real activity happens elsewhere in the app. Make **Sync Now** a routine step, not a one-time setup task — see [Sync status and fixing sync errors](/docs/accounting-guide/sync-status-and-errors).
