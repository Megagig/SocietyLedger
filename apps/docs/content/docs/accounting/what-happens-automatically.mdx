When money moves in the app, an entry is made in the books for it straight away, already posted. You do not approve it, and you do not have to press anything.

## How it works

Say a member pays their ₦5,000 monthly dues. The charge being raised makes one entry, and the payment being recorded makes another. Both are posted at once, and both are marked **Automatic** in the journal.

| Activity | Reaches the books on its own? |
|---|---|
| Charges, payments, donations and penalties | Yes |
| Cash lodgments, once a second officer checks them | Yes |
| Welfare contributions and payouts, once confirmed or paid | Yes |
| Savings deposits and withdrawals | Yes |
| Loans paid out, and repayments | Yes |

The book is only as good as its chart of accounts: the app needs the standard accounts (such as Member Dues Receivable and Membership Dues) to exist. Set up the [chart of accounts](/docs/accounting-guide/chart-of-accounts) before money starts to move.

## When something is missing

An entry can fail to be made. The commonest reasons are that the accounts were not set up yet, or that the date falls in a [closed period](/docs/accounting-guide/accounting-periods). The payment or charge itself is never blocked. Open the accounting dashboard to see what is **not in the books yet**, and why each one failed, then use **Catch up** (mobile) or **Sync Now** (web) once the cause is fixed. See [Sync status and fixing sync errors](/docs/accounting-guide/sync-status-and-errors).

## What you still do by hand

Anything that is not a movement of money in the app, such as buying equipment or a bank charge, is a [journal entry](/docs/accounting-guide/journal-entries) you write yourself. It needs a second officer to approve it before it is posted.
