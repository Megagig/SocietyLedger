## Member Portal Guide

### Getting Started

When you log in as a member, you'll land on **My Dashboard** - your personal overview page.

### Navigation Structure

```
📊 My Dashboard
   Your personal overview

💰 My Finances
   ├─ Overview - Financial summary
   ├─ My Ledger - Transaction history
   ├─ My Dues - Monthly/annual dues
   ├─ My Levies - Special assessments
   ├─ My Donations - Contributions
   ├─ Penalties - Late fees
   ├─ Payment History - Past payments
   └─ Make Payment - Pay online

👤 My Profile
   Your personal information

🏛️ Governance
   ├─ Meetings - View & RSVP
   ├─ My Attendance - Your attendance record
   ├─ Voting - Participate in elections
   └─ Polls - Quick votes

🏢 Cooperatives (Enterprise tier)
   ├─ My Savings - Your savings account
   ├─ My Loans - Your loan status
   ├─ Apply for Loan - New loan application
   └─ Guarantor Requests - Requests for you

❤️ Welfare
   ├─ My Contributions - Your welfare contributions
   └─ Request Payout - Submit payout request

🔔 Notifications
   Your alerts and messages

❓ Help & Support
   ├─ Help Center - Articles and guides
   ├─ FAQs - Common questions
   └─ My Tickets - Support tickets

⚙️ Settings
   Personal preferences
```

### Key Features

#### My Finances

**What You Can Do:**
- View your outstanding balance
- See all your dues, levies, and donations
- Check payment history
- Make payments online
- Download receipts

**How to Pay Your Dues:**
1. Click **My Finances** → **Make Payment**
2. Select the items you want to pay
3. Choose your payment method
4. Complete the payment
5. Download your receipt

#### Governance

**What You Can Do:**
- View upcoming meetings
- RSVP to meetings
- Check your attendance history
- Vote in elections
- Participate in polls

**How to Vote:**
1. Click **Governance** → **Voting**
2. View active elections
3. Click on an election to see candidates
4. Cast your vote
5. Confirm your selection

#### Cooperatives (If Enabled)

**What You Can Do:**
- View your savings balance
- Check loan status and repayment schedule
- Apply for new loans
- Respond to guarantor requests

**How to Apply for a Loan:**
1. Click **Cooperatives** → **Apply for Loan**
2. Select loan product
3. Enter loan amount
4. Choose guarantors
5. Submit application
6. Wait for approval

## Admin Portal Guide

### Getting Started

When you log in as an admin, you'll see a **dual-section sidebar** that gives you access to both your personal member features AND association management tools.

### Understanding the Dual Sidebar

Your sidebar is divided into two sections:

#### 👤 MY MEMBER AREA (Blue Section)

This section contains **your personal member features**:
- My Dashboard
- My Finances (your personal dues, payments, etc.)
- My Profile
- My Meetings (your attendance)
- My Cooperatives (your savings and loans)

**Why This Matters:**
As an admin, you're also a member of the association. You pay dues, attend meetings, and participate in cooperatives just like any other member. This section lets you manage your personal member activities.

#### 🛠️ ADMINISTRATION (Purple Section)

This section contains **association management tools**:
- Dashboard (association overview)
- Members (manage all members)
- Finance (association finances)
- Accounting (financial statements)
- Reports (generate reports)
- Governance (manage meetings, elections)
- Welfare (manage welfare funds)
- Cooperatives (manage all loans and savings)
- Communication (SMS, emails, announcements)
- AI Assistant (financial insights)
- Subscription (billing)
- Settings (association settings)
- Help & Support (manage support)
- Setup Wizard

### Key Admin Features

#### Managing Your Personal Finances

**To Pay Your Own Dues:**
1. Go to **👤 MY MEMBER AREA** → **My Finances** → **Make Payment**
2. You'll see YOUR outstanding dues
3. Pay just like any other member

**To View Your Personal Ledger:**
1. Go to **👤 MY MEMBER AREA** → **My Finances** → **My Ledger**
2. See all YOUR transactions

#### Managing Association Finances

**To View All Members' Finances:**
1. Go to **🛠️ ADMINISTRATION** → **Finance** → **Member Ledgers**
2. See all members' financial data

**To Record a Payment for a Member:**
1. Go to **🛠️ ADMINISTRATION** → **Finance** → **Payment Management**
2. Select member
3. Record payment

#### Managing Meetings

**To View Your Own Attendance:**
1. Go to **👤 MY MEMBER AREA** → **My Meetings**
2. See meetings you attended

**To Manage All Meetings:**
1. Go to **🛠️ ADMINISTRATION** → **Governance** → **Meetings**
2. Create, edit, or delete meetings
3. Mark attendance for all members

### Admin Best Practices

✅ **Use Member Area for Personal Tasks**
- Pay your own dues from "My Finances"
- RSVP to meetings from "My Meetings"
- Apply for loans from "My Cooperatives"

✅ **Use Administration for Management Tasks**
- Record payments for other members
- Generate financial reports
- Manage association settings

✅ **Keep Contexts Separate**
- Don't confuse your personal finances with association finances
- Remember: You're a member first, admin second

## Understanding Your Context

### Role Indicator Badge

When you're an admin, you'll see a **badge in the top-right corner** showing your current context:

- **👤 Member View** - You're in the member area (personal features)
- **🛠️ Admin View** - You're in the administration area (management features)

This helps you always know whether you're acting as a member or an administrator.

### Breadcrumbs

At the top of each page, you'll see **breadcrumbs** showing your current location:

```
Home > My Finances > My Dues
```

Click any breadcrumb to navigate back to that page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### For Members

**Q: Why can't I see the Members menu?**  
A: That's an admin-only feature. Members can only see their own information.

**Q: How do I know if I have outstanding dues?**  
A: Check your dashboard or go to My Finances → Overview. Your balance is shown prominently.

**Q: Can I pay someone else's dues?**  
A: No, you can only pay your own dues. Contact an admin if you need to pay for someone else.

### For Admins

**Q: Where did all my admin features go?**  
A: They're all still there! Look in the **🛠️ ADMINISTRATION** section of your sidebar.

**Q: Why do I see "My Finances" twice?**  
A: One is in **👤 MY MEMBER AREA** (your personal finances) and one is in **🛠️ ADMINISTRATION** (association finances). They serve different purposes.

**Q: Can I still access my personal member features?**  
A: Yes! That's what the **👤 MY MEMBER AREA** section is for. You have full access to all member features.

**Q: Do I have to pay dues like other members?**  
A: Yes! Being an admin doesn't exempt you from dues. You pay through "My Finances" in the Member Area.

### For Super Admins

**Q: Did anything change for me?**  
A: No, your platform admin dashboard remains exactly the same.

## Troubleshooting

### I can't see a feature I used to have access to

**Solution:**
1. Check if you're looking in the right section (Member Area vs Administration)
2. Verify your permissions with an admin
3. Try refreshing the page
4. Clear your browser cache

### The sidebar isn't showing on mobile

**Solution:**
1. Tap the **Menu icon** (☰) in the bottom navigation
2. The sidebar will slide in from the left
3. Tap outside the sidebar or the X to close it

### I'm getting "Permission Denied" errors

**Solution:**
1. Verify you're logged in
2. Check that your role has the required permissions
3. Contact an admin to verify your permissions
4. Try logging out and back in

### The page is loading slowly

**Solution:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Try refreshing the page
3. Clear your browser cache
4. Try a different browser
