## Content Performance Metrics

### Track Monthly
- **Page views** per post
- **Time on page** (target: 3+ minutes)
- **Bounce rate** (target: < 60%)
- **Social shares** count
- **Comments** and engagement
- **Backlinks** generated
- **Keyword rankings** for target terms
- **Conversions** (sign-ups from blog)

### Success Criteria
- 500+ views per post within first month
- 3+ minute average time on page
- 10+ social shares per post
- 2+ comments per post
- 1+ backlink per post within 3 months

## Content Guidelines

### Writing Style
- **Tone:** Professional but friendly
- **Voice:** Second person (you/your)
- **Length:** 1,500-2,500 words
- **Format:** Scannable (headings, bullets, short paragraphs)

### Structure
1. **Hook** - Grab attention in first paragraph
2. **Problem** - Identify reader's pain point
3. **Solution** - Provide actionable advice
4. **Examples** - Real-world applications
5. **CTA** - Clear next step

### Quality Standards
- Original content (no plagiarism)
- Fact-checked and accurate
- Actionable and practical
- Well-researched with sources
- Properly formatted
- Mobile-friendly
