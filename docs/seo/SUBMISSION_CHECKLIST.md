## Tips for Success

### Directory Submissions
1. **Be Consistent:** Use same business info everywhere
2. **Be Complete:** Fill out 100% of profile
3. **Be Visual:** Add logos, screenshots, videos
4. **Be Responsive:** Reply to reviews and messages
5. **Be Patient:** Listings take 1-4 weeks to go live

### Review Collection
1. **Ask Happy Customers:** Request reviews from satisfied users
2. **Make It Easy:** Send direct links to review pages
3. **Respond Always:** Reply to all reviews (positive and negative)
4. **Be Genuine:** Never buy fake reviews

### Social Media
1. **Post Regularly:** Consistency builds following
2. **Engage:** Respond to comments and messages
3. **Share Value:** Educational content, not just promotion
4. **Use Hashtags:** Relevant hashtags increase reach
