## Our Differentiation Strategy

### Unique Selling Points

1. **Nigeria-First Approach**
   - Local payment methods (Paystack, bank transfer)
   - Nigerian phone numbers and SMS
   - Naira pricing
   - Local support (WAT timezone)
   - Understanding of Nigerian associations

2. **Welfare Fund Management**
   - Unique feature not offered by competitors
   - Critical for Nigerian/African associations
   - Bereavement, medical, education funds
   - Contribution tracking and payouts

3. **Affordable Pricing**
   - 70-80% cheaper than international competitors
   - No forex risk
   - Transparent pricing
   - No hidden fees

4. **Association-Specific**
   - Built for cooperatives, HOAs, town unions
   - Not generic membership software
   - Features designed for African context
   - Understanding of local challenges

5. **Modern Technology**
   - Progressive Web App (works offline)
   - Mobile-first design
   - Real-time updates
   - Cloud-based
